# AI in Design Education

*This community clusters papers examining how AI is entering design education and what educators must now address. The shared inquiry is pedagogical: how do we teach design thinking, cultivate AI literacy, and maintain studio-based learning when generative AI is available as a tool? These papers grapple with what design literacy and competency mean in an AI-augmented context and how curricula must evolve to meet that challenge.*
