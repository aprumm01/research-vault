# AI Literacy

*This community isolates the specific inquiry around AI literacy — what it means to understand AI systems, how literacy is defined across educational and professional contexts, and what competencies practitioners need to engage critically with AI tools and outputs. The community has a constructionist pedagogical bent, with several papers examining how embodied, hands-on learning builds richer AI literacy than declarative instruction alone.*
