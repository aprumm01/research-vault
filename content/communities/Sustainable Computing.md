# Sustainable Computing

## Overview
This research community encompasses 22 papers examining the environmental dimensions of computing and digital infrastructure. Researchers in this space investigate the ecological footprint of data centers, cloud services, AI systems, and personal digital behaviors. The community bridges environmental science, computer science, and human-computer interaction to understand and mitigate the hidden environmental costs of digital technologies.

## Key Questions
- What is the true carbon and water footprint of digital services?
- How can we design computing systems that minimize environmental impact?
- What role do individual behaviors (like digital hoarding) play in aggregate environmental costs?
- How should we account for the full life cycle of computing devices and services?
- What are the environmental trade-offs of AI and generative AI adoption?
