# Design Theory and Cognition

*This community examines design as a cognitive and epistemic practice. The papers cluster around foundational scholarship on how designers think, reason, and develop professional knowledge — from Schön's reflective practitioner tradition to Cross's designerly ways of knowing and the co-evolution of problem and solution space. It is a theoretically foundational community whose ideas underpin both HCI Education and Pedagogy and AI in Design Education.*
