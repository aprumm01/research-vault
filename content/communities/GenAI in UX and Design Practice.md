# GenAI in UX and Design Practice

*This community — the largest in the graph — clusters papers examining how professional UX and design practitioners are actually using generative AI tools in their work. The shared inquiry is empirical and practice-centered: what does GenAI adoption look like in studios, product teams, and agencies, and what does it reveal about creative agency, cognitive offloading, and design judgment under AI assistance? Human-AI creative collaboration and generative UI are the established anchors; prompt engineering, creative ambiguity, metacognitive support, and vibe coding represent the emerging frontier of this practice shift.*
