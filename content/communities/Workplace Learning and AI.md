# Workplace Learning and AI

*This community examines how workers learn, adapt, and make sense of AI when it enters their organizational environments. Activity theory provides the theoretical foundation — treating work as mediated by tools, community norms, and rules — while sensemaking frameworks examine how workers collectively construct understanding of AI-driven change. The community includes notable representation from library and information science, where AI reskilling is a particularly urgent practical challenge.*
