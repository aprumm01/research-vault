# Location vs Market-Based Carbon Accounting

## Overview
Two approaches to carbon accounting: location-based (actual grid emissions where electricity is consumed) vs market-based (accounting for renewable energy purchases/certificates). Corporate renewable energy claims obscure actual grid emissions.

## Key Insight
Data center carbon intensity is 48% above US average despite renewable energy purchases. Market-based accounting allows "greenwashing" while location-based reveals true grid impact.
