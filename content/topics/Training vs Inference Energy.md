# Training vs Inference Energy

## Overview
The energy distribution between AI model training and inference (production use). Inference dominates production AI energy use (60-90% of lifecycle). At Meta, power allocation is 10:20:70 for experimentation:training:inference.

## Key Insight
Research focus on training efficiency misses the larger target. Inference optimization and deployment decisions have greater aggregate impact than training efficiency.
