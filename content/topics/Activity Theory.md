# Activity Theory

## Overview
A psychological and social theory originating with Vygotsky and developed by Leont'ev and Engeström that frames human action as mediated by tools, rules, community, and the division of labor within structured activity systems. Engeström's Cultural-Historical Activity Theory (CHAT) extends the model to collective, organizational contexts, foregrounding internal contradictions as the engine of expansive learning and transformation.

## Key Thinkers
- Lev Vygotsky
- Alexei Leont'ev
- Yrjö Engeström
