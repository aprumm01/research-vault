# Feminized Labor and AI

## Overview
An area of inquiry examining how AI-driven automation disproportionately affects occupations coded as feminine—care work, emotional labor, administrative coordination, and service work. Drawing on feminist political economy and labor process theory, scholars analyze how AI deployment reproduces and intensifies existing gender inequities in both the valuation of work and the distribution of automation's benefits and harms.
