# Sustainable Computing

## Overview
This topic encompasses research on the environmental impacts of computing infrastructure, digital services, and data storage. It addresses the carbon footprint, energy consumption, and water usage of data centers, cloud services, generative AI, and personal digital behaviors like data hoarding. The field spans life cycle assessment methodologies, green IT practices, and strategies for mitigating the environmental costs of our increasingly digital world.

## Key Themes
- Data center energy and water consumption
- Carbon footprint of digital services and AI
- Life cycle assessment for computing
- Digital hoarding and dark data
- Green IT and sustainable archiving
- Device obsolescence and e-waste
