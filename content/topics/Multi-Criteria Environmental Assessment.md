# Multi-Criteria Environmental Assessment

## Overview
Moving beyond "carbon tunnel vision" to assess multiple environmental impact categories: Global Warming Potential (GWP), Abiotic Depletion Potential (ADP/metal depletion), water footprint, land use, and toxicity.

## Key Insight
Different metrics identify different optimization targets. End-user terminals dominate metal depletion while inference dominates carbon. A carbon-only focus may shift burden to other impact categories.
