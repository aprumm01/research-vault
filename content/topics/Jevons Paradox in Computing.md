# Jevons Paradox in Computing

## Overview
Efficiency improvements enable more usage rather than reducing total impact. As computing becomes more efficient, it becomes cheaper and more accessible, driving increased demand that offsets or exceeds efficiency gains.

## Key Insight
Meta achieved 28.5% power footprint reduction while infrastructure scaled 2.9x. Net effect: continued growth despite efficiency gains. Efficiency alone cannot solve sustainability.
