# Barriers to Data Deletion

## Overview
Five psychological and practical barriers prevent people from deleting digital data: (1) "just in case" future value, (2) evidentiary value, (3) laziness/time cost, (4) emotional attachment, (5) "not my server, not my problem." Unlimited cloud storage removes natural constraints on accumulation.

## Key Insight
The barriers are both psychological (attachment, anxiety) and systemic (no cost signal, poor deletion UX). Design interventions must address both.
