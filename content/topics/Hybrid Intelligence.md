# Hybrid Intelligence

## Overview
A design paradigm and research agenda focused on combining human and machine intelligence in ways that leverage complementary strengths: human contextual judgment, creativity, and values alongside AI's pattern recognition, speed, and scale. Hybrid intelligence research seeks to identify optimal human-AI task division rather than defaulting to maximum automation, and to design systems that improve through human-AI collaboration over time.
