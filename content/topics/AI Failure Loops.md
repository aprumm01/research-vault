# AI Failure Loops

## Overview
A pattern in which AI systems deployed in devalued or marginalized work contexts amplify existing inequities through self-reinforcing cycles: poor-quality inputs yield poor outputs, which further devalue the work and the workers, reducing the likelihood of corrective investment. The concept draws attention to how AI failure is not random but structurally concentrated in already-disadvantaged labor contexts.
