# Designerly Ways of Knowing

## Overview
Nigel Cross's argument that design constitutes a third intellectual culture alongside science and the humanities, with its own distinct epistemology, values, and methods. Designers know through making—their mode of inquiry is synthetic, abductive, and embedded in material practice. This framing elevates design knowledge as legitimate and irreducible, with implications for how design is taught, researched, and evaluated.
