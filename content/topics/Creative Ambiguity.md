# Creative Ambiguity

## Overview
The productive role of open-endedness, underspecification, and unresolved meaning in design processes. Unlike precision-focused engineering, design often benefits from sustaining ambiguity long enough for unexpected framings and solutions to emerge. As AI generative tools accelerate convergence toward solutions, understanding when and how to preserve creative ambiguity becomes an important pedagogical and professional concern.
