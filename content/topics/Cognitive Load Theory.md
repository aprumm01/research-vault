# Cognitive Load Theory

## Overview
A theory of instructional design developed by John Sweller grounded in cognitive psychology's account of limited working memory. It distinguishes three types of load: intrinsic (inherent task complexity), extraneous (created by poor instructional design), and germane (invested in building schemas). Effective instruction minimizes extraneous load while managing intrinsic load and supporting schema formation.
