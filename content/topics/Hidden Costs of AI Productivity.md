# Hidden Costs of AI Productivity

## Overview
AI coding tools introduce hidden costs: technical debt (22.7% survival rate of AI-introduced issues), redistributed effort rather than reduced effort (84.3% report no reduction in code review time), and "spurious productivity" that masks accumulated maintenance burden.

## Key Insight
Apparent productivity gains from AI tools may be illusory when accounting for downstream effects. The environmental cost of AI assistance must be weighed against actual (not perceived) productivity benefits.
