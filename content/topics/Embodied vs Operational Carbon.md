# Embodied vs Operational Carbon

## Overview
The distinction between embodied carbon (emissions from manufacturing, materials, transport) and operational carbon (emissions from energy use during operation). For consumer devices, embodied carbon dominates (85-95%); for data centers, embodied carbon is becoming dominant as grids decarbonize.

## Key Insight
This distinction is fundamental to understanding where sustainability interventions should focus. Extending device lifetimes reduces embodied carbon; renewable energy reduces operational carbon.
