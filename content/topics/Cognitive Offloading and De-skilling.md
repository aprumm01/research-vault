# Cognitive Offloading and De-skilling

## Overview
Cognitive offloading is the practice of externalizing mental work onto tools, artifacts, or environments to reduce the demand on working memory. In AI contexts, critics argue that habitual offloading may erode expert skills and reduce practitioners' capacity for independent judgment—a concern central to debates about AI in design and professional work. The de-skilling thesis holds that automation systematically strips workers of tacit expertise over time.
