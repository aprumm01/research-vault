# Water-Energy Nexus

## Overview
The interdependence of water and energy consumption in data centers. Indirect water consumption (from electricity generation) often exceeds direct cooling water (71% vs 29%). Geographic optimization could reduce combined water/carbon footprint by up to 90%.

## Key Insight
Energy source choice typically dominates total water footprint over cooling technology choice. Using more energy increases water use, which increases energy use further (feedback loop).
