# Value-Sensitive Design

## Overview
A theoretically grounded design methodology developed by Batya Friedman at the University of Washington that proactively accounts for human values throughout the technology design process. VSD combines conceptual investigation (identifying stakeholders and their values), empirical inquiry (studying how values manifest in use), and technical work (translating values into design requirements). It attends to both direct users and indirect stakeholders, including marginalized groups whose values are often excluded from design processes.
