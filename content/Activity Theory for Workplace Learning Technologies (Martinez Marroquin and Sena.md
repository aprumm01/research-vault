# Activity Theory for Workplace Learning Technologies (Martinez Marroquin and Senadji)


## Abstract

**DOI:** [10.1108/ijilt-07-2024-0141](https://doi.org/10.1108/ijilt-07-2024-0141)
PurposeTechnology, such as artificial intelligence (AI), is transforming the way we work; however, it is yet to systemically transform learning at the workplace beyond augmentation of formal education’s learning processes. This paper derives functional requirements for technologies that support workplace learning and assesses the suitability and limitations of generative AI conversational agents, as an example of application.Design/methodology/approachUsing activity theory (AT) as theoretical framework, we model workplace learning as an activity, intertwined with work and mediated by technology, and expose contradictions that arise when technology developed for formal education is adopted at work. From these tensions, we derive functional requirements and illustrate their use by comparing them to ChatGPT’s affordances.FindingsA framework is proposed for design and assessment of enabling technologies for workplace learning. In applying it to ChatGPT, as paradigm of conversational agents, we find the aspects that are particularly suitable to enhance workplace learning, and those that need further development.Originality/valueThe theoretical approach is novel. Previous research is based on reported use-cases of enabling technologies, such as generative artificial intelligence (GenAI), in the workplace or on the analysis of the learner’s experience when these technologies are embedded in structured training modules. The present study addresses the limitations of current retrospective research, providing a forward-looking approach.

## Summary
Martinez Marroquin and Senadji use Activity Theory (AT) as a framework to analyze what workplace learning technologies need to do — and to assess where generative AI conversational agents like ChatGPT succeed and fall short. Modeling workplace learning as an activity intertwined with work and mediated by technology, they identify contradictions that arise when tools designed for formal education are adopted at work, and derive functional requirements for workplace learning technologies from those contradictions. ChatGPT proves suitable for some requirements but needs further development for others.

## Key Concepts
- Activity Theory (Engeström's CHAT framework)
- Workplace learning vs. formal education learning
- Functional requirements for learning technologies
- Generative AI conversational agents (ChatGPT)
- Contradictions in activity systems

## Theoretical Framework
Cultural-Historical Activity Theory (CHAT) — specifically Engeström's expansive learning model — frames workplace learning as an activity system with subject, object, tools, rules, community, and division of labor; contradictions between components drive learning and transformation.

## Methods
Theoretical framework application; models workplace learning as an AT activity system; derives functional requirements by analyzing contradictions; applies requirements to ChatGPT's documented affordances as a case study comparison.

## Main Arguments
- Learning at work is fundamentally different from formal education: it is intertwined with ongoing work tasks, embedded in organizational hierarchies, and driven by immediate operational needs rather than curriculum
- Technologies built for formal education create contradictions when used at work — they assume stable curricula, explicit learning objectives, and non-work time, none of which characterize workplace learning
- ChatGPT is particularly suitable for just-in-time knowledge access and informal Q&A but lacks support for collective/organizational learning and for developing complex tacit expertise
- AT provides a rigorous forward-looking design method that avoids the retrospective limitations of use-case analysis

## Limitations
- Framework application is theoretical; empirical validation of derived requirements in real workplace learning contexts is not provided
- ChatGPT as paradigm case may date quickly as conversational AI capabilities advance
