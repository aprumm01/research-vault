# Ethnography

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/Workplace_Learning_and_AI

## Overview
Ethnography involves extended immersive fieldwork — participant observation, informal interviews, and documentary analysis — to understand the lived practices, meanings, and social structures of a group or setting. In HCI and CSCW it is used to understand work practices in situ.
