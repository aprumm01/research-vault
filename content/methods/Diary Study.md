# Diary Study

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/AI_and_Future_of_Work

## Overview
Diary studies are longitudinal self-report methods in which participants log their experiences, behaviors, or tool use over days or weeks. They capture in-situ, naturalistic data that would be missed by lab studies, and are particularly suited to understanding how tools are integrated into everyday routines.
