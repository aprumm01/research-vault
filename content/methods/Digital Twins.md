# Digital Twins

## Overview
In the LLM simulation context, a digital twin is an AI agent conditioned on a specific real participant's profile (demographics, preferences, stated behaviors) to mirror that individual's responses. Unlike generic synthetic users generated from population archetypes, digital twins maintain a one-to-one pairing with a real human — enabling direct alignment comparisons between human and simulated behavior. The approach is used to evaluate whether LLM agents can substitute for real participants in structured tasks such as shopping decisions, survey responses, and usability sessions.
