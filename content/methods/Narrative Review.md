# Narrative Review

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/AI_and_Future_of_Work

## Overview
Narrative and integrative reviews synthesize a body of literature through critical reading and interpretation, without the exhaustive database search protocols of systematic reviews. They are better suited to conceptual integration and theory development than to meta-analytic synthesis of empirical findings.
