# Wizard-of-Oz Study

## Overview
In a Wizard-of-Oz study a human operator secretly simulates system behavior that does not yet exist, allowing researchers to study user interaction with a proposed AI or intelligent system before it is built. Essential for early-stage evaluation of AI interfaces and conversational agents.
