# Semi-Structured Interviews

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/Semi-Structured_Interviews

## Overview
Semi-structured interviews use a prepared guide of open-ended questions while allowing the interviewer to probe, follow up, and adapt. They balance comparability across participants with flexibility to capture unexpected insights. Widely used in HCI, design research, and workplace studies.
