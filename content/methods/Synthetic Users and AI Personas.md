# Synthetic Users and AI Personas

## Overview
Synthetic users are LLM-generated agent entities designed to simulate human behavior, opinions, or interactions in research contexts. They serve as proxies for human participants in usability testing, survey research, design evaluation, and formative studies — with the aim of reducing recruitment costs and enabling scale. The corpus spans optimistic validation studies (can LLMs approximate human responses?), critical analyses (do they reproduce normative biases and miss authentic behavior?), and applied frameworks for specific contexts such as A/B testing, heuristic evaluation, and cognitive walkthroughs.
