# Participatory Design Sessions

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/Semi-Structured_Interviews

## Overview
Participatory Design sessions are structured co-design activities — workshops, bodystorming, design games, future workshops — in which end-users and stakeholders actively shape design outcomes. Distinct from user testing in that participants contribute ideas, not merely evaluate proposals.
