# Protocol Analysis

## Overview
Protocol Analysis captures cognitive processes in real time through verbal think-aloud reports or behavioral traces during task performance. Ericsson & Simon's concurrent and retrospective verbal protocols are standard; design research uses it to study the cognitive processes of designers during ideation.

## Key Thinkers
- K. Anders Ericsson
- Herbert Simon
