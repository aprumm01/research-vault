# A/B Testing

## Overview
A/B testing (split testing) is an experimental method in which two or more design variants are presented to randomly assigned participant groups, and outcome metrics (conversion, click-through, purchase rate) are compared. It is the standard evaluation method in industry UX and product development. In this corpus it appears specifically in the context of replacing or augmenting human participants with LLM agents — testing whether simulated behavioral data from AI personas can approximate the results of large-scale live human A/B experiments.
