# User-Centered Design

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/Semi-Structured_Interviews

## Overview
User-Centered Design (UCD) / Human-Centered Design (HCD) is an iterative design process codified in ISO 9241-210 that keeps users and their needs at the center of design decisions through research, prototyping, and evaluation cycles. Norman's concept of affordances and feedback loops is foundational.

## Key Thinkers
- Don Norman
- Jakob Nielsen
- ISO 9241-210
