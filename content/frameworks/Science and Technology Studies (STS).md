# Science and Technology Studies (STS)

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/AI_and_Future_of_Work

## Overview
STS examines how scientific knowledge and technological artifacts are socially constructed, and how they in turn shape society. Core concepts include the Social Construction of Technology (SCOT), Actor-Network Theory (ANT), and interpretive flexibility — the idea that artifacts carry different meanings for different social groups.
