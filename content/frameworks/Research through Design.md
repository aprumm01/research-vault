# Research through Design

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/AI_and_Future_of_Work

## Overview
Research through Design (RtD) treats design practice itself as a mode of inquiry that produces knowledge not easily accessible through other methods. Design artifacts embody intermediate-level knowledge — more particular than theory, more general than a single design solution.
