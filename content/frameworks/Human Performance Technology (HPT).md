# Human Performance Technology (HPT)

## Overview
HPT is a systems-based approach to improving human performance through analysis, design, and evaluation of interventions. Gilbert's Behavior Engineering Model identifies environmental and individual factors; Rummler & Brache's process view extends it to organizational systems. ISPI and ASTD are the primary professional bodies.

- Geary Rummler
