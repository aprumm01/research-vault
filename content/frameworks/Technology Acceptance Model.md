# Technology Acceptance Model

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/Survey_/_Questionnaire_Study

## Overview
Davis's Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) posits that perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use are the primary determinants of technology adoption behavior. Venkatesh's UTAUT extends it to include social influence and facilitating conditions, and has been widely applied to AI tool adoption research.
