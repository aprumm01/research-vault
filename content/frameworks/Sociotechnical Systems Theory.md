# Sociotechnical Systems Theory

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/Semi-Structured_Interviews

## Overview
Sociotechnical Systems Theory treats technology and social organization as co-evolving, interdependent systems that must be jointly optimized. Originating in Tavistock Institute studies of coal mining, it has been extended to information systems design and human-computer interaction.
