# Cooperative Learning Theory

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/AI_and_Future_of_Work

## Overview
Cooperative Learning Theory, developed by Johnson & Johnson and Slavin, identifies the conditions under which small-group learning outperforms individual or competitive structures: positive interdependence, individual accountability, face-to-face interaction, and group processing of social skills.
