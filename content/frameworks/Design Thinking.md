# Design Thinking

## Overview
Design Thinking is a human-centered innovation process popularized by IDEO and Stanford's d.school, structured around five phases: Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test. It imports design sensibility and methods into non-design domains, emphasizing abductive reasoning, iteration, and tolerance for ambiguity.
