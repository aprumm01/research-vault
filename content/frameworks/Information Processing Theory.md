# Information Processing Theory

#graphify/document #graphify/EXTRACTED #community/Instructional_Design_Theory

## Overview
Information Processing Theory models cognition as a computer-like system with sensory registers, limited working memory, and virtually unlimited long-term memory. Gagné's nine events of instruction operationalize it for instructional design; Paivio's dual coding theory extends it to verbal and visual channels.
