# Nelson

## Overview
Harold Nelson is a design theorist and co-author with Erik Stolterman of *The Design Way*. His work argues for design practice as a distinct mode of professional action—different from both scientific inquiry and artistic expression—defined by intentional creation in the service of human purposes and the management of complex, ill-structured situations.
