# Brock

## Overview
André Brock Jr. is an associate professor at Georgia Tech whose critical technocultural studies examine race, power, and digital media. His historical analysis of expert systems situates contemporary AI enthusiasm within prior cycles of AI development and institutional investment, cautioning against narratives that treat the current moment as categorically new.
