# Stolovitch

## Overview
Harold D. Stolovitch is a professor emeritus at Concordia University and a leading figure in Human Performance Technology. A longtime ISPI contributor, his collaborative work with Beresford traces HPT's disciplinary evolution from behavioral engineering toward systemic performance consulting—positioning HPT as a rigorous applied science of human and organizational performance.
