# Cross

## Overview
Nigel Cross is Emeritus Professor of Design Studies at The Open University and one of design research's most cited scholars. His 1982 concept of 'designerly ways of knowing' established design as a third intellectual culture alongside science and the humanities, with its own distinct epistemology grounded in synthetic, abductive, material inquiry.
