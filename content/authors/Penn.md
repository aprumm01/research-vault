# Penn

## Overview
A critical AI studies researcher whose work 'Animo Nullius' argues that dominant AI origin narratives reproduce colonial logic by erasing the Indigenous knowledge, exploited labor, and historically marginalized contributions embedded in AI's data and development. Their work connects data colonialism scholarship to the politics of AI historiography.
