# Muller

## Overview
Michael Muller is a researcher at IBM Research Cambridge working on human-centered AI and participatory design. His CHI 2020 work with Casey Fiesler on design fiction examined how speculative narrative methods can help designers and researchers surface implicit values and anticipate social consequences of AI development.
