# Law

## Overview
A researcher whose 2025 systematic review with Varanasi examines how practitioners proactively reshape their roles in response to generative AI-driven work transformation. Applying job crafting theory, the review synthesizes evidence on how workers negotiate new human-AI task divisions and reconstruct professional identity under conditions of rapid technological change.
