# Hautala

## Overview
Johanna Hautala is a professor at the University of Vaasa (Finland) whose research examines futures imaginaries, anticipatory practices, and the sociology of AI practitioners. Her empirical work in Finland and Singapore maps how AI developers navigate between current technical constraints and imagined futures, and how the actual speed of AI progress consistently outpaces practitioner forecasting.
