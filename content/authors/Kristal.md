# Kristal

## Overview
A political economist whose research examines how computerization interacts with workers' bargaining power and the distribution of productivity gains. Their work on the capitalist machine investigates why the gains from automation have accrued disproportionately to capital rather than labor, situating AI in the longer history of technology and class-based distributional conflict.
