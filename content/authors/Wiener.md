# Wiener

## Overview
Norbert Wiener was an MIT mathematician and the founding theorist of cybernetics. His 1960 *Science* essay 'Some Moral and Technical Consequences of Automation' is one of the earliest scholarly warnings about automation's potential to displace human labor and concentrate economic power—anticipating debates about AI and employment that continue today.
