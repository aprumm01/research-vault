# Barrows

## Overview
Howard Barrows was a neurologist and medical educator at McMaster University who invented Problem-Based Learning (PBL) as a radical alternative to lecture-based medical training. His 1993 constructivist framing of PBL articulated how unstructured, clinically realistic problems drive self-directed inquiry and the integration of knowledge across domains. PBL spread from medicine to engineering, law, and general education worldwide.
