# Herring

## Overview
Susan C. Herring is a professor at Indiana University Bloomington who developed Computer-Mediated Discourse Analysis (CMDA)—a systematic methodology for studying language and communication in online environments. Her longitudinal web content analysis work and studies of race, gender, and self-presentation in digital spaces are widely cited across communication, linguistics, and HCI research.
