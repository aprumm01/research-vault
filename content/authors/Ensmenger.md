# Ensmenger

## Overview
Nathan Ensmenger is a professor in the Luddy School of Informatics, Computing, and Engineering at Indiana University whose historical and sociological research examines how computing became a profession and an industry. His book *The Computer Boys Take Over* (MIT Press, 2010) is a landmark account of how computer science was constructed as a predominantly male, credentialed discipline through deliberate boundary work—aptitude testing, personality stereotyping, and degree requirements—that systematically excluded the diverse, often female workforce that had built programming from the ground up.
