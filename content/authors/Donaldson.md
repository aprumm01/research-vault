# Donaldson

## Overview
A scholar of instructional design ethics contributing to the field's normative frameworks for professional conduct. Co-author on *Professional Ethics and Rules of Conduct in Instructional Design* with Sharon Smaldino and Susan Herring, examining the obligations and responsibilities of practitioners across diverse design contexts.
