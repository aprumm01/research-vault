# Fiesler

## Overview
Casey Fiesler is an associate professor of information science at the University of Colorado Boulder specializing in technology ethics, online communities, and fan culture. Her CHI 2020 work with Michael Muller on design fiction explored how speculative narrative methods can surface implicit values and enable critical reflection on AI's social futures.
