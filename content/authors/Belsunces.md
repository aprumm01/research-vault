# Belsunces

## Overview
Andreu Belsunces is a researcher at the Universitat Oberta de Catalunya (UOC) whose work draws on actor-network theory and performativity to theorize 'sociotechnical fictions' — forms of imagination operating inside the technosciences to materialize imagined entities through technological assemblage. His work bridges science and technology studies with media studies and technology theory.
