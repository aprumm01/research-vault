# Newby

## Overview
Timothy Newby is a professor of educational technology at Purdue University and co-author with Peggy Ertmer on the foundational learning theories comparison that has introduced generations of instructional designers to the behaviorist-cognitivist-constructivist spectrum and its implications for instructional strategy selection.
