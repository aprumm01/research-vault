# Kozma

## Overview
Robert Kozma is a researcher at SRI International whose 1994 response to Clark's 'media will never influence learning' argument contended that media and methods are inseparable—that media's attributes constrain and afford particular instructional methods in ways that shape learning. The Clark-Kozma debate remains a foundational exchange in educational technology theory.
