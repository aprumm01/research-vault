# Molenda

## Overview
Michael Molenda was a professor at Indiana University and co-editor of the AECT's 2008 definitional handbook with Alan Januszewski. He is also widely credited with helping codify the ADDIE model as a descriptive label for the iterative, systematic process underlying most formal instructional design work.
