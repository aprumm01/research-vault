# Mayer

## Overview
Richard E. Mayer is Distinguished Professor of Psychology at UC Santa Barbara and the leading researcher on multimedia learning. His cognitive theory of multimedia learning specifies how dual-channel processing, limited working memory, and active learning principles should guide the design of instructional materials combining words and visuals.
