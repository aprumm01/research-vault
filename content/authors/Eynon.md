# Eynon

## Overview
Rebecca Eynon is a professor at the Oxford Internet Institute whose research examines AI, digital technology, and education policy. Her 2020 study of how academia and industry construct AI narratives revealed systematic differences in how each sector legitimizes, frames, and contests artificial intelligence—illuminating the rhetorical work that shapes AI policy and public understanding.
