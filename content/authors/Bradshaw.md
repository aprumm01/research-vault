# Bradshaw

## Overview
A design educator and researcher publishing in *TechTrends*, Bradshaw examines how AI is reshaping the conceptual foundations and classroom practice of design and technology education—asking how educators should adapt curricula and pedagogy when generative tools alter what students can produce and how they learn to produce it.
