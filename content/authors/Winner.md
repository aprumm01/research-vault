# Winner

## Overview
Langdon Winner is a political theorist at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute whose foundational 1980 essay 'Do Artifacts Have Politics?' established that technological designs embed social relations, power structures, and values—not merely neutral functions. His work is a cornerstone of Science and Technology Studies and shapes how researchers think about AI's built-in assumptions and distributional effects.
