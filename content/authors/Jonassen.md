# Jonassen

## Overview
David Jonassen was a professor of education at the University of Missouri and a leading constructivist learning theorist. His frameworks on problem-solving environments, cognitive tools, and technology-mediated learning shaped how instructional designers think about supporting higher-order thinking and knowledge construction rather than content delivery.
