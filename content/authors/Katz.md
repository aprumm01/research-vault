# Katz

## Overview
A researcher examining AI's political and rhetorical construction. His 2017 SSRN working paper argues that narratives of an 'AI revolution' are manufactured through strategic institutional framing by industry and government actors rather than grounded in realized technical capability—positioning AI enthusiasm as ideological work with material consequences for labor and investment.
