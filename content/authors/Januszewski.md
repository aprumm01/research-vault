# Januszewski

## Overview
Alan Januszewski, formerly of SUNY Potsdam, co-edited the 2008 AECT definition of educational technology with Michael Molenda. The resulting volume is a definitive document for the instructional design and technology field, codifying the discipline's scope, terminology, and intellectual commitments at a key moment of technological change.
