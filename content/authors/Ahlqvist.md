# Ahlqvist

## Overview
Toni Ahlqvist is a researcher at the Finland Futures Research Centre (University of Turku) whose work bridges futures studies and science and technology studies, with a focus on how AI practitioners integrate anticipatory practices and futures imaginaries into their everyday work. His research examines the gap between near-term technical expectations and longer-range societal imaginaries in AI development.
