# Orlikowski

## Overview
Wanda Orlikowski is a professor at MIT Sloan School of Management whose structurational and practice-based theories of technology have been foundational in organizational informatics. Her study of Lotus Notes adoption at a law firm remains a canonical case study in how organizational practices shape—and are shaped by—information technology in ways that differ systematically from designers' intentions.
