# Attewell

## Overview
Paul Attewell is a sociologist at the CUNY Graduate Center whose research examines how technology shapes work, skill, and social stratification. His 1987 empirical analysis of the deskilling controversy assessed Braverman's labor process thesis and found that computerization often upskilled rather than deskilled workers in practice—a counterpoint that has shaped sociological debates about automation ever since.
