# Norman

## Overview
Don Norman is a cognitive scientist, design theorist, and founding director of the Design Lab at UC San Diego, widely regarded as one of the most influential figures in HCI and design education. Author of *The Design of Everyday Things* (1988, revised 2013), his work on affordances, feedback, and user-centered design has shaped generations of practitioners. His recent work with Michael Meyer on DesignX advocates for expanding design education to address complex sociotechnical challenges at societal scale.
