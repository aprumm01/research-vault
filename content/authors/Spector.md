# Spector

## Overview
J. Michael Spector is a professor at the University of North Texas whose theoretical work in educational technology has shaped how the field conceptualizes the foundations of learning design. His contributions to the Handbook of Research on Educational Communications and Technology provide systematic overviews of the field's intellectual commitments and research base.
