# Freeman

## Overview
Guo Freeman is a researcher at Clemson University whose CSCW work examines how the rise of generative AI is reshaping research agendas, educational priorities, and funding landscapes in HCI and computer-supported cooperative work. Her perspectives contributions advocate for the CSCW community to proactively shape GenAI's influence on its own field.
