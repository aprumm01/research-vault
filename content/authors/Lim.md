# Lim

## Overview
Youn-Kyung Lim is a professor at KAIST whose foundational ACM TOCHI paper on prototype anatomy articulated two core functions prototypes serve in design: as filters that constrain the design space by making certain qualities explicit, and as manifestations of design ideas that enable communication, reflection, and evaluation.
