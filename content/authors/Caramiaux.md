# Caramiaux

## Overview
Baptiste Caramiaux is a researcher at Inria/CNRS working at the intersection of human-computer interaction, creativity, and machine learning. His empirical work on generative AI and creative workers documents how practitioners in art, music, and design are narrating, valuing, and adapting their practices in response to AI-generated output.
