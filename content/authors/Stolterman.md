# Stolterman

## Overview
Erik Stolterman is a professor at Indiana University Bloomington and design theorist who articulates design practice as intentional action in the real world—distinct from both scientific inquiry and artistic expression. Co-author with Harold Nelson of *The Design Way*, his work examines the philosophical foundations of design as a discipline and profession.
