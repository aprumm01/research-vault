# Beresford

## Overview
A scholar affiliated with the International Society for Performance Improvement (ISPI), Beresford co-authored foundational historical and theoretical work on Human Performance Technology alongside Harold Stolovitch, tracing HPT's disciplinary evolution from behavioral engineering through systemic performance consulting.
