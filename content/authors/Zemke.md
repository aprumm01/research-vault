# Zemke

## Overview
Ron Zemke was a senior editor at *Training Magazine* and a practitioner-critic of formal instructional design. His 2000 co-authored 'Attack on ISD' with Jack Gordon challenged the field's reliance on ADDIE-style systematic processes, arguing for more agile, rapid development approaches suited to real workplace learning needs—a critique that helped spark the move toward action mapping and performance-first design.
