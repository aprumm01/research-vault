# Perez-Urbina

## Overview
Hector Perez-Urbina is a researcher at the University of Cambridge whose critical discourse analysis work examines the 'AI executive elite' — the small group of individuals who control AI funding, development, and public narrative. His Techno-Supremacy Doctrine (TSD) framework traces how AI leadership discourse systematically positions technology as inherently superior to human judgment in solving societal problems.
