# Reiser

## Overview
Robert A. Reiser is a professor emeritus at Florida State University and historian of the instructional design and technology field. His *TechTrends* writing traces how the field has defined, renamed, and repositioned itself across decades in response to shifting theoretical commitments and technological contexts—from audiovisual education through programmed instruction to digital learning.
