# Pershing

## Overview
A scholar and practitioner in Human Performance Technology who edited the foundational ISPI Handbook that codified HPT's models, methods, and professional standards. The Handbook remains a primary reference for performance improvement practitioners, synthesizing decades of theory and applied research across the field.
