# Van Tiem

## Overview
Darlene Van Tiem is a professor at the University of Michigan-Dearborn and co-author of foundational HPT frameworks published through ISPI. Her work provides systematic models for diagnosing organizational performance problems and designing comprehensive interventions that address environmental, managerial, and individual factors.
