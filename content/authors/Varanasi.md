# Varanasi

## Overview
A researcher whose 2025 systematic review with Law synthesizes evidence on how practitioners proactively craft their roles in response to generative AI-driven work transformation. Applying job crafting theory, their work examines the individual-level adaptive strategies through which workers renegotiate task boundaries and professional identity under conditions of rapid AI-driven change.
