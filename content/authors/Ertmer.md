# Ertmer

## Overview
Peggy Ertmer is a professor of educational technology at Purdue University known for her comparative synthesis of learning theories. Her 1993 paper with Timothy Newby—comparing behaviorism, cognitivism, and constructivism across critical instructional design features—remains one of the most-cited introductions to learning theory for practitioners and graduate students in the field.
