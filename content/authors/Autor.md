# Autor

## Overview
David Autor is an economist at MIT and co-director of the MIT Work of the Future Task Force. His tasks-based framework distinguishes routine from non-routine cognitive and manual tasks to explain why automation displaces some jobs while complementing others. His widely cited work argues that structural occupational transformation, not mass unemployment, is the characteristic outcome of labor-saving technology.
