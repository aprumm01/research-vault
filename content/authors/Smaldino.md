# Smaldino

## Overview
Sharon Smaldino is a professor of instructional technology at Northern Illinois University who has contributed to the field's ethical frameworks and professional standards. Her co-authored work on professional ethics and rules of conduct in instructional design addresses the normative responsibilities practitioners carry in diverse educational and organizational contexts.
