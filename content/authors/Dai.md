# Dai

## Overview
An AI education researcher whose work explores pedagogical approaches that integrate embodied, unplugged activities with computational tools for holistic AI literacy. Their constructionist frameworks examine how learners develop meaningful understanding of AI concepts through physical interaction and making before engaging with code and algorithms.
