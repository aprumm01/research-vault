# Katzenbach

## Overview
Christian Katzenbach is a media and internet researcher at the Alexander von Humboldt Institute for Internet and Society (HIIG) in Berlin whose work examines how future imaginaries shape the governance and development of digital technologies. He has published extensively on platform regulation, AI governance, and the performative role of visions of technological futures in shaping policy and industry practice.
