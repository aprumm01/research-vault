# Zuboff

## Overview
Shoshana Zuboff is an emerita professor at Harvard Business School and foundational theorist of computerization's social effects. Her 1985 analysis of workplace computing introduced the automate/informate distinction—arguing that information technology does not merely replace labor but transforms the nature of work, knowledge, and organizational power. Her later work coined 'surveillance capitalism.'
