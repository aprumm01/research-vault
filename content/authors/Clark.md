# Clark

## Overview
Richard E. Clark is an educational psychologist at the University of Southern California known for his 1994 argument that instructional methods—not media—are the active ingredients in learning. His debate with Robert Kozma over media's role shaped two decades of educational technology research and remains a touchstone for discussions of technology's impact on learning.
