# Marquette

## Overview
A methodologist examining the relationship between inductive qualitative approaches and computational text analysis. Their comparative study of grounded theory and structural topic modeling investigates whether the two methods converge, diverge, or offer complementary insights—with implications for mixed-methods researchers studying AI discourse and organizational texts.
