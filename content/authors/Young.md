# Young

## Overview
A researcher affiliated with the Oxford Internet Institute and co-author with Rebecca Eynon on studies of how AI is rhetorically constructed across academic and industry contexts. Their work examines the discursive strategies through which different institutional actors legitimize, challenge, and contest AI narratives.
