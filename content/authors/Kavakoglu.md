# Kavakoglu

## Overview
Aysegul Akcay Kavakoglu is a researcher and design educator at Istanbul Technical University whose work focuses on AI-driven creativity in early design education within Industry 5.0 frameworks. Her research examines how generative AI tools can be integrated into foundational design curricula as augmentation for creative thinking rather than substitutes for it.
