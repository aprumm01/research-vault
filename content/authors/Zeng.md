# Zeng

## Overview
Jing Zeng is a communication researcher at the University of Zurich whose work examines platform governance, AI imaginaries, and the cross-national dynamics of technology discourse. Her research on news media imaginaries and stakeholder negotiations of AI futures bridges media studies and science and technology studies with comparative empirical methods.
