# Perrolle

## Overview
Judith Perrolle is a sociologist whose early work on the rationalization of professional knowledge work anticipated contemporary debates about AI and expert labor. Her concept of 'intellectual assembly lines' described how computerization fragments and routinizes knowledge work in ways that transfer control from workers to management—a precursor to later accounts of AI-driven cognitive deskilling.
