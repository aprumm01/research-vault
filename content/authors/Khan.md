# Khan

## Overview
An HCI and design researcher whose empirical work examines how UX professionals and designers perceive and integrate AI as a creative collaborator. Their findings document a shift from AI-as-tool toward AI-as-partner framings among practitioners, with implications for how design expertise and creative agency are understood and attributed.
