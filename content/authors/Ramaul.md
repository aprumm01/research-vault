# Ramaul

## Overview
An organizational theorist contributing to the reconceptualization of AI in management and organizational studies. Their work argues for theoretical frameworks that move beyond binary displacement/augmentation accounts to capture the relational, processual, and power-laden dynamics through which AI becomes embedded in organizational life.
