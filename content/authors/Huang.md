# Huang

## Overview
Chieh-Ju Huang is a design educator at Yuan Ze University in Taiwan whose research examines the integration of UX principles and generative AI into UI design education through Problem-Based Learning approaches. His empirical curriculum studies assess how GenAI-augmented PBL affects student creativity, iterative design practice, and professional readiness.
