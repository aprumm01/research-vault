# Gordon

## Overview
Jack Gordon was a senior editor at *Training Magazine* who, with Ron Zemke, published a high-profile practitioner critique of formal ISD in 2000. Their 'Attack on ISD' argued that ADDIE-style systematic design processes were too slow, cumbersome, and theory-driven for the speed and informality of real workplace learning needs—sparking a field-wide debate about the relevance of formal instructional design methodology.
