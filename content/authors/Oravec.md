# Oravec

## Overview
Jo Ann Oravec is a researcher at the University of Wisconsin whose interdisciplinary work spans technology ethics, labor, and AI policy. Her critical review of David Autor's labor economics research surfaces the assumptions and omissions in mainstream framings of automation and employment—arguing for more attentive accounts of power, precarity, and the political economy of technological change.
