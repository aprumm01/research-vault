# Vibe Coding for Product Design (Li et al.)

**DOI:** [10.1145/nnnnnnn.nnnnnnn](https://doi.org/10.1145/nnnnnnn.nnnnnnn)

## Summary
Li et al. investigate "vibe coding" — a workflow where product team members express design intent in natural language and AI translates it directly into functional prototypes and code — through interviews with 22 product professionals across enterprise, startup, and academic contexts. They find that vibe coding follows a four-stage workflow (ideation, generation, debugging, review) that accelerates iteration and lowers barriers but introduces new tensions around code reliability, trust asymmetries, and de-skilling. The paper argues for responsible human-AI collaboration frameworks that balance efficiency-driven prototyping with reflective design practice.

## Key Concepts
- Vibe coding (natural language → functional prototype via AI)
- Four-stage workflow: ideation → generation → debugging → review
- Deskilling and creative autonomy preservation
- Disclosure and ownership in AI-assisted work
- Trust asymmetries within product teams

## Theoretical Framework
Responsible human-AI collaboration lens; draws on HCI literature on AI-assisted design and CSCW literature on work transformation to frame vibe coding as a sociotechnical phenomenon with individual and organizational implications.

## Methods
Semi-structured interviews with 22 product team members (designers, engineers, PMs) across enterprise companies, startups, and academia; qualitative thematic analysis.

## Main Arguments
- Vibe coding follows a consistent four-stage workflow that accelerates ideation but introduces verification and integration challenges
- "Intending the right design" (efficiency) conflicts with "designing the right intention" (reflection) — a core tension
- Code unreliability and AI over-reliance create new risks for product quality and team trust
- Social stigma and disclosure norms around AI use remain unresolved in professional contexts

## Limitations & Critiques
Sample skews toward tech-forward contexts; vibe coding is an emerging practice and findings may shift rapidly as tools mature; the study captures a snapshot of a transitional moment in professional practice.
