# Design Education Methodology Using AI (Hashem & Hakeem, 2024)

## Summary
Proposes an AI-integrated design education methodology drawing on case study evidence from MSA University in Cairo, examining how AI tools can be embedded in the design process as pedagogical instruments rather than productivity shortcuts. Argues for structured AI methodology aligned with design values of iteration, critique, and craft, positioning AI integration as a curricular design problem requiring deliberate sequencing and scaffolding.

## Key Concepts
- **AI-integrated methodology**: A deliberate curricular approach to embedding AI tools in design process instruction
- **Design iteration with AI**: Using AI-generated outputs as raw material for critique and design development
- **Pedagogical instrument vs. shortcut**: The distinction between AI as a learning scaffold and AI as a bypass of learning

## Main Arguments
1. AI in design education requires a methodology, not merely tool access
2. Effective AI integration preserves the iterative and critical core of design process pedagogy
3. Case study evidence from MSA suggests structured AI methodology improves both output quality and process literacy
