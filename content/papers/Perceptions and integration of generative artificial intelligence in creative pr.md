# Perceptions and integration of generative artificial intelligence in creative practices and industries

## Summary
Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is fundamentally transforming notions of creativity and creative production across disciplines, yet a comprehensive understanding of professional attitudes and integration patterns remains challenging This scoping review examines how creative professionals perceive and integrate GenAI technologies across four domains: visual art and design, writing and literature, performing arts, and environmental and spatial design Following PRISMA-ScR guidelines, we analysed 57 papers (2022–2025) from multiple databases, focusing mainly on empirically based studies of professional creative practice.

## Key Concepts
- GenAI transformation of creative production
- Professional attitudes toward AI in creative work
- Integration patterns across creative domains
- Visual art and design applications
- Writing and literature automation
- Performing arts and AI collaboration
- Environmental and spatial design with AI
- PRISMA-ScR systematic review methodology
- Empirically-based studies of creative practice
- Cross-disciplinary creativity analysis

## Theoretical Framework
- Creativity theory in AI-augmented contexts
- Technology adoption in creative industries
- Practice-based research frameworks
- Sociotechnical systems in creative work
- Professional identity and technological change

## Methods
- Scoping review following PRISMA-ScR guidelines
- Analysis of 57 papers (2022-2025)
- Multiple database search strategy
- Focus on empirical studies of professional practice
- Cross-domain synthesis across four creative areas

## Main Arguments
- GenAI fundamentally transforming creativity and creative production concepts
- Professional attitudes vary significantly across creative domains
- Integration patterns reveal domain-specific adoption strategies
- Comprehensive understanding of professional attitudes remains challenging
- Visual arts showing distinct integration patterns from performing arts
- Writing/literature professionals express unique concerns about AI
- Spatial design reveals different affordances of GenAI tools
- Recent emergence (2022-2025) limits longitudinal understanding
- Need for empirical grounding in studies of creative AI practice
- Cross-disciplinary analysis reveals common themes and unique domain challenges

## Limitations & Critiques
- Limited to publications from 2022-2025, early stage of GenAI adoption
- Scoping review methodology provides breadth over depth
- Rapidly evolving technology may outdate findings quickly
- Potential publication bias toward positive or novel applications
- May not capture full range of creative professional experiences
- Geographic and cultural diversity of studies not specified
- Varying definitions of "creativity" across domains not fully reconciled
- Limited longitudinal data on lasting impacts of GenAI on creative practice
- Power dynamics and economic impacts underexplored
