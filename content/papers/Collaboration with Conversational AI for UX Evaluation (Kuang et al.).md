## Summary
Kuang et al. investigate how UX practitioners can collaborate with conversational AI assistants for UX evaluation tasks, comparing voice and text interaction modes and examining how AI can help practitioners formulate better evaluation questions. The study finds that AI assistants can meaningfully support UX evaluation question formulation, with text interaction generally more precise for professional tasks, and identifies collaboration patterns that enhance evaluation quality. The paper contributes practical knowledge about human-AI collaboration in professional UX evaluation workflows.

## Key Concepts

- Voice vs. text AI interaction for professional tasks
- AI-assisted UX evaluation question formulation
- Human-AI collaboration in evaluation workflows

## Theoretical Framework
Human-AI collaboration and conversational agent research frameworks; examines how interaction modality (voice vs. text) mediates the quality of professional collaboration with AI assistants.

## Methods
Comparative empirical study; participants interact with conversational AI assistants via voice and text to formulate UX evaluation questions; analysis of collaboration patterns and output quality; published in ACM CHI or related venue.

## Main Arguments
- Conversational AI assistants can support UX evaluation through collaborative question formulation
- Text interaction is generally more effective than voice for professional UX evaluation tasks
- Productive human-AI collaboration for evaluation requires specific interaction strategies
- AI-assisted UX evaluation has potential but requires careful integration into professional workflows
