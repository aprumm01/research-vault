# Responsible AI Governance


## Summary
Responsible AI Governance is a thematic node examining the institutional, regulatory, and organizational structures through which AI development and deployment are overseen, regulated, and held accountable. Distinct from Responsible AI Ethics (which focuses on normative principles), this node addresses governance mechanisms: who has authority over AI systems, how compliance is enforced, what regulatory frameworks exist or are emerging, and how organizations can embed accountability into their AI development processes. In this research vault, the node is most relevant to understanding how design organizations, UX teams, and educational institutions can build governance structures that ensure AI tools are used responsibly within professional practice.

## Key Concepts
- AI regulation: EU AI Act, US Executive Orders, voluntary frameworks vs. binding rules
- Organizational AI governance: internal review, impact assessment, accountability structures
- Algorithmic auditing and third-party assessment
- Model cards, datasheets, and transparency documentation
- Human oversight requirements for high-risk AI applications
- Governance gaps: where current frameworks fail to address emerging AI harms

## Theoretical Framework
Regulatory theory; organizational governance; public policy and technology law; critical AI studies; connects to Responsible AI and Ethics (normative) and Algorithmic Bias and Fairness (technical); situated in this vault as the policy and institutional layer of the AI ethics community.
