# Artificial intelligence a promising technology

## Summary
This paper addresses the question of how the ups and downs in the development of artificial intelligence (AI) since its inception can be explained It focuses on the development of artificial intelligence in Germany since the 1970s, and particularly on its current dynamics An assumption is made that a mere reference to rapid advances in information technologies and the various methods and concepts of artificial intelligence in recent decades cannot adequately explain these dynamics, because from a social science perspective, this is an oversimplified, technology-centred explanation.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/Promising Technology]] - Technologies driven by expectations and visions about future capabilities that coordinate actors and mobilize resources
- [[concepts/Technological Promise]] - Narratives offering visions that justify expectations and open innovation paths for actors
- [[concepts/AI Winter]] - Periods of reduced funding and interest in AI following disappointed expectations
- [[concepts/Socio-technical Field]] - Institutionalized constellations of actors from science, policy, and business coordinating around technology
- [[concepts/Technological Utopia]] - Vision of technology as means to overcome societal challenges and create better society
- [[concepts/AI Community]] - Network of scientists, policymakers, and businesses driving AI development and promises
- [[concepts/Innovation Ecosystem]] - Open networks integrating knowledge domains across disciplines and sectors
- [[concepts/Solutionism]] - Expectation that technology can autonomously solve complex societal problems

## Theoretical Framework
- [[frameworks/Social Construction of Technology]] - Technology development shaped by social and societal conditions intertwined with technical advances
- [[frameworks/Sociology of Expectations]] - How expectations about future opportunities influence course and direction of technological innovations
- [[frameworks/Innovation Systems]] - National and multi-level systems coordinating actors, knowledge transfer, and resources

## Methods
- Qualitative discourse analysis of grey documents, policy statements, websites, and specialist publications
- Reinterpretation of existing research on digitalization
- 19 expert interviews with AI professionals in business and academia (October 2021 - February 2022)

## Main Arguments
- AI dynamics cannot be explained solely by technical advances; social science perspective reveals technological promises drive development across boom-bust cycles
- Germany's AI development since 1970s shows four distinct stages each characterized by renewed promises: scientific niche (1970s), commercial dawning (1980s), crisis (late 1980s), consolidation (1990s-2000s), and current boom (2010s-present)
- Current AI boom driven by far-reaching technological promises about deep learning capabilities, supported by influential AI community, extensive policy support, and public discourse legitimization
- AI-specific innovation mode requires transdisciplinary knowledge transfer, fluid cooperation, agile strategies, heterogeneous actors, wider skill profiles, and socio-technical understanding
- Technological promise of AI employs persuasive rhetoric through ambiguous metaphors, inevitability framing, growth acceleration claims, generalization of success cases, and social legitimacy securing

## Limitations & Critiques
- Limited to German AI development context, though closely linked to international trends particularly from United States
- Technology-centered explanations oversimplify complex social and societal conditioning factors shaping AI dynamics
- Fundamental functional problems persist: poor handling of "open worlds," inability to incorporate everyday knowledge, lack of explainability in machine learning, and uncertain data quality requirements
- Promises often exaggerated relative to actual system capabilities, leading to disappointed expectations and withdrawal of key actors
- Ambiguity of "AI" term enables vague promises disconnected from concrete application limits and adaptation needs
- Criticism that current dynamics may lead to new AI winter as performance capabilities overestimated and gap between expectations and reality becomes apparent
- Questions whether artificial general intelligence can overcome fundamental technological hurdles or if human intelligence fundamentally differs from computation
