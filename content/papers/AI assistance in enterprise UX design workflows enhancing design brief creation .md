# AI assistance in enterprise UX design workflows enhancing design brief creation for designers

## Summary
TYPE Original Research PUBLISHED 12 November 2024 DOI 10 1404647 OPEN ACCESS EDITED BY Francesca Cordella, Campus Bio-Medico University, Italy REVIEWED BY Cássio Leonardo Rodrigues, Universidade Federal de Goiás, Brazil Rae Yule Kim, Montclair State University, United States AI assistance in enterprise UX design workflows: enhancing design brief creation for designers Zijian Zhu , Hyemin Lee , Younghwan Pan * and Pengyu Cai.

## Key Concepts
- AI-assisted design brief creation in enterprise contexts
- ChatGPT integration in UX design workflows
- Generative AI roles: Generators and Assistants
- Cross-disciplinary knowledge gaps addressed by AI
- Efficiency and accuracy in design project management
- AI tool impacts on designer behavior and client perceptions
- Design brief elements: accuracy (59%), efficiency (41%)
- User experience mapping of AI-integrated workflows

## Theoretical Framework
- Generative AI applications framework (Strobel: Generators, Reimaginators, Synthesizers, Assistants, Enablers)
- Design brief creation theory
- Project management and design efficiency models

## Methods
- Expert interviews with 8 designers (5 UX, 3 visual design backgrounds from diverse sectors)
- Experimental testing with medium-scale virtual project ("Gaming Community" platform)
- Third-party expert evaluations using 5-point evaluation system
- User experience mapping methodology
- Two-phase task execution to reduce fatigue effects
- Documentation of actual work scenarios and behaviors

## Main Arguments
- AI language models can help designers overcome cross-disciplinary knowledge gaps by interpreting requirements from unfamiliar fields
- Design briefs are critical tools in initial project stages requiring substantial time/effort; AI can significantly enhance efficiency
- Traditional design brief challenges include poor information transmission, changing client requirements, time/cost pressures, and unfamiliar domain work
- AI tools show mixed but promising impacts: save time, help organize tasks, but require careful verification of accuracy
- Integration of "AI thinking" into workflows can enhance both efficiency and design quality
- Designers universally use AI tools (100% adoption) but with varying degrees of reliance
- Clear objectives, functional scope, historical data, and documentation remain crucial regardless of AI integration
- Accuracy ensures right direction; efficiency involves cost control, resource allocation, team collaboration

## Limitations & Critiques
- AI-provided information occasionally contains inaccuracies requiring verification and careful handling
- Academic community still in early stages of defining AI-assisted work patterns
- Limited research specifically on AI's impact on design brief creation processes
- Lack of mature, comprehensive application standards for integrating AI into enterprise workflows
- Most existing studies employ big data quantitative methods, lacking detailed descriptions of specific work scenarios and actual processes
- Risks associated with GenAI include bias, transparency issues, hallucinations, misuse, and societal impact
- Need for proactive measures to ensure ethical, safe, and fair use of GenAI in professional contexts
- Variable designer reliance on AI tools suggests inconsistent integration patterns
- Study focuses on virtual project; real-world complexity may introduce additional challenges
- Limited sample size (8 designers) may not fully capture diversity of enterprise UX practices
- Long-term effects on designer skill development and professional judgment not addressed
