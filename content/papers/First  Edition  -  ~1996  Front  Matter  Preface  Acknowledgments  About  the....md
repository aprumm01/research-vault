## Summary
Jonassen, Driscoll, and colleagues' 1996 Handbook of Research on Educational Communications and Technology represents a comprehensive synthesis of the field's foundational research, covering learning theory, instructional design, media and technology research, diffusion of innovation, and evaluation methods. As the first major research handbook in the field, it established educational communications and technology (ECT) as a serious research discipline with its own methods, phenomena, and theoretical traditions. The handbook's breadth makes it a foundational reference for understanding the intellectual roots of educational technology and instructional design.

## Key Concepts
- Educational communications and technology (ECT) as a field
- Foundational research paradigms in educational technology
- Learning theory and instructional design connections
- Media comparison research and media effects
- Diffusion of innovation in educational technology

## Theoretical Framework
Encyclopedic meta-theoretical framework; draws on behavioral, cognitive, and constructivist learning theory; systems theory; communication theory; and empirical educational research traditions.

## Methods
Edited handbook synthesizing research across ECT's foundational areas; chapter-length reviews of research programs by field experts; no single primary methodology.

## Main Arguments
- Educational communications and technology has developed a distinctive research tradition worthy of systematic documentation
- The field's research spans from psychological learning theory to sociology of technology diffusion and system design
- Multiple theoretical perspectives are necessary to address the full range of ECT phenomena
- Empirical research methods must be chosen to match the phenomena being studied rather than applying a single paradigm uniformly

## Limitations & Critiques
As a 1996 volume, it predates the internet era, digital learning environments, and contemporary AI — primarily of historical and foundational value; the field has evolved significantly since publication.
