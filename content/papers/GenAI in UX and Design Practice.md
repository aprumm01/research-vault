# GenAI in UX and Design Practice


## Summary
GenAI in UX and Design Practice is the primary thematic community in this research vault, capturing research on how generative AI tools are being integrated into UX design workflows, how designers perceive and respond to AI capabilities, and what this integration means for design practice, professional identity, and education. Papers in this community range from empirical studies of AI tool adoption across design process phases, to qualitative accounts of how designers negotiate AI collaboration, to critical analyses of how AI reshapes design expertise, creativity, and professional value. The community is closely connected to design education, AI ethics, and workforce transformation topics.

## Key Concepts
- AI tool integration across the Design Thinking process phases
- Human-AI creative collaboration models in UX practice
- Designer perceptions of AI: assistive vs. generative vs. replacement framings
- Vibe coding, prompt engineering, and natural language design workflows
- AI adoption differentials: experienced vs. junior designers; individual vs. team
- Design authenticity, ownership, and professional identity under AI augmentation

## Theoretical Framework
HCI research on AI-assisted design; design cognition; professional practice studies; situated in this vault at the intersection of AI in Design Education, Cognitive Offloading and De-skilling, Human-AI Creative Collaboration, and Responsible AI and Ethics communities; draws on practitioner interview studies, survey research, and design process analysis.
