# HCI Education and Pedagogy

## Summary
HCI Education and Pedagogy is a thematic node encompassing research on how to teach human-computer interaction, UX design, and interaction design effectively -- including curricular choices, pedagogical models, studio practices, and the emerging challenge of AI tools in design classrooms. In this research vault, the node bridges instructional design theory, studio pedagogy, and AI in design education communities. Papers address questions such as: how should HCI curricula prepare students for AI-augmented practice? What pedagogical approaches (studio critique, project-based learning, co-creation) develop the judgment and reflection skills that AI cannot replace? How does GenAI change what students need to learn and how?

## Key Concepts
- Studio-based pedagogy as the dominant model in HCI/UX education
- Curriculum design for AI-augmented practice
- Scaffolding and formative feedback in design education
- Metacognitive development through design critique and reflection
- AI as both subject of study and tool within HCI education
- Assessment challenges when AI can perform design tasks

## Theoretical Framework
Design education research; studio pedagogy tradition; constructivist and problem-based learning frameworks; instructional design theory; connected in this vault to AI in Design Education, Design Judgment, Studio Pedagogy, and Metacognitive Support communities; draws on both pedagogical research and practitioner reflection.
