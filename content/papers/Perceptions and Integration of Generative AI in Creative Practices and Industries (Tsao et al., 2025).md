# Perceptions and Integration of Generative AI in Creative Practices and Industries (Tsao et al., 2025)

## Summary
Scoping review and conceptual model examining how creative professionals across visual art and design, writing and literature, and performing arts perceive and integrate generative AI technologies. Finds that professional adoption patterns vary significantly by creative domain, with recurring tensions between GenAI as creativity enhancer and GenAI as threat to authorship, professional identity, and labor conditions in creative industries.

## Key Concepts
- **GenAI in creative industries**: The adoption of generative AI tools across design, writing, visual art, and performance
- **Authorship and identity**: How GenAI challenges professional claims to creative originality and authorial agency
- **Conceptual model**: A cross-domain framework mapping GenAI integration patterns and professional attitudes

## Main Arguments
1. GenAI adoption in creative domains is domain-specific, not uniform across art, design, and writing
2. Professional identity and authorship concerns are the most consistent cross-domain tension in GenAI integration
3. A conceptual model of GenAI integration patterns enables comparative analysis and curriculum design for creative education
