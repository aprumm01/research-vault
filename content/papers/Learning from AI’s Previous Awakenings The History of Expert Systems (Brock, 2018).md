# Learning from AI’s Previous Awakenings: The History of Expert Systems (Brock, 2018)


## Summary

\
## Summary
Presents an institutional and technical history of expert systems as a "previous awakening" of AI—one of several cycles of intense interest and commercialization followed by disappointment—arguing that today's AI developers should study this history to avoid repeating its failures. Centers on the "knowledge acquisition bottleneck," hype outpacing capability, and misaligned commercialization incentives as structural causes of the first AI winter.

## Key Concepts
- Expert systems and AI winter
- Knowledge acquisition bottleneck
- AI hype cycle
- Commercialization of AI research
- Institutional history of technology

## Theoretical Framework
History of technology with an institutional focus; AI development cycles as recurring patterns of enthusiasm, investment, and disillusionment; lessons-from-history framing for contemporary AI practitioners.

## Methods
Historical analysis drawing on oral histories, archival research, and the May 2018 Computer History Museum pioneer meeting; analysis of technical failures and business dynamics in the expert systems era.

## Main Arguments
- Expert systems succeeded technically in narrow domains but failed commercially due to the knowledge acquisition bottleneck—the enormous labor required to capture and maintain expert knowledge
- Unrealistic expectations set by researchers and vendors created a hype cycle that crashed when expert systems could not scale
- Today's AI developers face structurally similar risks from unchecked hype, narrow benchmarks, and misaligned incentives
- Deep study of the expert systems history offers practical lessons for responsible AI development and deployment

## Limitations & Critiques
Historical perspective may not map cleanly onto deep learning, which learns from data rather than hand-coded rules; the expert systems case is specific in ways that limit direct analogy; the "lessons" are prescriptive and their application to current AI is not straightforward.
