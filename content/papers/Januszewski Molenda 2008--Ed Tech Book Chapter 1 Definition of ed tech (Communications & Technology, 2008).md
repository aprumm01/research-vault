# Januszewski Molenda 2008--Ed Tech Book Chapter 1 Definition of ed tech

## Summary
First, the definition of the concept: Educational technology is the study and ethical practice of facilitating learning and improving performance by creating, using, and managing appropriate technological processes and resources Elements of the Definition Each of the key terms used in the definition will be discussed as to its intended meaning in the context of the definition The theoretical understanding of, as well as the practice of, educational technology, requires continual knowledge construction and refinement through research and reflective practice, which are encompassed in the.

## Key Concepts
- Definition of educational technology as study and ethical practice
- Facilitating learning and improving performance
- Creating, using, and managing technological processes and resources
- Ethical practice in educational technology
- Knowledge construction through research and reflective practice
- Appropriate technology selection and use
- Performance improvement beyond learning
- Integration of theory and practice
- Technological processes as distinct from technological tools

## Theoretical Framework
- Systems theory applied to educational technology
- Constructivist epistemology for knowledge building in the field
- Ethical frameworks for technology use in education
- Performance technology and improvement science

## Methods
- Conceptual analysis
- Definitional framework development
- Historical review of field evolution
- Synthesis of theory and practice domains

## Main Arguments
- Educational technology encompasses both study (theory/research) and practice
- Ethical considerations must be central to educational technology work
- Field focuses on both learning facilitation and performance improvement
- "Appropriate" technology means context-specific, not newest or most advanced
- Creating, using, and managing represent distinct activities requiring different expertise
- Technological processes (systematic approaches) are as important as resources (tools)
- Field requires ongoing knowledge construction through research and reflection
- Educational technology is more than instructional design or media use
- Performance improvement broadens scope beyond traditional learning outcomes

## Limitations & Critiques
- Definition may be too broad to provide clear professional boundaries
- "Appropriate technology" remains somewhat ambiguous
- Relationship between study and practice not fully articulated
- Ethical dimensions acknowledged but not deeply explored
- May conflate disparate activities under single umbrella term
- Performance improvement focus potentially dilutes learning emphasis
- Limited guidance on resolving tensions between different elements
- Does not fully address power dynamics in educational technology implementation
