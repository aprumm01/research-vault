## Summary
Tenakwah and Watson argue that successfully integrating AI and automation into the workforce requires proactive strategic human resource management (HRM) that aligns AI adoption with organizational goals. Through a conceptual review drawing on industry examples, they develop a framework for building AI-literate, adaptable workforces that can collaborate effectively with intelligent systems. The paper emphasizes leadership responsibility in enabling workforce transitions through reskilling and new AI-centric role creation.

## Key Concepts
- Strategic HRM and AI
- Workforce adaptability
- AI literacy
- Organizational change management
- Human-AI collaboration

## Theoretical Framework
Strategic HRM theory frames organizational preparation for AI adoption, with attention to change management, leadership development, and workforce planning processes.

## Methods
Conceptual framework development; literature review and analysis of industry case studies; expert insights synthesis.

## Main Arguments
- AI integration requires strategic alignment between technology adoption and human capital development
- Organizations must create new AI-centric roles rather than simply automating existing ones
- HR leadership bears primary responsibility for developing AI-literate and adaptable workforces
- Workforce planning must anticipate both displacement risks and emerging skill needs
