# (Forthcoming, 2014). New Media & Society. Race, Gender, and Self-Presentation...

## Summary
Herring & Kapidzic (2014) examine racial and gender identity self-presentation in teen profile photographs on social media using systematic content analysis. The study codes a sample of profile images for gender expression, racial cues, and self-presentation style. Findings reveal intersectional patterns in how teen identity is performed visually online, with significant variation by race and gender in pose, gaze, and clothing choices.

## Theoretical Framework
Self-presentation theory; intersectionality; visual CMC analysis.

## Methods
Content analysis; web content analysis; coding scheme; systematic image coding; computer-mediated communication analysis
