# The impact of the development of artificial intelligence on computer graphic design

## Summary
The article is aimed at studying the impact of artificial intelligence(AI) on graphic design and presenting a possible future vision of the industry as a result of its development Since the advent of artificial intelligence in graphic design, it has posed a serious challenge to the traditional graphic design industry and designers In the article, we will look at how AI is used in graphic design, including task automation, the creation of new design ideas and the development of individual design, we will also consider the potential advantages and disadvantages of AI in computer art design.

## Key Concepts
- AI automation of graphic design tasks
- Task automation vs. creative innovation
- Three-dimensional design expansion
- Web design and automated website creation
- Personalization through AI algorithms
- Template-based design tools vs. professional applications
- Machine Learning in design
- Design uniformity and loss of uniqueness concerns

## Theoretical Framework
Survey-based analysis examining AI's transformative impact on graphic design practice, drawing on professional perspectives and experimental comparison between AI-based and traditional design workflows.

## Methods
Survey of graphic design professionals and educators in Armenia (92% of respondents used AI tools), conducted from November 2023 to January 2024 through online platform and State Academy of Fine Arts of Armenia. Experimental study comparing two student groups: one using only AI tools (Midjourney AI, Leonardo AI), the other using traditional programs (Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator).

## Main Arguments
- AI poses serious challenge to traditional graphic design industry but offers significant advantages
- AI excels at task automation, time savings, and increasing productivity but cannot achieve independent innovation
- Professional graphic design applications (Adobe) focus on automation while maintaining designer control over creative process
- AI tools democratize design for non-specialists through template-based systems (Wix ADI, Canva, Adobe Spark)
- AI enables personalization and interaction but risks creating uniform, generic designs lacking uniqueness
- 73% of survey participants believe AI positively affects graphic designers' work
- Experimental evidence shows AI tools produce faster results but traditional methods maintain quality
- AI should support and enhance designer work, not replace creative professionals

## Limitations & Critiques
- Survey limited to Armenian design community, reducing generalizability
- Small sample experimental study with only two student groups
- Heavy reliance on author's personal experience as practitioner and educator
- Limited discussion of specific AI capabilities and technical mechanisms
- Does not adequately address copyright and ownership issues in AI-generated design
- Lacks longitudinal data on long-term impacts of AI adoption
- Minimal engagement with ethical implications beyond brief mention
- Geographic and cultural bias toward Armenian design education context
