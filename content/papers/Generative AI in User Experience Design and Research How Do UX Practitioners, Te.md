# Generative AI in User Experience Design and Research: How Do UX Practitioners, Teams, and Companies Use GenAI in Industry?

**DOI:** [10.1145/3643834.3660720](https://doi.org/10.1145/3643834.3660720)

**Venue:** acm.org/doi/10.1145/3643834.3660720 . . RESEARCH-ARTICLE Gen
**Institution:** ry? MACY TAKAFFOLI, University of Waterloo, Waterloo, ON, Ca

## Summary
Interviews 24 UX practitioners from eight countries with varying roles and seniority levels to examine how GenAI is adopted at the individual, team, and company levels in the UX sector. Finds a significant absence of formal company GenAI policies, no team-wide practices, individual use favored for research and writing tasks but limited for visual design activities, and widespread need for prompting and output evaluation training.

## Key Concepts
- GenAI adoption in UX practice
- Company GenAI policies
- Team-level collaboration with AI
- Prompt engineering challenges
- UX research vs. design tasks with AI

## Theoretical Framework
Human-AI collaboration; organizational technology adoption; HCI industry practice research; positions GenAI as a wave requiring both individual and institutional adaptation.

## Methods
Qualitative content analysis of online interviews with 24 UX practitioners (designers, researchers, managers) from 8 countries and companies ranging from startups to large corporations; 10 themes coded under 3 dimensions (policies, practices, challenges).

## Main Arguments
- Companies largely lack formal GenAI policies, relying on informal caution or individual judgment
- UX teams use GenAI individually rather than collectively, without shared workflows
- GenAI is valuable for text-heavy research tasks but practitioners report limitations for wireframing, prototyping, and graphic design
- Practitioners need better training on writing effective prompts and evaluating AI output quality

## Limitations & Critiques
Qualitative snapshot during a period of rapid change; 24 participants represent only a slice of global UX practice; findings may not hold as GenAI tools evolve and company policies mature.
