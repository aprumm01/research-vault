# Spectrum of AI Futures Imaginaries by AI Practitioners in Finland and Singapore (Hautala & Heino, 2023)

## Summary
Empirical study mapping the spectrum of AI futures imaginaries held by AI practitioners in Finland and Singapore, revealing significant variation between dystopian and utopian visions even among the technical community responsible for building AI systems. A distinctive finding is practitioners' acknowledgment of 'unimagined speed'—the actual pace of AI progress consistently and dramatically exceeds even optimistic practitioner forecasts, creating a persistent condition of imaginative lag.

## Key Concepts
- **AI futures spectrum**: The range from dystopian to utopian AI imaginaries held within a single practitioner community
- **Unimagined speed**: The practitioner-reported phenomenon that AI progress outpaces even optimistic forecasting
- **Practitioner imaginaries**: How AI builders and researchers envision the futures their own work is creating

## Main Arguments
1. AI practitioners hold diverse, often contradictory imaginaries about the futures their work will produce
2. Unimagined speed creates a condition where AI governance and ethics frameworks are perpetually behind the technology
3. Cross-national comparison (Finland/Singapore) reveals context-specific imaginary variations within practitioner communities
