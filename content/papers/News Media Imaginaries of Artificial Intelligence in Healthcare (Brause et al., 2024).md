# News Media Imaginaries of Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare (Brause et al., 2024)

## Summary
Qualitative comparative analysis of how AI in healthcare is framed by news media across China, Germany, and the United States using the lens of sociotechnical imaginaries. Finds distinct national imaginary patterns—China emphasizes state-directed efficiency, Germany foregrounds ethics and precaution, the US highlights innovation and market opportunity—with healthcare emerging as a globally contested priority sector for AI governance and public narrative.

## Key Concepts
- **News media imaginaries**: The sociotechnical visions of AI futures circulated through journalistic discourse
- **National imaginary variation**: How geopolitical and cultural contexts shape distinct AI narratives even in the same domain
- **AI in healthcare**: Medical applications as a high-stakes arena where imaginaries interact with clinical reality

## Main Arguments
1. Sociotechnical imaginaries of AI in healthcare are nationally distinct, not globally monolithic
2. Media framing constructs public expectations and policy pressure before clinical evidence accumulates
3. Healthcare AI is contested terrain where optimism, ethics, and governance tensions are most visible
