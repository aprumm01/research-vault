## Summary
Aranda et al. propose a mixed-methods approach combining Critical Discourse Analysis (CDA) with Structural Topic Modeling (STM) to analyze large corpora of management texts, arguing that neither method alone captures the complexity of organizational discourse. The integration enables researchers to identify macro-level topic patterns through STM while using CDA to unpack the ideological dimensions of dominant discourses. The paper contributes methodologically to management research and discourse analysis.

## Key Concepts
- Critical Discourse Analysis (CDA)
- Structural Topic Modeling (STM)
- Mixed methods research
- Organizational discourse
- Big data analysis

## Theoretical Framework
Critical discourse theory (Fairclough) and computational text analysis are integrated to show how combining interpretive and quantitative methods enriches organizational discourse research.

## Methods
Mixed-methods; combines Structural Topic Modeling on large text datasets with in-depth Critical Discourse Analysis; applied to management discourse data.

## Main Arguments
- CDA and STM have complementary strengths: CDA provides interpretive depth; STM provides pattern coverage across large corpora
- Combining both methods overcomes the limitations of each approach applied alone
- The integrated approach enables detection of ideological patterns in management discourse at scale
- Mixed-method discourse analysis is well-suited for studying how AI and technology are discussed in organizational contexts
