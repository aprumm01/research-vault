## Summary
This paper provides a broad overview of AI-driven learning approaches emerging in the current era of advanced AI, examining how machine learning, large language models, and adaptive systems are transforming educational delivery, personalization, and assessment. The paper evaluates the promise and risks of AI-driven learning across formal education, corporate training, and self-directed learning contexts. It argues for learning design principles that harness AI's personalization and feedback capabilities while maintaining the social, motivational, and identity-formation dimensions of learning that AI cannot replicate.

## Key Concepts
- AI-driven learning systems (adaptive learning, LLM tutors)
- Personalized learning at scale
- AI assessment and feedback
- Social and motivational dimensions of learning
- Learning design principles for AI era

## Theoretical Framework
Educational technology; AI in education; learning sciences; draws on adaptive learning research, personalization theory, and learning design frameworks.

## Methods
Conceptual review and synthesis of AI-driven learning research across contexts; evaluation of emerging approaches against learning theory principles; case analysis of deployed AI learning systems.

## Main Arguments
- AI-driven learning offers genuine improvements in personalization, feedback frequency, and learning efficiency for well-defined skills
- The social, motivational, and identity dimensions of learning are not well-served by AI-driven approaches and require human instructor presence
- Learning design for AI-augmented education must explicitly preserve the social and identity-formation dimensions that AI cannot provide
- Equity implications of AI-driven learning are significant: the same tools that personalize learning for some may create new digital divides for others

## Limitations & Critiques
The review spans very heterogeneous AI approaches and educational contexts; evidence quality for many AI-driven learning systems is limited; the field is moving faster than systematic evidence can accumulate.
