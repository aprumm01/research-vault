# Hybrid Intelligence


## Summary
Hybrid Intelligence is a thematic node capturing the vision and reality of systems that combine human and artificial intelligence in ways that are mutually complementary -- where humans and AI each contribute their distinctive strengths, and the combined system outperforms either alone. In this research vault, the node connects to debates about how AI should be positioned relative to human expertise in design, UX, and knowledge work: not as a replacement but as a complementary system that handles what humans find difficult (speed, scale, pattern-matching) while humans handle what AI cannot (judgment, ethics, contextual understanding, creativity). The node is relevant to both theoretical frameworks for human-AI partnership and practical questions about workflow design.

## Key Concepts
- Complementarity: human and AI strengths as distinct and mutually reinforcing
- Human-in-the-loop systems: maintaining human oversight and decision authority
- Division of cognitive labor between human and AI
- Centaur model: human intelligence directing AI execution
- Mutual adaptation: how humans and AI systems learn to work together over time
- Hybrid intelligence as a design aspiration vs. a practical achievement

## Theoretical Framework
HCI and AI research (Kamar on hybrid systems); cognitive systems engineering; sociotechnical systems theory; situated in this vault between AI and Future of Work, Human-AI Creative Collaboration, and GenAI in UX and Design Practice communities; draws on both experimental and qualitative research on AI-augmented professional practice.
