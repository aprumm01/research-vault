# Reflecting on the Integration of Generative AI in Design Education (Voces y Silencios, 2024)


## Abstract
This testimonial article reflects on integrating Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) into design education through three distinct experiences: personal explorations as a designer and educator, collaborative faculty learning in design technology, and integration within an undergraduate digital design course. This paper aims to contribute to discussions on how GenAI tools can support creative and educational practices. It employs a phenomenological approach to document these experiences, assessing the impact of AI on creativity, pedagogical practices, and learning outcomes. ## Summary
This paper from a Latin American education journal reflects critically on the integration of generative AI into design education from a sociocultural perspective, examining how AI tools interact with local educational contexts, linguistic diversity, and power dynamics. The author argues that uncritical adoption of Northern-developed AI tools in Latin American design education risks perpetuating colonial patterns in design knowledge and erasing culturally situated design practices. The paper calls for critical pedagogy frameworks that situate AI integration within local educational and cultural contexts.

## Key Concepts
- Critical pedagogy and AI in design education
- Decolonial design perspectives
- Generative AI and linguistic/cultural bias
- Local design knowledge and AI tools
- North-South dynamics in design education technology adoption

## Theoretical Framework
Critical pedagogy (Freire); decolonial design studies; sociocultural learning theory; draws on Latin American educational research traditions to situate AI adoption critically.

## Methods
Critical reflective essay with case analysis from Latin American design education contexts; draws on existing literature and author's pedagogical experience.

## Main Arguments
- Generative AI tools encode cultural and aesthetic biases that reflect the contexts in which they were developed and trained
- Uncritical AI adoption in design education can undermine locally situated design practices and diverse aesthetic traditions
- Critical AI literacy in design education requires students to analyze whose values and aesthetics AI tools privilege
- Design educators have a responsibility to contextualize AI tools within critical frameworks, not just teach their use

## Limitations & Critiques
Primarily critical/theoretical; limited empirical evidence from specific courses or student populations; the decolonial framing, while important, may be difficult to operationalize in resource-constrained design education settings.
