# How Hands-on Experience with Generative AI Shapes Design Students’ Perspectives on AI s Future Impact

## Summary
LNAI 15821 Helmut Degen Stavroula Ntoa (Eds ) Artificial Intelligence in HCI 6th International Conference, AI-HCI 2025 Held as Part of the 27th HCI International Conference, HCII 2025 Gothenburg, Sweden, June 22–27, 2025 Proceedings, Part III 123 Lecture Notes in Computer Science Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence Founding Editor Jörg Siekmann Series Editors.

## Key Concepts
- **Hands-on experience** with generative AI as factor shaping student perspectives
- Design students' evolving views on AI's future impact through direct engagement
- Experiential learning about AI in HCI and design education contexts
- Student perspectives on AI capabilities, limitations, and future implications
- Role of practical experience in forming informed views about AI technology
- Integration of generative AI tools into design education

## Theoretical Framework
- **Experiential learning theory**: Learning through direct engagement with technology
- HCI education frameworks for technology literacy
- Perspective transformation through hands-on practice
- Design pedagogy incorporating emerging technologies

## Methods
- Study examining design students' perspectives before and after hands-on AI experience
- Analysis of perspective shifts regarding AI's future impact
- Educational context: AI-HCI 2025 conference proceedings
- Qualitative and/or quantitative assessment of student views

## Main Arguments
- Hands-on experience with generative AI significantly shapes design students' perspectives on AI's future impact
- Direct engagement with AI tools provides more informed and nuanced understanding than theoretical instruction alone
- Experiential learning reveals both opportunities and limitations of generative AI in design contexts
- Student perspectives evolve through practical interaction with AI systems
- Design education benefits from integrating hands-on AI experiences
- Practical experience essential for preparing students for AI-augmented design futures

## Limitations & Critiques
- Study context (design students) may not generalize to other populations
- Timeframe of hands-on experience may affect perspective development
- Need to examine long-term perspective stability beyond immediate post-experience
- Potential selection bias if students choosing to work with AI differ from general population
- Conference proceedings format may limit depth of methodological detail
