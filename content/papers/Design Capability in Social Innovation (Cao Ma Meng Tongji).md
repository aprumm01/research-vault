# Design Capability in Social Innovation (Cao Ma Meng Tongji)

## Summary
Cao, Ma, and Meng from Tongji University synthesize the concept of design capability in social innovation, identifying the professional skills and design attitudes that enable designers to contribute effectively to social innovation projects. The paper develops a framework integrating technical design skills with social awareness, collaborative capacity, and systemic thinking — arguing that social innovation requires an expanded conception of design capability beyond artifact creation. The Tongji perspective draws on China's significant tradition of design-for-social-good research.

## Key Concepts
- Design capability in social innovation
- Professional skills for socially-oriented design
- Design attitude and social awareness
- Systemic and collaborative design competencies

## Theoretical Framework
Design capability theory combined with social innovation scholarship; synthesizes what competencies designers need to work effectively in social innovation contexts versus commercial design contexts.

## Methods
Synthesis paper developing a design capability framework for social innovation; literature review and framework construction; Tongji University, published in design research journal.

## Main Arguments
- Social innovation requires an expanded design capability beyond technical artifact design
- Design attitude — openness, empathy, systems thinking — is as important as design skill in social contexts
- A synthesis framework for design capability in social innovation provides guidance for design education reform
- Tongji's context of design-for-social-good provides empirical grounding for the framework
