## Summary
Petrosyan examines how AI development is impacting computer graphic design as a profession, analyzing both the productive capabilities AI enables (automation, new creative possibilities, personalization) and the challenges it creates (authenticity concerns, skill displacement, professional identity questions). The essay considers possible future visions for the graphic design industry as AI becomes more capable and accessible.

## Key Concepts
- AI and graphic design
- Task automation in design
- Creative identity
- Design profession futures
- AI-generated imagery

## Theoretical Framework
Professional and practice theory frame the analysis of how AI tools are changing the competency landscape for graphic designers and what skills will remain distinctively human.

## Methods
Essay; conceptual analysis and literature review; examination of AI capabilities and their implications for graphic design practice.

## Main Arguments
- AI automates repetitive design tasks and enables faster realization of creative concepts
- Graphic designers face challenges to professional identity as AI tools democratize visual production
- The distinctively human contribution to graphic design shifts toward conceptual direction, cultural sensitivity, and client communication
- The future of graphic design involves human-AI collaboration rather than replacement, but requires skill evolution
