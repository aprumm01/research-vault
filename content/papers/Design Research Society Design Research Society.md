## Summary
This paper, presented at the Design Research Society, examines how generative AI is shaping the concept of distributed creativity in design contexts. Building on theories of distributed cognition and collective creativity, the paper investigates how GenAI tools extend the creative system beyond individual designers to include AI as a participant in distributed creative processes. The work contributes a distributed creativity framework for understanding GenAI's role in collective and collaborative design work.

## Key Concepts
- Distributed creativity in design
- GenAI as participant in distributed creative systems
- Collective and collaborative design with AI
- Distributed cognition applied to AI-assisted design

## Theoretical Framework
Distributed cognition (Hutchins) and distributed creativity frameworks; examines how GenAI extends or modifies the socio-cognitive systems through which design creativity is distributed across people, tools, and artifacts.

## Methods
Theoretical and empirical paper; DRS conference presentation; examines distributed creativity through analysis of GenAI's role in design work contexts.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI changes the configuration of distributed creative systems in design
- AI tools are not merely instruments but participants in distributed creative processes
- Distributed creativity theory provides a richer account of GenAI's design role than individual tool-use models
- Understanding GenAI as distributed creativity participant has implications for design practice and team composition
