# Steering Blind Algorithms: Exploring the Impact of Generative AI on the Role of Designers


## Summary

## Summary
Jasmin el Kordy at Aalto University examines how generative AI is reshaping the role of designers, framing AI systems as "blind algorithms" that require human guidance and contextual steering. The extended abstract argues that designers are becoming algorithmic directors—navigating, curating, and directing AI outputs rather than directly creating artifacts. This shift raises questions about what design expertise means when production is delegated to AI systems.

## Key Concepts
- Generative AI and design roles
- Algorithmic design
- Designer as director
- Creative expertise
- Human-AI design collaboration

## Theoretical Framework
Design theory and human-computer interaction frame the analysis of how generative AI changes the fundamental nature of design work and the competencies required of designers.

## Methods
Conceptual analysis; extended abstract likely drawing on literature review and theoretical argument; presented at CHI EA 2025.

## Main Arguments
- Generative AI shifts designers from direct creators to directors and curators of algorithmic outputs
- Designers must develop new competencies for steering, evaluating, and contextualizing AI-generated content
- The "blind" nature of algorithms—lacking contextual understanding—makes human judgment essential for design quality
- Design education must evolve to develop algorithmic direction skills alongside traditional design craft
