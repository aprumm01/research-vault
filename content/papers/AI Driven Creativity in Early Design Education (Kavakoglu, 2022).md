# AI Driven Creativity in Early Design Education (Kavakoglu, 2022)

## Summary
Examines AI-driven creativity in early-stage design education within an Industry 5.0 context, proposing a pedagogical approach that integrates AI tools into creative design workflows at the foundational level. Conducted at Istanbul Technical University, the study argues that AI can amplify generative thinking in design studios rather than replace it, and introduces a framework for AI-augmented critique and iteration in early design courses.

## Key Concepts
- **Industry 5.0**: The next industrial paradigm emphasizing human-machine collaboration and societal value over pure automation
- **AI-driven creativity**: Using AI as a generative partner in ideation and early design exploration
- **Early design education**: Foundational-year studios and curricula where creative habits of mind are formed

## Main Arguments
1. AI tools in early design education can scaffold generative thinking rather than supplant it
2. A structured pedagogy for AI-augmented design requires clear boundaries between AI output and student authorship
3. Industry 5.0 framing offers a more human-centered rationale for AI in design than productivity-focused Industry 4.0
