# Optimizing employee roles in the era of generative AI a multi-criteria decision-making analysis of co-creation dynamics

## Summary
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has the ability to transform the way organizations operate, but imposes a unique challenge for them Adoption of AI might reshape the human resource management patterns which calls for optimization of roles in workplaces for dealing with technology Critical decision points and alternatives are identified through a systematic review of literature and Analytical Hierarchy Approach (AHP) is applied to analyze their priority weights.

## Key Concepts
- Multi-criteria decision-making in AI adoption contexts
- Human resource management transformation through AI
- Co-creation dynamics between humans and AI systems
- Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) methodology
- Role optimization and workforce reconfiguration
- Human-AI collaboration models
- Organizational adaptation to generative AI technologies
- Decision alternatives in AI implementation
- Priority weighting of organizational factors

## Theoretical Framework
- Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP) - structured decision-making framework
- Co-creation theory applied to human-AI collaboration
- Human resource management transformation frameworks
- Sociotechnical systems theory for AI integration

## Methods
- Systematic literature review
- Analytical Hierarchy Process (AHP)
- Multi-criteria decision analysis
- Priority weight calculation for decision alternatives

## Main Arguments
- AI adoption requires fundamental rethinking of employee roles and responsibilities
- Organizations face critical decision points regarding how to optimize workforce roles alongside AI
- Co-creation between humans and AI systems offers superior outcomes to replacement models
- AHP provides structured methodology for navigating complex AI implementation decisions
- Human resource management patterns must evolve to accommodate AI capabilities
- Priority weighting reveals which role optimization strategies are most viable
- Successful AI integration depends on systematic analysis of decision alternatives
- Organizations must balance efficiency gains with human workforce capabilities

## Limitations & Critiques
- AHP methodology relies on expert judgment which may introduce bias
- Limited empirical validation of proposed role optimization strategies
- Rapidly evolving AI capabilities may outpace analytical frameworks
- Cultural and organizational context variations not fully addressed
- May oversimplify complex human-AI interaction dynamics
- Cross-industry generalizability of findings uncertain
- Long-term impacts of role optimization strategies remain unknown
- Does not fully address employee concerns and resistance to change
