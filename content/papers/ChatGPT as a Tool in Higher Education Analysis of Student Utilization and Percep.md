# ChatGPT as a Tool in Higher Education: Analysis of Student Utilization and Perception

**Institution:** PAPERS OF SILESIAN UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY 2025 ORGANIZATIO

## Key Finding
SCIENTIFIC PAPERS OF SILESIAN UNIVERSITY OF TECHNOLOGY 2025 ORGANIZATION AND MANAGEMENT SERIES NO. 224 http://dx.doi.org/10.29119/1641-3466.2025.224.4 http://managementpapers.polsl.pl/

## Summary
Surveys 260 management students at the University of Gdańsk to explore how they use ChatGPT for academic purposes, finding widespread adoption for information retrieval, writing assistance, and idea refinement. Students primarily value efficiency but raise concerns about content reliability and potential negative effects on creativity and critical thinking.

## Key Concepts
- Generative AI in higher education
- Student perception of AI tools
- Academic integrity and over-reliance
- AI literacy
- Personalized learning

## Theoretical Framework
Theoretical-empirical approach drawing on Kasneci et al. (2023) and Gimpel et al. (2023) on AI in education; frames ChatGPT as a dual-edged transformative tool that simultaneously enhances and risks undermining learning outcomes.

## Methods
Mixed method: literature review combined with a survey of 260 students at one faculty of management; descriptive statistics; no longitudinal component.

## Main Arguments
- ChatGPT is widely used for quick information retrieval, writing assistance, and idea refinement
- Students primarily value efficiency and quality improvement potential
- Key concerns: reliability of output, risk of over-reliance, possible suppression of critical thinking and creativity
- Institutions need structured programs supporting responsible, ethical AI use

## Limitations & Critiques
Limited to one faculty at one Polish university, restricting generalizability; no long-term data on learning outcomes; the rapidly evolving nature of generative AI makes findings time-sensitive.
