# User Experience and AI: Assessing the Qualities of AI-Infused Systems (Springer)

## Summary
Spallazzo, Sciannamè, and Ceconello present the outcomes of the Meet-AI research project at Politecnico di Milano, which developed a systematic framework for assessing the user experience of AI-infused products. The book argues that conventional UX assessment tools are inadequate for AI-infused systems because those systems are dynamic, adaptive, and opaque in ways that static products are not. The authors propose new UX dimensions specific to AI systems (trustworthiness, intelligence, meaningfulness, and conversational qualities) and present the AIXE (AI Experience) assessment method developed and validated through the project.

## Key Concepts
- UX assessment of AI-infused products
- AIXE (AI Experience) assessment framework
- AI-specific UX dimensions: trustworthiness, intelligence, meaningfulness, conversational qualities
- Gap between AI technological capability and user experience quality
- Human-centered design for AI systems

## Theoretical Framework
Human-centered design and UX research; draws on user-centered design principles, HCI research on conversational agents and intelligent systems, and the design research tradition at Politecnico di Milano; the Meet-AI project reframes AI UX assessment as a design research problem requiring new instruments rather than adaptations of existing tools.

## Methods
Design research and mixed methods; includes literature review of UX and AI-infused system research, qualitative user research, iterative framework development, and validation of the AIXE assessment method; conducted by a team of eight researchers across multiple project phases.

## Main Arguments
- Conventional UX assessment frameworks fail to capture the qualities that distinguish AI-infused systems from static products, particularly trustworthiness and adaptability
- New UX dimensions (trustworthiness, intelligence, meaningfulness, conversational qualities) are necessary and distinct for evaluating AI-infused systems
- The AIXE method provides researchers, designers, and companies with a validated instrument for evaluating and improving AI product UX
- The rapid pace of AI technology integration has consistently outpaced human-centered design considerations, creating a gap this research aims to close

## Limitations & Critiques
The AIXE framework was developed and validated within a specific research context (Politecnico di Milano, European product design); its generalizability to diverse product categories and cultural contexts requires further study. As an emergent framework for a rapidly evolving technological landscape, the dimensions may need ongoing revision as AI capabilities change.
