# Exploring the Impact of AI Language Model ChatGPT on User Experience

## Summary

This study investigates how ChatGPT can be integrated into the Design Thinking process by UX designers. Through semi-structured interviews with 5 UX professionals, researchers explored perceptions and practices of using AI in UX design workflows. Findings indicate ChatGPT is most beneficial in the Define and Ideate stages, helping with user research synthesis, persona creation, and generating design alternatives.

## Key Concepts

- **Design Thinking Process**: Five-stage model (Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test) as framework for AI integration
- **AI-Assisted Design**: Using ChatGPT as a collaborative tool rather than replacement for designers
- **UX Research Synthesis**: AI helping consolidate user research findings into actionable insights
- **Prompt Engineering**: The skill of crafting effective prompts to get useful AI outputs

## Theoretical Framework

The study grounds itself in the Design Thinking methodology (Stanford d.school model) as the primary framework for examining AI integration points. It positions AI as a tool within human-centered design processes, exploring how technology can augment rather than replace designer expertise.

## Methods

- **Design**: Qualitative exploratory study
- **Participants**: 5 UX designers with varying experience levels (1-10 years)
- **Data Collection**: Semi-structured interviews via video conferencing
- **Analysis**: Thematic analysis of interview transcripts
- **Focus**: How designers currently use or envision using ChatGPT in their practice

## Main Arguments

1. **Define Stage Benefits**: ChatGPT excels at synthesizing user research data, creating personas, and developing user journey maps from raw research inputs
2. **Ideate Stage Support**: AI accelerates brainstorming by generating numerous design alternatives and feature suggestions quickly
3. **Limitations in Empathize/Test**: Human skills remain essential for conducting user interviews and usability testing that require emotional intelligence
4. **Complementary Role**: AI functions best as a "thought partner" that augments rather than replaces designer expertise
5. **Quality Concerns**: Outputs require careful validation and refinement by human designers to ensure accuracy and relevance

## Limitations

- Small sample size (n=5) limits generalizability
- Self-reported data subject to recall and social desirability bias
- Rapidly evolving AI capabilities may quickly date findings
- Study conducted early in ChatGPT adoption (2023), so practices likely still developing
- No observation of actual design work with AI tools
- Geographic/cultural context not specified
