# A Formative Study to Explore the Design of Generative UI Tools to Support UX Practitioners and Beyond

## Summary

This is an earlier/preliminary version of the GenUI Study investigating how 37 UX-related professionals (UX designers, UX researchers, product managers, and software engineers) would adopt Generative UI tools in their work. The study uses a week-long diary methodology followed by interviews.

Note: This appears to be an earlier ArXiv version of "The GenUI Study" paper. See the more complete summary at [[The GenUI Study - Exploring the Design of Generative UI Tools to Support UX Practitioners and Beyond (Chen, Knearem & Li, 2024)]].

## Key Research Questions

- RQ1: How would users of various UX-related roles adopt GenUI in their work?
- RQ2: What are the promises and opportunities—specific aspects of their work where GenUI can provide support?
- RQ3: What are the gaps—challenges and areas for improvement—for GenUI to realize such support?
