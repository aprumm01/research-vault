# The Anatomy of Prototypes: Prototypes as Filters, Prototypes as Manifestations of Design Ideas

## Summary

This influential paper by Lim, Stolterman, and Tenenberg proposes a conceptual framework for understanding prototypes in HCI and design. Published in ACM TOCHI in 2008, it reconceptualizes prototyping from merely a tool for evaluation to a fundamental means of exploring and understanding design possibilities.

The authors argue that prototypes serve two critical functions: as **filters** that help designers focus on specific qualities while temporarily ignoring others, and as **manifestations** that externalize and communicate design ideas. This dual nature means prototypes are not just about testing designs but about discovering possibilities within a design space.

## Key Concepts

- **Fundamental Prototyping Principle**: "Finding the manifestation that in its simplest form filters the qualities the designers are interested in, without distorting the understanding of the whole"

- **Prototypes as Filters**: Prototypes allow designers to selectively attend to certain qualities while temporarily bracketing others, enabling focused exploration

- **Prototypes as Manifestations**: Prototypes externalize design ideas, making tacit knowledge explicit and enabling communication among stakeholders

- **Design Space**: The conceptual space of all possible design alternatives that prototypes help explore and traverse

## Methodology

Conceptual framework development supported by two case studies demonstrating the filtering and manifestation dimensions of prototypes.

## Key Findings

1. **Beyond Evaluation**: Prototypes are not just for evaluating designs but for learning about the design space, generating ideas, and communicating design rationale

2. **Fundamental Principle**: Effective prototyping finds the simplest manifestation that filters relevant qualities without distorting holistic understanding

3. **Design Space Exploration**: Prototypes are tools for traversing and understanding design possibilities, not just testing predetermined solutions

4. **Reflective Practice**: Prototypes stimulate reflection and enable designers to discover properties of problems through solution attempts

## Relevance to AI and Design Education

This framework has significant implications for AI-augmented design:

- **AI-Generated Prototypes**: When AI generates prototypes, does it preserve the filtering function that enables designer learning?
- **Loss of Exploration**: If AI produces "finished" outputs rather than explorable prototypes, designers may lose the iterative discovery process
- **Manifestation of Whose Ideas?**: AI-generated prototypes manifest AI capabilities, not necessarily designer intentions
