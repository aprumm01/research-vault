# Mapping Value Sensitive Design onto AI for Social Good Principles

## Summary

This theoretical paper maps Value Sensitive Design (VSD) methodology onto the emerging "AI for Social Good" (AI4SG) principles. The authors argue that VSD provides a rigorous, empirically grounded approach for operationalizing abstract AI ethics principles into concrete design practices. By systematically connecting VSD's tripartite methodology with AI4SG's normative guidelines, the paper offers a practical framework for designing AI systems that genuinely serve social good.

## Key Concepts

- **Value Sensitive Design (VSD)**: Design methodology that accounts for human values throughout the design process
- **AI for Social Good (AI4SG)**: Movement focused on developing AI applications that address social challenges
- **Tripartite methodology**: VSD's three-part approach - conceptual, empirical, and technical investigations
- **Value tensions**: Conflicts between values (e.g., privacy vs. public health) that must be navigated in design
- **Stakeholder analysis**: Identifying and engaging all parties affected by an AI system

## Theoretical Framework

Integration of:
1. **Value Sensitive Design (Friedman & Hendry)**: Systematic approach to embedding values in technology
2. **AI for Social Good principles**: Normative guidelines for beneficial AI development
3. **Design ethics**: Connecting abstract values to concrete design decisions

## Methods

- Conceptual/theoretical analysis
- Systematic mapping of VSD methodology to AI4SG principles
- Framework development through philosophical argumentation
- Case examples illustrating the mapping

## Main Arguments

1. **Operationalization need**: AI ethics principles are often too abstract; VSD provides operationalization pathway
2. **Tripartite mapping**: Each component of VSD (conceptual, empirical, technical) can address specific AI4SG concerns
3. **Stakeholder engagement**: VSD's emphasis on stakeholder analysis aligns with AI4SG's inclusion principles
4. **Value tensions in AI**: VSD provides tools for navigating inevitable value conflicts in AI design
5. **Iterative design**: Both VSD and AI4SG require ongoing reflection and adjustment throughout development
6. **Practical guidance**: The mapping provides concrete steps for designers and developers

## Limitations & Critiques

- Framework may be resource-intensive for smaller organizations
- Cultural specificity of "values" requires careful attention in global contexts
- May not address power asymmetries in whose values are prioritized
- Empirical validation of the proposed mapping is limited
- Rapidly evolving AI capabilities may outpace framework application
