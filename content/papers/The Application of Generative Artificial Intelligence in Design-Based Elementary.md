# The Application of Generative Artificial Intelligence in Design-Based Elementary Education

## Summary
This proceedings volume contains two papers on AI in design education: (1) "Creativity Catalyst" examining ChatGPT's role in creative problem-solving using TAM model and diary studies, and (2) a systematic literature review on AIGC's impact on critical thinking in design processes.

## Key Concepts
- Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) applied to ChatGPT adoption in creative problem-solving
- Perceived Ease of Use, Perceived Usefulness, Self-Efficacy, Collaborative Learning as acceptance factors
- AIGC (AI-Generated Content) transforming design workflows from sketch iteration to intelligent generation
- Critical thinking as core competency challenged by AI delegation of cognitive tasks
- Cognitive laziness, information overload, and bias spread as AIGC challenges
- Diary research method for capturing extended human-AI interaction patterns
- PRISMA systematic literature review methodology

## Theoretical Framework
[[frameworks/Technology Acceptance Model]] - TAM framework for analyzing ChatGPT adoption

## Methods
mixed methods (quantitative survey + qualitative diary study), systematic literature review

## Main Arguments
- Perceived Ease of Use and Perceived Usefulness significantly impact students' behavioral intention to use ChatGPT
- ChatGPT improves information integration efficiency, enhances empathy, and increases confidence in creative tasks
- Self-efficacy positively impacts perceived ease of use and behavioral intention
- Collaborative learning introduced as external TAM factor positively influences acceptance
- AIGC replaces traditional sketch evolution and prototype iteration, advancing intelligent design progression
- Transition from "designers creating with machine assistance" to "machine creation guided by designer evaluation"
- Critical thinking crucial for evaluating AIGC-generated content accuracy and providing refinement feedback
- AIGC enables early evaluation and feedback, improving iteration efficiency and reducing trial-and-error costs

## Limitations & Critiques
- ChatGPT shows inconsistent emotional expression during interactions
- Information accuracy insufficiencies require verification and critical evaluation
- Prompt design challenges create barriers for effective AI interaction
- TAM study based on 120 valid questionnaires (70.59% bachelor's degree students) - limited generalizability
- Diary study limited to 23 industrial design undergraduates over 3-week period
- Does not address long-term impacts on critical thinking skill degradation
- Systematic review limited to post-2020 publications - may miss foundational work
