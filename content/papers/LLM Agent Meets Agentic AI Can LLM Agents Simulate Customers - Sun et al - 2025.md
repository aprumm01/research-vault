## Main Arguments
- LLM agents can potentially simulate customer behaviors for evaluating shopping AI
- Agentic AI shopping assistants require robust evaluation methods
- Synthetic customers offer scalable alternative to expensive human testing
- Need to validate whether LLM agents accurately represent real customer behaviors
- Different customer types and scenarios affect simulation validity
- Survey data can assess gaps between LLM simulation and human behavior
- Cost and time benefits of synthetic evaluation must be balanced against validity
- LLM agents show promise but also significant limitations in customer simulation
- Context-specific factors influence effectiveness of agent-based evaluation

## Limitations & Critiques
- LLM agents may not capture full complexity of human customer behavior
- Validity concerns about generalizing from synthetic to real customers
- Survey methodology may not capture all relevant behavioral dimensions
- Temporal limitations - 2025 technology will continue evolving
- Potential biases in LLM training data affecting customer simulations
- Limited diversity in simulated customer personas
- Does not fully address ethical implications of synthetic customer use
- Cross-cultural customer behavior variations may not be well-represented
- Long-term shopping relationships and loyalty not adequately modeled
