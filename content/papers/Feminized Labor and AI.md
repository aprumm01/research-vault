# Feminized Labor and AI


## Summary
Feminized Labor and AI is a thematic node examining how AI-driven automation and workforce transformation intersect with gender -- particularly how work that has historically been coded as feminine (care work, relational labor, communication, creativity, coordination) is being reshaped, devalued, or displaced by AI systems. In this research vault, the node connects AI's impact on creative and knowledge work to broader feminist political economy critiques: as AI automates tasks that were once seen as uniquely human contributions, questions arise about whose labor is made visible or invisible, whose creative work is appropriated as training data, and how AI reshapes power relations in design and UX professions that have gendered histories.

## Key Concepts
- Feminized labor: work categories historically associated with women and undervalued
- AI appropriation of creative labor: training data, style transfer, and compensation
- Reproductive labor and care work in AI-mediated workflows
- Invisible labor in AI systems: human annotation, content moderation, prompt engineering
- Gender and professional identity in design and UX fields
- Critical AI studies feminist perspectives (Crawford, Hicks, Noble)

## Theoretical Framework
Feminist political economy; critical labor studies; feminist STS (science and technology studies); postcolonial critiques of AI labor chains; connected in this vault to AI and Future of Work, Responsible AI and Ethics, and creative labor debates in GenAI in UX and Design Practice.
