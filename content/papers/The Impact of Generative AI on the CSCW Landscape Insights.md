# The Impact of Generative AI on the CSCW Landscape: Insights

## Summary
Freeman et al. (2024) offer a multi-institutional perspective on generative AI's transformative impact on the CSCW research landscape, drawing on viewpoints from academia (Clemson, Northeastern, Michigan) and industry (IBM Research, Microsoft Research, Google DeepMind). The paper argues that discussing GenAI's effects on specific CSCW research directions is insufficient — the field must engage in deeper cross-sector dialogue about GenAI's systemic effects on HCI education, industry dynamics, and research funding priorities. The authors call for deliberate reflection on what CSCW research should look like in a GenAI-saturated world.

## Key Concepts
- CSCW research agenda transformation
- Cross-sector academic-industry dialogue
- GenAI's impact on HCI education and funding
- Systemic effects vs. topic-specific impacts

## Theoretical Framework
Sociotechnical systems lens applied at the field level — examines how GenAI reshapes research infrastructure, not just individual research objects.

## Methods
Panel/position paper (4 pages); synthesizes perspectives from 6 authors spanning 5 institutions across academia and industry. Published at CSCW 2024.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI's impact on CSCW is multidimensional — affects the field's structure, not just its research topics
- HCI education must adapt to train students who can work alongside AI systems
- Industry-academic collaboration is essential for understanding GenAI's real-world deployment effects
- Research funding priorities need to shift to address emerging GenAI challenges

## Limitations & Critiques
Short position paper format limits empirical grounding; represents senior researchers' perspectives which may not reflect junior scholar or student experiences.
