# Co-Designing with Transformers: GenAI in Design Education (Sandhaus et al. Cornell)

**Year:** 2025
**Venue:** CHI 2025
**Institution:** Cornell Tech

## Key Finding
GenAI plays complex multifaceted role in interactive system design education

## Connections

## Summary
Sandhaus et al. investigate the complex roles GenAI plays in interactive system design education at Cornell, examining how design students engage with transformer-based AI tools in co-design activities. The paper finds that GenAI occupies multiple, sometimes contradictory roles — as creative collaborator, constraint, critic, and distraction — and that students must develop metacognitive strategies to productively work with AI in design contexts. The study contributes a nuanced educational account of GenAI integration in design studio pedagogy.

## Key Concepts
- Co-designing with transformer-based AI tools
- Roles of GenAI in interactive system design education
- Metacognitive strategies for AI-assisted design learning
- Design studio pedagogy and AI integration

## Theoretical Framework
Design education and human-AI interaction frameworks; examines how students navigate multiple GenAI roles in design learning contexts using qualitative and participatory methods.

## Methods
Empirical study at Cornell Tech; design studio context; qualitative analysis of student experiences co-designing with GenAI tools; likely observations, interviews, or reflective journals; 16-page paper.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI plays multiple complex roles in design education — not just tool but collaborator, constraint, and critic
- Students need metacognitive strategies to work productively with GenAI in design contexts
- Studio pedagogy must evolve to address the new dynamics introduced by AI co-design partners
- GenAI integration in design education requires deliberate scaffolding, not just tool access
