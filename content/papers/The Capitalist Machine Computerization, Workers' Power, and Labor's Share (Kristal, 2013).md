# The Capitalist Machine: Computerization, Workers' Power, and Labor's Share (Kristal, 2013)

## Summary
Analyzes the 6% decline in labor's share of national income across 43 U.S. private industries from 1969 to 2007, arguing that the primary driver was the erosion of workers' positional power through class-biased technological change (computerization) that favored employers over most employees. Error correction models with fixed-effect estimators test the independent effects of computerization and power indicators (unionization, capital concentration, unemployment, import penetration).
