# Using Generative AI Tools in Collaborative UX Design Courses

# Study Partner Note: Using Generative AI Tools in Collaborative UX Design Courses

## Summary
This paper examines how generative AI tools (like ChatGPT) can enhance collaborative learning in User Experience (UX) design courses by facilitating prompt-based conversations between instructors and students during co-creative design tasks. The research demonstrates that GenAI significantly supports ideation, persona development, and mockup testing phases while identifying barriers in web development implementation.

## Key Concepts
- **Generative AI (GenAI)** – AI systems capable of creating content through human-machine dialogue and interaction
- **Co-creation/Co-creative tasks** – collaborative processes where humans and AI jointly produce design artifacts
- **Prompt-based dialogue** – structured communication using prompts to exchange information and guide AI outputs
- **UX design stages** – distinct phases in user experience design (ideation, persona definition, testing, etc.)
- **Human-machine collaboration** – socio-technical perspective positioning AI as an actor in collaborative design processes

## Theoretical Framework
The paper adopts a socio-technical view of GenAI, framing AI as an actor participating in co-creation patterns alongside humans. The research emphasizes prompt-based collective knowledge building and argumentative dialogue between humans and AI systems to support emergent joint knowledge construction.

## Methods
Two cohorts of UX design students participated in the study: the first engaged in GenAI-assisted persona profiling and ideation; the second collaborated on mockup testing and website design evaluation. The research documented how students and instructors used generative tools across different UX design stages.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI tools effectively facilitate co-creative ideation and persona development by enabling prompt-based exchanges between instructors and students
- Prompt mechanisms are crucial for sharing and collaboratively constructing knowledge in UX design contexts
- GenAI can enhance teamwork by acting as "creative and intelligence conservation agents" that synthesize information
- Certain barriers exist when applying GenAI to web development tasks, though the technology enables new instructional scenarios overall

## Limitations & Critiques
The paper focuses on ideation and testing phases but identifies implementation gaps in web development tasks, suggesting the findings may not generalize across all UX design stages. The research lacks detailed analysis of what specific barriers prevented effective GenAI use in development work or how to overcome them.
