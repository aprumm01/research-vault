# User Experience and AI: Assessing the Qualities of AI-Infused Systems (Springer)

## Summary
This second Springer Brief on AI and UX presents a framework for assessing the qualities of AI-infused systems from a user experience perspective, examining how AI capabilities change the nature of the user-product relationship and what new quality dimensions this creates. The book extends traditional product quality models (ISO 9241, usability heuristics) to account for AI's adaptive, learning, and predictive behaviors. It provides UX designers and evaluators with a comprehensive quality model for AI-infused products that balances AI capability assessment with human-centered quality criteria.

## Key Concepts
- AI-infused system quality assessment
- Extended quality models for AI products (ISO 9241 extensions)
- Adaptive system UX quality
- AI product evaluation criteria
- Human-AI interaction quality dimensions

## Theoretical Framework
ISO 9241 product quality framework; user experience theory; AI system quality research; draws on software quality engineering and UX research to extend quality models for AI contexts.

## Methods
Quality model development; case studies of AI-infused product evaluation; literature synthesis of AI quality dimensions; expert review.

## Main Arguments
- AI-infused systems require extended quality models that account for adaptivity, uncertainty, and emergent behavior beyond traditional usability
- Quality dimensions specific to AI include: appropriate confidence calibration, graceful failure handling, explanation quality, and learning transparency
- UX practitioners need structured quality frameworks to avoid ad hoc AI product evaluation
- The quality model enables cross-product comparison and serves as a communication tool between UX teams and AI development teams

## Limitations & Critiques
Quality models are context-dependent; the proposed extensions may not cover all AI product types equally well; the ISO 9241 framework as a base is itself contested in contemporary UX practice; validation studies across diverse AI product categories are needed.
