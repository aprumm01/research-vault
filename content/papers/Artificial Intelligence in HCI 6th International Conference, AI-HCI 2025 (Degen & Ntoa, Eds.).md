# Artificial Intelligence in HCI: 6th International Conference, AI-HCI 2025 (Degen & Ntoa, Eds.)


## Summary

## Summary
This is the proceedings volume for the 6th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in HCI (AI-HCI 2025), held as part of the 27th HCI International Conference in Gothenburg, Sweden (June 22–27, 2025). Edited by Helmut Degen and Stavroula Ntoa, the volume (Lecture Notes in Artificial Intelligence 15821) collects peer-reviewed papers on AI applications in human-computer interaction, including AI-assisted design, AI in education, explainable AI, and human-AI teaming.

## Key Concepts
- Artificial intelligence in HCI (conference proceedings)
- AI-HCI 2025 (6th International Conference)
- Human-AI interaction and teaming
- AI in design and education contexts

## Theoretical Framework
Conference proceedings spanning multiple theoretical frameworks; covers HCI, AI, design, and education research at the intersection of AI and human-computer interaction.

## Methods
Peer-reviewed conference proceedings; LNAI 15821; Springer publication; 449 pages covering AI-HCI research from the 2025 HCII conference.

## Main Arguments
- AI-HCI 2025 represents the current state of research on AI in human-computer interaction
- The volume covers a broad range of AI applications in HCI including design tools, educational systems, and human-AI teaming
- Key research themes include explainability, trust, and the design of effective human-AI collaboration
