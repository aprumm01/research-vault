# Tracing the Techno-Supremacy Doctrine - A Critical Discourse Analysis of the AI Executive Elite

## Summary
T RACING THE T ECHNO -S UPREMACY D OCTRINE : A C RITICAL D ISCOURSE A NALYSIS OF THE AI E XECUTIVE E LITE arXiv:2509 CY] 22 Sep 2025 A P REPRINT Héctor Pérez-Urbina University of Cambridge Cambridge, UK hmp56@cam uk A BSTRACT This paper critically analyzes the discourse of the ‘AI executive elite,’ a group of highly influential individuals shaping the way AI is funded, developed, and deployed worldwide.

## Key Concepts
- Techno-Supremacy Doctrine (TSD) as analytical framework
- Tech Goggles, Solutionism, Techno-Chauvinism, Cult of Technology
- AI executive elite discourse
- Benign Techno-Optimism (BTO) distinction
- Value-Neutrality Thesis deflection strategy
- Post-ChatGPT polarization
- TSD cycle of mutual reinforcement

## Theoretical Framework
Critical Discourse Analysis (Fairclough) examining how belief systems about technology's inherent superiority are produced, reproduced, and challenged through discourse of powerful AI industry leaders. Integrates quantitative heuristics with qualitative investigation.

## Methods
Two-phase Critical Discourse Analysis of 14 texts by AI executive elite (2017-2025). Phase 1: Broad corpus-wide coding using TSD and AI-concern response schemes, followed by quantitative metrics (TSD Adherence, TSD Balance) to identify patterns. Phase 2: Deep qualitative analysis of selected texts examining linguistic features, vocabulary, grammar. Novel quantitative heuristics developed to systematically compare texts and ground selection.

## Main Arguments
- TSD is comprehensive framework synthesizing four previously distinct belief systems into self-reinforcing cycle
- AI executive elite is not monolithic bloc but exhibits spectrum of stances from pernicious TSD to Benign Techno-Optimism
- Marked polarization and increase in pro-TSD discourse following ChatGPT launch (post-2022)
- Dominant pro-TSD narrative combines utopian promises with claims of inevitable progress
- Common tactic: acknowledging risks only as strategic preamble to proposing further technological solutions
- TSD manifests perniciously in education by reducing learning to quantifiable output amenable to optimization
- Fostering critical awareness of discursive patterns essential for practitioners, policymakers, and public
- TSD adoption by powerful individuals undermines responsible innovation by prioritizing technology over social programs, policy, ethics

## Limitations & Critiques
- Limited to discourse analysis without examining actual impacts of rhetoric on policy or technology development
- Selection of 14 texts may not capture full range of AI elite discourse
- Weighted coding scheme involves subjective decisions despite theoretical grounding
- Focus on English-language texts from primarily Western AI leaders
- Does not analyze audience reception or cognitive effects of TSD discourse
- Metrics provide heuristics but cannot fully capture nuanced ideological positions
- Limited temporal scope (2017-2025) may miss earlier foundational discourse
