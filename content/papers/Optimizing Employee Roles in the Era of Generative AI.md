# Optimizing Employee Roles in the Era of Generative AI

## Summary
This paper examines how generative AI is reshaping employee roles in organizations through a multi-criteria decision-making (MCDM) analysis of co-creation dynamics, proposing an optimization framework for role allocation in AI-integrated work environments. Published in Cogent Social Sciences, the paper addresses the managerial challenge of deciding which tasks and roles should be assigned to humans, AI, or human-AI co-creation teams, using quantitative MCDM methods to identify optimal configurations.

## Key Concepts
- Employee role optimization under GenAI
- Multi-criteria decision-making (MCDM) for role allocation
- Human-AI co-creation dynamics
- Organizational role reconfiguration with AI

## Theoretical Framework
Operations research and organizational management frameworks; MCDM methodology applied to the problem of optimizing human and AI role configurations in work organizations.

## Methods
Multi-criteria decision-making (MCDM) quantitative analysis; models different co-creation configurations (human-only, AI-only, hybrid) against multiple criteria; published in Cogent Social Sciences.

## Main Arguments
- Employee role optimization in AI-integrated organizations requires systematic multi-criteria analysis
- Different task types warrant different human-AI configurations — no universal optimal solution
- Co-creation dynamics between humans and AI are context-dependent and must be empirically assessed
- MCDM provides a rigorous framework for managers to make principled role allocation decisions
