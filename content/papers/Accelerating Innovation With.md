## Summary
This paper examines how generative AI tools are accelerating innovation workflows through AI-augmented digital prototyping and innovation methods, arguing that GenAI represents a qualitative shift in innovation capacity by compressing the ideation-to-prototype cycle. The authors present case studies of organizations using GenAI in early-stage innovation, documenting how AI-augmented digital prototyping enables rapid exploration of a wider solution space than traditional methods allow. The paper argues for a new "AI-augmented innovation" methodology that systematically integrates GenAI into established innovation frameworks.

## Key Concepts
- AI-augmented digital prototyping
- Innovation acceleration with GenAI
- Rapid solution space exploration
- AI-augmented innovation methodology
- Integration of GenAI into innovation frameworks

## Theoretical Framework
Innovation management; design thinking; human-AI collaboration; draws on innovation process research and emerging GenAI in design literature to frame the acceleration dynamic.

## Methods
Case studies of organizations using GenAI tools in digital prototyping and innovation workflows; qualitative analysis of workflow changes, speed improvements, and quality outcomes.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI compresses the ideation-to-prototype cycle in ways that enable wider solution space exploration without proportional time cost
- AI-augmented prototyping produces more diverse initial concepts than traditional brainstorming, though concept quality requires human curation
- Innovation teams need new workflows and competencies to benefit from GenAI acceleration rather than simply using it to do the same work faster
- The methodology requires integrating GenAI as a structural element of innovation process, not an ad hoc speed hack

## Limitations & Critiques
Case study evidence; GenAI innovation acceleration effects may be context-specific and depend on organizational readiness; the quality of AI-generated concepts is difficult to assess without long-term outcome tracking.
