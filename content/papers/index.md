# Research Papers

This folder contains summaries and analyses of research papers related to AI and Design Education.

## About Paper Notes

Each paper note includes:
- **Summary**: High-level overview of the paper's contribution
- **Key Concepts**: Main ideas and terminology
- **Theoretical Framework**: Underlying theories and perspectives
- **Methods**: Research methodology used
- **Main Arguments**: Core claims and findings
- **Limitations & Critiques**: Known issues and counterarguments
- **Connections**: Links to related topics, methods, and frameworks
