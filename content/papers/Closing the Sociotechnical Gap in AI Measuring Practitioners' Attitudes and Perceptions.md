# Closing the Sociotechnical Gap in AI: Measuring Practitioners' Attitudes and Perceptions

## Summary
Argues that AI practitioners—developers, data scientists, engineers—suffer from a "sociotechnical gap" caused by technical training that renders them "environment blind" to human and social implications of the systems they build. The article calls for the HCI community to develop standardized metrics for measuring practitioner attitudes and evaluating the effectiveness of interventions designed to shift those mindsets.
