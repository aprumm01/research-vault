# The Integration of AI in Design Thinking for Enhancing Student Creativity and Critical Thinking in Digital Media Learning

## Summary
Article history: The use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in education has advanced rapidly, creating new prospects for improving creativity and critical thinking, particularly in digital media learning This paper does a literature analysis to answer the research question: How beneficial is the employment of AI in design thinking-based activities in improving students' creativity and critical thinking in digital media education A total of 118 peerreviewed papers from the Scopus database (2020-2025) were initially discovered, with 36 selected after rigorous screening for relevance, methodology, and theme.

## Key Concepts
- Computational thinking development through AI-supported design problems
- Creative pedagogy using generative AI tools (ChatGPT, text-to-image engines)
- Hybrid learning environments combining traditional and digital modalities
- AI as creative co-participant in design thinking
- Critical AI literacy alongside technical skills

## Theoretical Framework
Design Thinking framework integrated with AI tools; Systematic literature review methodology

## Methods
Systematic literature review of Scopus database (2020-2025); 900 initial articles narrowed to 36 through rigorous screening for relevance, methodology, and theme alignment; Thematic analysis organized around computational thinking, creative pedagogy, and hybrid learning

## Main Arguments
- AI-enhanced design thinking improves algorithmic reasoning and problem-solving abilities essential for computational thinking
- Creative pedagogical techniques with AI tools (project-based learning) increase divergent thinking, motivation, and innovative outcomes
- Hybrid learning environments (AI-powered platforms + human-centered design) provide personalized, flexible, immersive experiences
- Generative AI tools enable creative experimentation and reflective learning
- Challenges include insufficient teacher preparedness, ethical considerations, reduced teamwork from over-specialized tasks
- Need for multidisciplinary, ethically conscious educational methods to maximize AI benefits

## Limitations & Critiques
- Measurement flaws with many studies relying on self-reported surveys or isolated performance tasks
- Concern that over-reliance on AI-generated outputs may diminish students' innate creative processes
- Methodological variation across studies (experimental, case studies, quasi-ethnographic) makes generalizable conclusions difficult
- Gap in mixed-method approaches combining quantitative performance data with qualitative observations
- Ethical concerns about data privacy, algorithmic bias, transparency in AI decision-making
- Problems of authorship and originality when AI contributes significantly to creative products
- Scalability and sustainability challenges across diverse educational environments
- Variations in resource availability, institutional culture, policy support
