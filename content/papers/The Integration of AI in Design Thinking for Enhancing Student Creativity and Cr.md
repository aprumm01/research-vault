# The Integration of AI in Design Thinking for Enhancing Student Creativity and Critical Thinking in Digital Media Learning (2025)

**Venue:** Journal of Creative Art and Design 4, Issue 1 (2025) 24-37

## Summary
Systematic literature review of 36 selected papers (from 118 initially identified via Scopus, 2020–2025) examining how AI integration in design thinking affects student creativity and critical thinking in digital media education. Identifies three core themes—computational thinking, creative pedagogy, and hybrid learning—and finds consistent evidence that AI-enhanced design thinking improves problem-solving, divergent thinking, and learning personalization.

## Key Concepts
- Design thinking framework
- Computational thinking
- Creative pedagogy
- Hybrid learning environments
- AI literacy

## Theoretical Framework
Design thinking as an iterative, human-centered framework; three-theme analytical structure derived inductively from the literature; draws on constructivist and project-based learning traditions.

## Methods
Systematic literature review; Scopus database, 2020–2025; 118 papers screened, 36 selected based on relevance, methodology, and theme alignment; thematic synthesis.

## Main Arguments
- AI-enhanced design thinking improves students' algorithmic reasoning and computational thinking
- Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, no-code platforms) enable creative experimentation and reduce barriers to reflective learning
- Hybrid learning environments combining AI with human-centered design thinking provide more personalized, flexible experiences
- Key challenges: insufficient teacher preparedness, ethical concerns, and risk of reduced teamwork through over-specialized AI tasks

## Limitations & Critiques
Many reviewed studies rely on self-reported surveys and isolated performance tasks, limiting confidence in causal claims about cognitive engagement; rapid pace of AI tool development means the review may quickly become outdated.
