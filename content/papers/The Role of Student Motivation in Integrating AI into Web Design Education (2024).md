# The Role of Student Motivation in Integrating AI into Web Design Education (2024)

## Summary
Longitudinal case study spanning Spring 2023–Spring 2024 in introductory UI/UX web design courses at Lindenwood University, finding that integrating text-based and image-based AI tools significantly enhanced student creative capabilities and project quality. Reveals that student resistance to AI is lower for tasks outside their primary major, with CS students accepting AI for design tasks more readily than for coding.
