# From Disruptions to Discussions: How GenAI Impacts Human Interactions in Software Development

# Study Partner Note: GenAI Impact on Human Interactions in Software Development

## Summary
This paper investigates how generative AI influences human-to-human interactions within software development teams, moving beyond individual productivity gains to examine team dynamics. The research reveals that GenAI shifts interaction patterns—enabling developers to become more self-sufficient on routine technical questions while potentially redirecting human conversation toward higher-level reasoning, mentorship, and social connection.

## Key Concepts
- **GenAI as technical mentor**: GenAI's ability to answer low-level technical questions positions it as an alternative to seeking help from teammates
- **Flow disruption**: Developers' subjective experience of focus and continuity in their work
- **Interaction delegation**: Shift from human-to-human technical support toward tool-based answers
- **Contextual expertise and human judgment**: Knowledge and decision-making that still requires human-to-human interaction
- **Team interaction dynamics**: The evolving norms and patterns of collaboration in the presence of AI tools

## Theoretical Framework
The paper employs an empirical, mixed-method approach grounded in understanding workplace technology adoption and team collaboration. It implicitly draws on sociotechnical perspectives that recognize technologies reshape not just individual workflows but also interpersonal relationships and organizational practices.

## Methods
Two-phase mixed-method study: Phase 1 involved 30 industrial developers providing experience sampling responses (627), end-of-workday surveys (207), and interviews (22 participants) over 5–12 days. Phase 2 surveyed 131 additional developers to validate and generalize findings across a larger population.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI enables developers to reduce dependency on teammates for routine technical questions, positioning the tool as a "technical mentor"
- Developers perceive GenAI reduces flow disruptions and supports sustained focus during work
- GenAI creates opportunities for more meaningful human interactions by shifting conversations toward clarification, joint reasoning, and alternative perspectives
- Despite GenAI capabilities, developers continue to value human interaction for contextual expertise, mentorship, and social connection
- Teams should adopt delegation norms, sustain human judgment in complex decisions, and intentionally preserve spaces for expertise-sharing and connection

## Limitations & Critiques
The study captures developer perceptions and self-reported experiences but may not fully capture actual behavioral changes in team interaction patterns. The research is limited to relatively short observation windows (5–12 days) and does not longitudinally track whether initial perceptions of GenAI's role in team dynamics persist over extended periods of adoption.
