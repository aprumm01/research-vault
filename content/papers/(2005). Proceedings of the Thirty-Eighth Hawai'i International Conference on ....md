## Summary
Herring et al. (2005) analyze patterns of conversation and interactivity in the blogosphere using content analysis of blog posts and comments. The study examines how bloggers engage readers, respond to comments, and participate in cross-blog conversations. Findings characterize the blogosphere as having low interactivity compared to earlier CMC forms, with conversation concentrated in specific blog types.

## Theoretical Framework
Computer-mediated discourse analysis (CMDA); interactivity frameworks in CMC.

## Methods
Content analysis; web content analysis; coding scheme; computer-mediated communication analysis
