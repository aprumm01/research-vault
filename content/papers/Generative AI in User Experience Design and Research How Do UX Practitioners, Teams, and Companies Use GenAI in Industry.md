# Generative AI in User Experience Design and Research: How Do UX Practitioners, Teams, and Companies Use GenAI in Industry?

## Summary
Interviews 24 UX practitioners from eight countries with varying roles and seniority levels to examine how GenAI is adopted at the individual, team, and company levels in the UX sector. Finds a significant absence of formal company GenAI policies, no team-wide practices, individual use favored for research and writing tasks but limited for visual design activities, and widespread need for prompting and output evaluation training.
