# Thinking through Prompting Cognitive Mediation in Human–AI

## Summary
Prompts are central to creative work with generative AI systems, yet they are often treated as simple and static interface components This paper proposes a different view: prompts function as cognitive interfaces that mediate between user intent and algorithmic generation Integrating theories of external cognition, interface semiotics, and pragmatist theories of situated inquiry, the paper provides a theoretical framing of prompting along three interrelated dimensions: semantic projection, compositional structuring, and interpretive negotiation.

## Key Concepts
- **Prompts as Cognitive Interfaces**: Prompts function as cognitive interfaces mediating between user intent and algorithmic generation, rather than simple static interface components or command-line tinkering in natural language
- **Semantic Projection**: Users project intent into representational form through metaphorical or affective language, engaging in informed speculation about what will resonate with system's opaque representation space; involves projecting expectations about system responses and revising based on assumptions
- **Compositional Structuring**: Prompts are assembled from conceptual components (task, tone, subject, style, medium, constraints) following implicit segmentation logic, forming modular reusable templates that scaffold communication strategies
- **Interpretive Negotiation**: Users continually assess generative responses against goals, iteratively adjusting prompts based on subtle misalignments to refine representational strategies; depends on ambiguity tolerance and domain-specific goals
- **Emergent Interface Concept**: Real interface lies in evolving semiotic and interpretive space created through prompt-response cycles, not in sparse visible interface; challenges classical affordance-based interface models
- **Epistemic Externalization**: Prompting engages users in constructing and refining representations of intent as form of epistemic work revealing how meaning gets processed, especially in exploratory or uncertain contexts
- **Domain-Specific Framing**: Designers, writers, and developers frame same goals differently drawing on distinct conventions reflecting varied mental models of how system parses language
- **Social Co-Construction of Literacy**: Prompt interpretation becomes shared labor in communities where users compare outputs, refine phrasing conventions, and collectively build prompting literacy as socio-cultural practice

## Theoretical Framework
- **External Cognition Theory (Scaife & Rogers, Zhang & Norman)**: Understanding how graphical and linguistic representations work to support distributed cognitive tasks and externalized thought
- **Interface Semiotics (Bertelsen & Pold)**: Treating interfaces as cultural systems shaped by representational strategies and aesthetic norms requiring ongoing interpretation to construct meaning
- **Pragmatist Design Theory (Schön, Dewey)**: Drawing on reflective practice and instrumental inquiry frameworks where tools mediate thinking and action
- **Three-Dimensional Prompting Framework**: Integrates semantic projection, compositional structuring, and interpretive negotiation as interdependent dimensions of cognitive mediation
- **Vignette-Based Analysis Method**: Uses concise narrative accounts to foreground patterns of behavior, decision-making, or interpretation in context, bridging empirical observation and theoretical synthesis

## Main Arguments
- **Prompting as Situated Representational Practice**: Prompts are best understood as instruments supporting users in articulating, refining, and transforming goals over time through dynamic representational activity deeply embedded in cognitive processes
- **Shift from Affordance to Speculation**: Classical affordance-based interface models inadequate for generative AI; prompting involves speculation rather than direct manipulation, with constraints discovered through use rather than visible cues
- **Opacity Necessitates Interpretive Work**: Generative models provide interpretive rather than legible feedback, creating substantial cognitive and representational activity despite minimalist visible interface
- **Three Dimensions Operate in Concert**: Semantic projection, compositional structuring, and interpretive negotiation work interdependently rather than in isolation, forming integrated system of cognitive mediation
- **Prompting as Tool for Thinking**: Interaction is less about "getting AI to do something" and more about exploring what can and should be asked, said, or represented, extending cognition through external representations
- **Temporal Hermeneutic Process**: Prompting constitutes sustained interpretive engagement where users maintain continuity across iterations, constructing cumulative sense of interaction as unfolding dialogue

## Limitations & Critiques
- **Conceptual Rather Than Empirical**: Paper is theory-led with analysis based on conceptual frameworks rather than formal empirical studies, potentially limiting external validity
- **Illustrative Not Representative Vignettes**: Examples are drawn from author's observations rather than systematic data collection, highlighting recurring dynamics without establishing general user trends or population-level patterns
- **Limited Scope of Empirical Grounding**: Single vignette from branding communication context may not generalize to other domains with different constraints, goals, or user expertise levels
- **Lack of Comparative Analysis**: No comparison between prompting strategies across different generative AI systems (ChatGPT vs Midjourney vs DALL-E) to establish whether theoretical dimensions hold across platforms
- **Minimal Treatment of Power Dynamics**: While discussing opacity and interpretation, paper does not systematically address how algorithmic inscrutability may reinforce asymmetric power relations between users and system designers
- **Underspecified Skill Development**: Theory describes prompting as learned practice but does not detail how users develop from novice to expert or what pedagogical approaches might support prompting literacy acquisition
- **Missing Quantitative Validation**: Three-dimensional framework (semantic projection, compositional structuring, interpretive negotiation) presented without quantitative evidence of their independence, interdependence, or relative importance
- **Limited Discussion of Failure Modes**: Paper focuses on successful or productive prompting interactions without systematically analyzing what happens when semantic projection fails or interpretive negotiation breaks down
- **Temporal Granularity Unexamined**: While noting temporal hermeneutic process, paper does not specify timescales or distinguish between within-session iteration and across-session learning trajectories
