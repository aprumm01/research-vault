## Relevance to Research

This proceedings volume contains peer-reviewed papers from the AI-HCI 2025 track, which focuses on artificial intelligence in human-computer interaction education and practice. Papers relevant to design students' experiences with GenAI, AI literacy in HCI education, and pedagogical approaches to AI tools would be found in this volume.
