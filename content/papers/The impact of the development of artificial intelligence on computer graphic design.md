# The Impact of the Development of Artificial Intelligence on Computer Graphic Design

## Summary
This article examines how artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming the graphic design industry, presenting both opportunities and challenges for designers. The author argues that since AI's advent in graphic design, it has posed a serious challenge to the traditional industry and designers by automating repetitive tasks, generating new design ideas, and enabling personalized design creation.

The paper explores the intersection of AI and graphic design across three main categories: automation in professional commercial applications, design tools based on templates for amateurs (often online), and experimental academic research in the field. The author provides case studies including Nutella's AI-powered packaging design (7 million unique designs sold in under a month) and the rise of platforms like Wix ADI for automated website creation.

The author conducted a survey among graphic designers in Armenia (November 2023 - January 2024) and conducted experiments with students at the State Academy of Fine Arts of Armenia, comparing work created with AI tools (Midjourney, Leonardo AI) versus traditional software (Photoshop, Illustrator). Results showed that 92% of designers used AI tools, 73% believed AI development positively affects individual designers' work, while 64% noted AI-created visual effects lack uniqueness and creativity.

## Key Concepts
- **Task Automation**: AI automates repetitive graphic tasks such as image management (brightness, contrast, color balance), typography adjustments (font size, spacing), and layout optimization (position, spacing, size of page elements)
- **Two-Dimensional vs Three-Dimensional Design**: Traditional 2D graphic design (posters, logos, book design, web interfaces) is expanding toward 3D design capabilities through AI, offering greater visual impact
- **Machine Learning (ML)**: A subset of AI that allows computers to analyze and interpret data without explicit programming, learning and improving as more data is processed
- **Wix ADI (Artificial Design Intelligence)**: Automated website creation system that allows users to create professional websites through AI-powered tools without traditional design skills

## Theoretical Framework
The paper draws on the historical relationship between graphic design and technology, citing Yaron Meron's research that the modern history of graphic design is inextricably linked with the technologies of each era. The integration of AI tools into graphic design in the 1990s marked a paradigm shift, with Kaiser's prediction that AI use in graphic design will become increasingly relevant. The author also references Meng's (2012) work on how AI unification in design could negatively impact creativity and originality.

## Methods
The study employed a mixed-methods approach:
1. **Survey research**: Conducted from November 2023 to January 2024 among graphic designers in Armenia and students/teachers at the State Academy of Fine Arts of Armenia
2. **Comparative experiment**: Two groups of students completed design tasks - one using only AI tools (Midjourney, Leonardo AI), the other using traditional software (Photoshop, Illustrator)
3. **Literature review**: Analysis of existing studies on AI in graphic design

## Main Arguments
- AI has the potential to greatly influence the future development of graphic design by automating functions, simplifying processes, saving time, and increasing productivity
- The development of AI in computer graphics has more positive than negative effects, helping professionals focus on creative approaches while solving technical problems faster
- AI can simulate existing systems but cannot achieve independent innovation - it lacks innovative thinking
- The shift from 2D to 3D design capabilities through AI brings fresh innovation and greater visual impact to the design industry
- AI enables personalization and relevant user experiences by analyzing demographics, browsing history, and preferences

## Limitations & Critiques
- **Lack of originality**: 64% of survey respondents noted that visual effects created using AI are devoid of uniqueness and are identical/not creative
- **Dependency risks**: Over-reliance on AI algorithms can lead to loss of human creative abilities and reduction in overall design quality
- **Excessive similarity**: AI-created designs show excessive similarity and generality, potentially leading to design industry unification
- **Cost barriers**: Introduction of AI technologies requires significant investment in logistics, software, and human resources, creating barriers for small businesses and independent designers
- **Ethical concerns**: Issues of copyright and ownership of AI-created projects require further study and regulation
- **Technical skill requirements**: Effective use of AI tools still requires technical skills that may be challenging to develop
- **Job displacement risk**: While AI supports designers, it may also lead to job cuts and loss of human creativity in the profession
