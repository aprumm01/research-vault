# How knowledge workers think gen AI will (not) transform their industries

## Summary
1 Generative AI is expected to have transformative effects in multiple knowledge industries To better understand how knowledge workers expect generative AI may affect their industries in the future, we conducted participatory research workshops for seven different industries, with a total of 54 participants across three US cities We describe participants’ expectations of generative AI’s impact, including a dominant.

## Key Concepts
- **Knowledge worker expectations** of generative AI's transformative effects across industries
- Participatory research workshops across seven different industries
- Dominant narratives about AI transformation vs skepticism about actual impact
- 54 participants across three US cities representing diverse knowledge work sectors
- Gap between hype and worker perceptions of realistic AI impact
- Industry-specific perspectives on AI transformation potential

## Theoretical Framework
- **Participatory research** methodology for understanding worker perspectives

narrative

## Main Arguments
- Knowledge workers hold nuanced and often skeptical views about generative AI's transformative potential
- Dominant narrative of AI transformation contrasts with workers' grounded industry-specific expectations
- Seven industries show varying degrees of expected AI impact based on work characteristics
- Workers recognize both opportunities and limitations of generative AI in their specific contexts
- Participatory workshops reveal gap between public AI discourse and practitioner reality
- Industry transformation expectations shaped by hands-on understanding of work complexity

## Limitations & Critiques
- Sample limited to 54 participants across three US cities
- Seven industries may not represent full diversity of knowledge work
- Workshop format may influence expressed perspectives
- Snapshot of expectations at specific moment in rapidly evolving AI landscape
- Potential geographic and cultural specificity to US contexts
- Need for longitudinal research to track how expectations evolve with AI development
