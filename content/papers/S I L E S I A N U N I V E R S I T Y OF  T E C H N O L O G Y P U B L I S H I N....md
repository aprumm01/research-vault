# ChatGPT as a Tool in Higher Education: Analysis of Student Utilization and Perception

## Summary
This study from Silesian University of Technology analyzes how higher education students are utilizing ChatGPT and how they perceive its role in academic work. Through surveys and usage analysis, the paper documents that students primarily use ChatGPT for writing assistance, explanation, and summarization, with significant variation by field of study and academic level. The study finds broadly positive student perceptions of ChatGPT's utility but significant concerns about accuracy and academic integrity. The paper provides empirical baseline data for institutional policy development on AI in higher education.

## Key Concepts
- Student ChatGPT adoption in higher education
- AI use patterns across academic disciplines
- Academic integrity and AI tools
- ChatGPT perceptions and satisfaction
- Higher education AI policy implications

## Theoretical Framework
Technology acceptance model; educational technology adoption; survey methodology; draws on emerging AI in education literature and academic integrity frameworks.

## Methods
Survey study with university students across faculties; usage frequency and purpose analysis; perception and satisfaction assessment; statistical analysis comparing adoption across student populations.

## Main Arguments
- Students across disciplines are adopting ChatGPT rapidly and primarily for writing and explanation tasks, regardless of institutional policy
- Academic integrity concerns are real but nuanced: most students distinguish between using AI for understanding vs. using it as a ghostwriter
- Student perceptions are broadly positive but tempered by accuracy concerns, especially for technical and specialized knowledge
- Institutions need policies that acknowledge the reality of student AI use rather than prohibition-only approaches

## Limitations & Critiques
Single institution study limits generalizability; self-reported usage may underestimate AI use due to integrity concerns; the specific capabilities of ChatGPT have evolved significantly since initial data collection; Polish higher education context may not transfer to other national systems.
