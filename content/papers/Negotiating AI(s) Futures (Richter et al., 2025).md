# Negotiating AI(s) Futures (Richter et al., 2025)

## Summary
Comparative stakeholder interview study examining how AI imaginaries are actively negotiated and co-constructed across institutional sectors and national contexts in the United States, China, and Germany. Challenges monolithic accounts of national AI strategies, showing that within-country variation between industry, government, academia, media, and civil society rivals cross-national differences—AI futures are multiple and contested within nations, not only between them.

## Key Concepts
- **Competing AI imaginaries**: The coexistence of contested, sector-specific visions of AI futures within and across nations
- **Stakeholder negotiation**: The active process by which different institutional actors contest and co-construct AI futures
- **Cross-national comparison**: US, China, Germany as distinct but internally heterogeneous AI imaginary spaces

## Main Arguments
1. AI imaginaries are not monolithic at the national level—within-country sectoral variation is substantial
2. Stakeholders actively contest AI futures; no single actor group controls the dominant imaginary
3. Cross-national comparison reveals both shared anxieties and distinctive framings shaped by political-economic context
