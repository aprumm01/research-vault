# Exploring the Impact of AI Language Model ChatGPT on User Experience (IJTIM)

## Summary
AL-Sa'di and Miller investigate the integration of ChatGPT (version 3.5) into the Design Thinking process for product design. The study focused on the Define and Ideate stages, redesigning two previous projects with AI assistance and supplementing this with interviews with five designers and analysis of ten Product Manager reports. Findings indicate that AI tools can improve the efficiency and creativity of UI design within Design Thinking workflows, but that successful implementation requires a combination of technical expertise and creative design judgment. The paper positions AI as a complement to human creativity rather than a replacement.

## Key Concepts
- ChatGPT integration into Design Thinking process
- Define and Ideate stages as primary AI-assisted phases
- AI as a complement to human creative expertise
- Product design efficiency and creativity with AI tools
- Practitioner interviews and Product Manager report analysis

## Theoretical Framework
Design Thinking methodology (empathize, define, ideate, prototype, test); situates AI tool use within established innovation frameworks; draws on emerging literature on AI in product design and Fourth Industrial Revolution contexts; published in IJTIM (International Journal on Technology, Innovation, and Management).

## Methods
Qualitative and practice-based mixed-methods study: redesign of two previous design projects using ChatGPT; semi-structured interviews with five designers; analysis of ten Product Manager reports; case study approach applied in a New Zealand educational and professional context.

## Main Arguments
- Integrating ChatGPT into the Define and Ideate phases of Design Thinking can improve both efficiency and creative output in product design
- Successful AI-assisted design requires practitioners to possess both technical expertise with AI tools and domain-level creative design thinking
- AI tools complement human creativity rather than supplanting it; the human designer remains central to meaningful design decisions
- Prior research focused primarily on Design Thinking as a methodology but had not examined AI tool integration at the process level

## Limitations & Critiques
The study is limited to two redesigned projects and a small sample (five designers), which restricts generalizability. The findings are specific to ChatGPT 3.5 in 2023 and may not generalize to newer models or different AI tools. The research context (New Zealand, IJTIM) may reflect particular organizational and professional norms not widely representative.
