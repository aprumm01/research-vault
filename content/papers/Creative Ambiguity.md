# Creative Ambiguity

## Summary
Dalsgaard argues that when generative AI tools produce outputs that misalign with user expectations, the resulting creative ambiguity and cognitive tension should be reframed as generative forces rather than failures to be corrected. Drawing on theories of cognitive dissonance, frame analysis, and predictive processing, he proposes that these moments of misfit can catalyze reflection, conceptual reframing, and creative reinterpretation. The paper examines five cross-modal cases spanning writing, design, music, code, and prototyping to demonstrate how friction with AI can open new pathways for ideation.

## Key Concepts
- Creative ambiguity
- Cognitive tension and cognitive dissonance
- Predictive processing and frame conflict
- Productive misalignment in human-AI interaction
- Creativity support tools

## Theoretical Framework
The paper synthesizes theories of ambiguity in design (Gaver et al.), pragmatist design theory, cognitive dissonance, frame analysis, and predictive processing to construct a unified account of how misalignment between human intent and AI output can be a driver of creative insight rather than a source of error.

## Methods
Conceptual and theoretical paper grounded in cross-modal case analysis. Five cases spanning writing, image generation, music, code, and physical form prototyping are examined as illustrations of how creative ambiguity and cognitive tension function in practice. No primary empirical data collection.

## Main Arguments
- Misalignment between user intent and AI output is not merely a usability failure but a potential catalyst for creative insight
- Creative ambiguity (outputs that resist easy interpretation) and cognitive tension (interpretive conflict between user models and AI responses) function as generative forces
- Designing for reflection rather than optimization requires deliberately working with friction rather than minimizing it
- Productive misalignment invites users into deeper interpretive labor that can expand the conceptual space of a creative project

## Limitations & Critiques
The paper is primarily theoretical and relies on illustrative cases rather than controlled empirical study, making it difficult to determine when cognitive tension is productively generative versus simply frustrating. The framework may not account for users who lack the domain expertise to meaningfully reframe unexpected AI outputs.
