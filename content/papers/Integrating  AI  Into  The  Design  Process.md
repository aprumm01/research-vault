# Integrating AI into the Design Process

## Summary
This practitioner-oriented paper examines methods and frameworks for integrating AI into the design process across multiple design disciplines, offering a taxonomy of AI integration points and design process stages where AI adds most value. Drawing on case studies from product design, graphic design, and UX, the paper argues that AI integration is most effective when designed around the designer's process rather than around the AI tool's capabilities. The paper provides practical frameworks for designers seeking to systematically incorporate AI into professional practice.

## Key Concepts
- AI integration in design process
- Design process stage analysis (research, ideation, prototyping, production)
- AI as process augmentation vs. replacement
- Human oversight in AI-integrated design
- Taxonomy of AI design tools by function

## Theoretical Framework
Design process methodology; human-centered AI integration; draws on established design process models (double diamond, UCD) to organize AI integration strategy.

## Methods
Multiple case studies across design disciplines; practitioner documentation and reflection; taxonomy development from empirical cases; literature review of AI design tool capabilities.

## Main Arguments
- AI integration works best when it augments specific, bounded phases of the design process rather than being applied uniformly across all design work
- Designers who understand their own process can identify the highest-value AI integration points; those who don't tend toward superficial AI use
- Human oversight and editorial judgment remain essential at all stages of AI-integrated design work
- The taxonomy of AI tools by design function helps designers match tools to process needs rather than adopting tools opportunistically

## Limitations & Critiques
Case study evidence is primarily from design disciplines with strong visual output; service design and systems design integration is less thoroughly addressed; the pace of AI tool development means specific tool recommendations will date quickly.
