# Comparing Grounded Theory and Topic Modeling: Extreme Divergence or Complementary Tools? (Marquette, 2017)

## Summary
This methodological paper compares grounded theory and topic modeling as approaches to qualitative data analysis, examining their epistemological assumptions, strengths, weaknesses, and appropriate contexts of use. The authors find that despite surface similarities (both generate categories from data without a priori frameworks), grounded theory and topic modeling reflect fundamentally different epistemologies: grounded theory seeks substantive theory about social processes; topic modeling seeks statistical patterns in text. The paper offers guidance for researchers choosing between and potentially combining the two approaches.

## Key Concepts
- Grounded theory (Glaser, Strauss, Charmaz)
- Topic modeling (LDA, structural topic models)
- Epistemological comparison of qualitative methods
- Theory generation vs. pattern discovery
- Mixed-methods implications

## Theoretical Framework
Research methodology meta-analysis; constructivist grounded theory (Charmaz); computational text analysis; draws on qualitative research methodology literature and natural language processing to compare the two approaches.

## Methods
Methodological review and comparison; analysis of the epistemological assumptions, procedural requirements, and output types of grounded theory and topic modeling; demonstration using a shared dataset.

## Main Arguments
- Grounded theory and topic modeling are not interchangeable despite both being inductive, data-driven approaches
- Grounded theory produces substantive theoretical accounts of social processes with interpretive richness; topic modeling produces statistical descriptions of textual patterns
- The choice between them should be driven by the research question's epistemological requirements, not by sample size or convenience
- Under appropriate conditions, the two approaches can be combined: topic modeling for initial pattern identification; grounded theory for theory-building on those patterns

## Limitations & Critiques
The comparison does not cover all variants of either approach; the demonstration case may not generalize; the epistemological comparison may be perceived as privileging qualitative over computational approaches by some readers.
