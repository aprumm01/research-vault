# The Impact of Generative AI on the CSCW Landscape (Freeman et al., 2025)

## Summary
Perspectives paper examining how the rise of generative AI is reshaping the CSCW research landscape across three dimensions: what HCI education must now teach, how industry funding and agenda-setting are shifting toward GenAI applications, and how funding bodies are redirecting research priorities in response to GenAI's dominance. Argues that the CSCW community must actively shape rather than merely respond to GenAI's influence on its own field.

## Key Concepts
- **GenAI impact on HCI education**: How generative AI changes what skills, methods, and ethical frameworks HCI programs must teach
- **Industry dynamics**: Shifts in corporate research agendas toward GenAI applications and away from traditional HCI concerns
- **Funding priorities**: Changes in grant funding and institutional support for GenAI vs. other research directions

## Main Arguments
1. GenAI is reshaping CSCW's research questions, not merely adding a new application domain
2. HCI education must actively adapt to teach GenAI-specific skills, ethics, and critical evaluation frameworks
3. The CSCW community risks losing agency over its own agenda if industry funding dominates GenAI research direction
