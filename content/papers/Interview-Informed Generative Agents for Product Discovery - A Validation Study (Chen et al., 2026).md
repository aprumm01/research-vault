# Interview-Informed Generative Agents for Product Discovery A Validation Study - Wang et al - 2026

## Summary
Large language models (LLMs) have shown strong performance on standardized social science instruments, but their value for product discovery remains unclear We investigate whether interviewinformed generative agents can simulate user responses in concept testing scenarios Using in-depth workflow interviews with knowledge workers, we created personalized agents and compared their evaluations of novel AI concepts against the same participants’ responses.

## Key Concepts
- **Interview-informed generative agents**: LLM-based agents personalized from user interviews
- **Product discovery** and concept testing applications of AI agents
- Validation study comparing agent responses to actual participant responses
- In-depth workflow interviews with knowledge workers as basis for agent creation
- Novel AI concepts as test cases for agent simulation validity
- LLM performance on social science instruments vs product discovery tasks
- Personalized agents vs generic LLM responses

## Theoretical Framework
- **User research methodology**: Traditional interview-based approaches
- **Agent-based modeling**: Simulation of human responses through computational agents
- Validation frameworks for synthetic vs authentic user data
- Product discovery and concept testing methodologies

## Methods
survey, interview

## Main Arguments
- LLMs show strong performance on standardized instruments but value for product discovery unclear
- Interview-informed agents can potentially simulate user responses in concept testing
- Validation against actual participant responses essential for establishing agent utility
- Personalization from workflow interviews may improve agent fidelity over generic LLMs
- Knowledge worker workflows provide rich foundation for creating representative agents
- Product discovery requires different validation than social science instrument performance
- Comparison between agent and participant evaluations reveals boundary conditions for synthetic methods

## Limitations & Critiques
- Validation study focuses on concept testing, may not generalize to other product discovery activities
- Knowledge worker sample may not represent all user populations
- Interview depth and quality affects agent personalization effectiveness
- Temporal stability: agent responses based on interview snapshot may not capture user evolution
- Novel AI concepts as test cases may introduce domain-specific biases
- Need for broader validation across different product categories and user types
- Ethical considerations in using synthetic agents to replace or supplement real user research
