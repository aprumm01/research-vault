# Creating and Evaluating Personas Using Generative AI: A Scoping Review of 81 Articles (Amin et al., 2026)

## Summary
Amin, Salminen, Ahmed, Tervola, Sethi, and Jansen conduct a scoping review of 81 articles (2022-2025) on the application of generative AI (GenAI) in persona development -- the creation, evaluation, and application of AI-generated user personas. The review addresses how GenAI is used in persona development, how GenAI personas are evaluated, and what ethical considerations apply. Key findings include: 61% of articles share resources (personas, code, or datasets); 45% lack any evaluation; 86% use only GPT models; and conversational persona interfaces are increasingly common alongside traditional static profiles. The authors flag the risk of circularity -- when the same model both generates and evaluates its own outputs -- and find evidence that GenAI is reducing human developer involvement in persona creation. Guidelines for responsible GenAI persona development are proposed.

## Key Concepts
- Generative AI personas (GenAIPs): LLM-generated user representation profiles
- Scoping review methodology: 885 articles screened, 81 included
- Circularity risk: same GenAI model used to both generate and evaluate personas
- Reproducibility: 61% of articles shared resources, but evaluation is often absent
- GPT dominance: 86% of included studies rely on GPT family models
- Conversational interfaces as an emerging persona modality
- Responsible integration guidelines for human-AI collaboration in persona development

## Theoretical Framework
Scoping review following PRISMA guidelines and HCI literature review standards; five academic databases searched (ACMDL, IEEE Xplore, WoS, Scopus, arXiv); builds on prior persona research reviews (quantitative personas, data-driven personas, social impact personas); positions findings within the broader context of HCI user modeling and responsible AI; presented at CHI 2026 (Barcelona).

## Methods
Systematic scoping review: keyword search across five databases, initial yield of 885 articles; deduplication, multi-stage screening for English language, peer review status, GenAI application, and HCI alignment; snowball sampling via Google Scholar Alerts; final corpus of 81 articles from 2022 to August 2025; deductive content analysis across three research questions: GenAI methods, evaluation approaches, and ethical considerations.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI is rapidly transforming persona development, but current practice lacks standardization: nearly half of studies skip evaluation entirely, and the field is narrowly concentrated on GPT
- Conversational persona interfaces represent an important design direction that goes beyond static profile generation, but their evaluation quality lags behind their proliferation
- Circularity (using LLMs to both generate and evaluate) is an underacknowledged threat to validity across the GenAI persona literature
- GenAI is eroding human developer involvement in the persona creation process, raising concerns about oversight, bias amplification, and accountability
- Responsible integration requires explicit evaluation protocols, human-in-the-loop design, and attention to bias, privacy, and transparency

## Limitations & Critiques
Search was restricted to articles up to August 2025; arXiv preprints were included, which may reduce peer review rigor; scoping reviews map breadth rather than assessing quality, so findings are descriptive rather than evaluative; single rater for some analysis phases; the rapid pace of the field means the review may already be partially outdated at publication.
