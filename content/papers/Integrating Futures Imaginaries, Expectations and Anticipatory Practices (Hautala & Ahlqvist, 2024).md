# Integrating Futures Imaginaries, Expectations and Anticipatory Practices (Hautala & Ahlqvist, 2024)

## Summary
Examines how AI practitioners integrate futures imaginaries, expectations, and anticipatory practices into their everyday work, drawing on interviews with Finnish AI developers and researchers. Proposes a conceptual framework for understanding how practitioners navigate between current technical constraints and imagined futures, arguing that anticipatory practices—not just outcomes—shape how AI systems are designed, justified, and abandoned before they are built.

## Key Concepts
- **Anticipatory practices**: The routinized activities through which practitioners orient their work toward imagined futures
- **Expectations**: Specific, near-term predictions about AI capability that guide resource allocation and project scope
- **Futures imaginaries**: Broader, values-laden visions of what AI could mean for society and work

## Main Arguments
1. Practitioners hold distinct but interrelated futures imaginaries, expectations, and anticipatory practices simultaneously
2. The gap between expectation and imaginary is where most AI project failures and pivots occur
3. Anticipatory practices deserve empirical study as a primary site of AI future-making
