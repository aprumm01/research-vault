# Inventing Ourselves Out of Jobs: America's Debate over Technological Unemployment (Review, 2001)

## Summary
Reviews a book examining America's long-running debate over whether machinery destroys more jobs than it creates, tracing the argument from the 19th century through the 20th century's automation anxieties. Situates recurring technological unemployment fears in historical context, showing how each technological wave generates similar concerns and similar reassurances.
