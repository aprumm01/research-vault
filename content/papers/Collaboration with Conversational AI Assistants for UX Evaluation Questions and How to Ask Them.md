# Collaboration with Conversational AI Assistants for UX Evaluation: Questions and How to Ask Them

## Summary
Kuang, Jahangirzadeh Soure, Fan, Zhao, and Shinohara (RIT, University of Waterloo, HKUST Guangzhou, CHI 2023) investigate how UX evaluators can collaborate with conversational AI assistants to formulate effective evaluation questions — comparing voice and text input modalities. The study addresses a practical bottleneck in UX evaluation: generating comprehensive, appropriately scoped questions is cognitively demanding, and AI assistance could reduce this burden if evaluators know how to ask. The paper uses diary study, Wizard-of-Oz, and survey methodologies to assess both the effectiveness of AI assistance and the effects of modality (voice vs. text) on question quality and user experience.

## Theoretical Framework
Human-computer interaction evaluation methodology; extends conversational AI research into the UX practitioner workflow context.

## Methods
Diary study + Wizard-of-Oz + survey/questionnaire; multi-modal comparison (voice vs. text input) with UX evaluators using a conversational AI assistant to formulate evaluation questions. CHI 2023, Hamburg (16 pages, DOI: 10.1145/3544548.3581247). Published April 2023.

## Main Arguments
- Conversational AI can meaningfully support UX evaluators in generating more comprehensive evaluation questions, reducing cognitive load
- Voice and text input produce different interaction patterns and question types — voice enables more exploratory questioning while text supports precise specification
- How you ask AI matters as much as what you ask — question formulation skill transfers to AI-assisted evaluation contexts
- AI assistance is most valuable at the question-generation stage, less so at interpretation and synthesis stages of evaluation

## Limitations & Critiques
Wizard-of-Oz design means participants were not interacting with actual AI — findings may not transfer to real AI systems with different capabilities and failure modes. Limited to question formulation, not full UX evaluation workflows.
