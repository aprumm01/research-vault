# Visions of the Future (Dzreke et al., 2025)

## Summary
Critical discourse analysis of tech CEO predictions about AI and the labor market, arguing that CEOs construct futures rather than merely predict them. Examines how CEO discourse about automation and job displacement shapes institutional, policy, and worker responses to AI, finding that CEO predictions systematically frame AI transformation as beneficial for innovation and competitiveness while displacing risk and disruption onto workers and labor markets.

## Key Concepts
- **CEO discourse as future-construction**: How tech CEO predictions perform the futures they claim to predict
- **AI labor market framing**: The rhetorical strategies through which AI job displacement is normalized and de-politicized
- **Critical discourse analysis**: Analytical framework for revealing ideological assumptions in public executive communication

## Main Arguments
1. Tech CEO predictions about AI and labor are performative — they help construct the futures they claim to describe
2. CEO discourse frames AI transformation as inevitable and beneficial while distributing costs onto workers
3. Critical discourse analysis reveals the political-economic interests embedded in AI futures narratives
