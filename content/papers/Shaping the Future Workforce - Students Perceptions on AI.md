## Abstract

Research purpose. This paper aims to analyse how university students perceive the role of Artificial Intelligence and human-centric technologies in shaping the future workforce within the context of Industry 5.0. By focusin

## Summary
Ferhataj, Biçoku, and Memaj examine how university students perceive AI and human-centric technologies in the context of Industry 5.0—a framework emphasizing human-machine collaboration and social value creation. Their survey-based study finds that students recognize AI's transformative potential for future work but are ambivalent about its impact on human roles, with significant variation by discipline and prior AI exposure.

## Key Concepts
- Student perceptions of AI
- Industry 5.0
- Future workforce
- Human-centric technology
- AI literacy in higher education

## Theoretical Framework
Industry 5.0 theory and workforce readiness frameworks inform the investigation of how students across disciplines perceive AI's role in shaping their future professional contexts.

## Methods
Survey study; quantitative analysis of student perceptions across disciplines; analysis of variation by background characteristics and AI exposure.

## Main Arguments
- Students recognize AI as a transformative force but show significant uncertainty about its impact on human roles
- Industry 5.0 framing—emphasizing human-AI collaboration rather than replacement—resonates positively with students
- AI literacy and prior exposure significantly shape how students perceive AI's workforce implications
- Higher education must actively address student concerns and develop AI readiness alongside technical skills
