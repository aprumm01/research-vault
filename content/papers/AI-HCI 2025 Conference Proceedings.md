# Artificial Intelligence in HCI: 6th International Conference AI-HCI 2025

## Summary

This volume constitutes the proceedings of the 6th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence in HCI (AI-HCI 2025), held as part of HCI International 2025 in Gothenburg, Sweden. The conference brings together researchers exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence and human-computer interaction, covering topics from AI-powered user interfaces to ethical considerations in AI design. As a proceedings volume, it contains multiple papers across various AI-HCI topics rather than a single coherent argument.

## Key Concepts

- **AI in HCI**: Integration of artificial intelligence techniques in human-computer interaction research and design
- **User experience with AI**: How users perceive, interact with, and trust AI-powered systems
- **AI ethics in interface design**: Ethical considerations for AI-powered interfaces
- **Explainable AI (XAI)**: Making AI decision-making transparent and understandable to users
- **Human-AI collaboration**: Designing systems that effectively combine human and AI capabilities

## Theoretical Framework

As a conference proceedings, papers draw on various frameworks:
- Human-centered AI design principles
- User experience research methodologies
- AI/ML technical foundations
- HCI research traditions (usability, accessibility, user studies)
- Ethics of technology frameworks

## Methods

Various methods represented across papers:
- User studies (experiments, interviews, surveys)
- Design research
- Technical AI/ML development
- Literature reviews and theoretical analysis
- Case studies

## Main Themes (across proceedings)

1. **AI-enhanced interfaces**: Using AI to improve user interface design and interaction
2. **Trust and transparency**: How users develop trust in AI systems and the role of transparency
3. **Accessible AI**: Making AI-powered systems accessible to diverse users
4. **AI for creative tools**: AI applications in creative and design software
5. **Ethical AI design**: Incorporating ethical considerations in AI system development
6. **Multimodal AI interaction**: Voice, gesture, and other modalities for AI interaction
7. **AI in education**: Educational applications of AI-HCI research

## Note on Scope

This is a large conference proceedings volume (46MB) containing many individual papers. A comprehensive summary would require examining each paper individually. The document represents the current state of AI-HCI research as of 2025, with particular relevance to:
- AI-powered educational technologies
- Human-centered AI design
- Ethical considerations in AI development
- User studies of AI systems
