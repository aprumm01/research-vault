# Reflecting on the Integration of Generative AI in Design Education (Voces y Silencios, 2024)

**DOI:** [10.18175/VyS16.2.2025.9](https://doi.org/10.18175/VyS16.2.2025.9)

**Venue:** distinct experiences: personal explorations as a designer a
**Institution:** orth Carolina State University, North Carolina (USA) https:/

## Key Finding
This testimonial article reflects on integrating Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) into design education through three distinct experiences: personal explorations as a designer and educator

## Summary
A testimonial phenomenological reflection by a design educator at NC State on integrating GenAI into three contexts: personal creative exploration, collaborative faculty professional development, and an undergraduate digital design course. Finds that GenAI democratizes creative engagement and improves equity in access to design tools, while also introducing ethical risks around bias, intellectual property, and over-reliance.

## Key Concepts
- Democratization of creativity
- TPACK (Technological Pedagogical Content Knowledge)
- Kolb's Experiential Learning Theory
- Constructivist learning
- Reflective practice

## Theoretical Framework
Draws on Constructivist Learning Theory, Kolb's Experiential Learning Theory, Reflective Practice, TPACK, and the concept of democratization of creativity; uses a phenomenological approach to frame lived experience as data.

## Methods
Phenomenological testimonial; autoethnographic reflection on three teaching/practice experiences; synthesis of recent empirical literature to contextualize personal findings; no quantitative data.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI tools lower barriers to creative expression and can improve educational equity for students with limited technical backgrounds
- Experiential, iterative learning cycles are the most appropriate pedagogical model for integrating GenAI
- Educators must engage critically with ethical considerations—bias in training data, IP concerns, and risks of over-reliance
- Faculty collaborative learning communities are essential infrastructure for responsible GenAI pedagogy

## Limitations & Critiques
Based on a single educator's subjective experience at one institution; testimonial format limits replicability and generalizability; the Latin American educational context of the publishing journal may create cultural framing that does not transfer broadly.
