# Impacts of AI on Human Designers: Systematic Literature Review (Sinlapanuntakul and Zachry)

## Abstract

The intersection of artificial intelligence (AI) and design is gaining significant attention among professional communication researchers. Although challenges in designing with and for AI have been generally investigated, current systematic literature reviews have neglected to consider the tensions between AI and human designers during design work and how that work is impacted. This study extends previous work by offering a more nuanced understanding of how AI affects designers — examining both enabling and constraining effects on design practice, professional identity, and the nature of design work itself.

## Summary

Sinlapanuntakul and Zachry conduct a systematic literature review specifically focused on tensions between AI and human designers, distinguishing their contribution from earlier reviews by centering the *conflict* and *negotiation* inherent in AI-assisted design rather than treating AI as simply a tool. The review identifies patterns in how AI constrains and enables designers and synthesizes implications for professional communication and design practice. Published in IEEE Transactions on Professional Communication, it sits at the intersection of communication studies and design research.

## Key Concepts
- AI-human designer tensions (enabling vs. constraining effects)
- Professional communication in AI-assisted design
- Systematic literature review methodology
- Design work transformation under AI

## Theoretical Framework
Professional communication framework combined with design cognition literature; examines how the introduction of AI into design work creates communicative tensions and professional identity challenges documented in the scholarly literature.

## Methods
Systematic literature review following PRISMA or similar protocol; database search of design, HCI, and professional communication literature; thematic synthesis of findings across included studies.

## Main Arguments
- AI introduction creates distinct tensions with human designers, not just opportunities for augmentation
- These tensions involve professional identity, design judgment, and communicative roles
- Existing reviews have underrepresented the conflict dimensions of AI-designer interaction
- A tension-aware framework is needed for understanding AI's impact on design practice
