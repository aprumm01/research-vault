# Prompt Engineering


## Summary
Prompt Engineering is a thematic node capturing the emerging practice and study of crafting effective natural language prompts to elicit desired outputs from large language models and generative AI systems. In this research vault, the node is relevant both as a new technical skill demand for designers and knowledge workers, and as a site of theoretical interest regarding how the human-AI interface is being redefined through language. Papers in the corpus discuss prompt engineering as a new form of design literacy, as a source of creative and professional inequality (those who can prompt well vs. those who cannot), and as a key element of vibe coding workflows where design intent is communicated through language rather than direct manipulation.

## Key Concepts
- Prompt structure: instructions, context, examples, constraints, output format
- Few-shot and zero-shot prompting strategies
- Prompt engineering as design skill and professional competency
- Chain-of-thought and iterative prompting for complex tasks
- Prompt quality as a mediator of AI output quality
- Creative ambiguity in prompts: leaving space for AI generativity

## Theoretical Framework
HCI and AI research; natural language processing; design cognition (framing intention through language); situated in this vault within GenAI in UX and Design Practice, Human-AI Creative Collaboration, and AI in Design Education communities; connects to debates about who benefits from GenAI tools and what literacy they require.
