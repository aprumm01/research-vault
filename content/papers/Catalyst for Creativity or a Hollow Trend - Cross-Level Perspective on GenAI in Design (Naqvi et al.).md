# Catalyst for Creativity or a Hollow Trend: Cross-Level Perspective on GenAI in Design (Naqvi et al.)

## Abstract

Generative AI image creation tools have the potential to transform design education and practice, but raise critical concerns for creativity and ownership. The authors leverage the 2022 launch of tools like Midjourney and DALL-E as a dividing point among design enthusiasts, examining perspectives from multiple levels — individual practitioners, design education institutions, and the broader field — on whether GenAI tools catalyze or commodify creativity.

## Summary

Naqvi et al. offer a cross-level analysis of how GenAI image tools are received across individual designers, design schools, and the design profession, finding that enthusiasm and skepticism are distributed unevenly across these levels. The paper challenges the assumption that GenAI tools straightforwardly enhance creativity, showing that at some levels the tools risk deskilling or trivializing design knowledge. The cross-level framing is the key methodological contribution, revealing contradictions that single-level analyses miss.

## Key Concepts
- Cross-level analysis (individual, institutional, field)
- Creativity and ownership concerns with AI-generated imagery
- GenAI tools as disruptive vs. catalytic
- Design education and competency formation under AI pressure

## Theoretical Framework
Multi-level organizational/sociological framing examining adoption and reception of technology across individual, institutional, and field levels; draws on creativity theory and design education scholarship.

## Methods
Conceptual/analytical paper drawing on discourse analysis of community responses and literature, structured around the 2022 Midjourney/DALL-E launch as a natural dividing event.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI tools are perceived very differently depending on the level of analysis (individual vs. institutional vs. field)
- Creativity and authorship concerns are most acute at the individual and institutional levels
- The same tool can be both catalyst and hollow trend depending on how it is adopted and framed
- Design education needs cross-level thinking to respond adequately to GenAI integration
