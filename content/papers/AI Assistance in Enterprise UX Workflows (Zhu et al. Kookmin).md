# AI Assistance in Enterprise UX Workflows (Zhu et al. Kookmin)

## Summary
Zhu et al. examine how ChatGPT affects enterprise UX designers' design brief creation processes at Kookmin University, using expert interviews, experimental testing, and third-party evaluations. The study finds that AI tools significantly enhance both operational efficiency and subjective perceptions across most design brief tasks, though benefits vary by task type and designer experience level. The paper provides empirical evidence for AI's role in professional UX workflows and visual comparisons of AI-assisted vs. unassisted design brief quality.

## Key Concepts
- AI-assisted design brief creation
- Enterprise UX design workflows
- ChatGPT in professional design practice
- Operational and perceptual effects of AI tool use

## Theoretical Framework
Human-computer interaction and professional practice frameworks; uses mixed-methods design to assess both objective workflow changes and subjective designer experiences with AI assistance.

## Methods
Three-phase empirical study: expert interviews, experimental testing with designers, and third-party expert evaluations of output quality; Interaction Design Lab, Graduate School of Techno Design, Kookmin University; published in Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence.

## Main Arguments
- ChatGPT significantly enhances design brief creation efficiency for enterprise UX designers
- AI assistance improves both the process experience and subjective quality perception
- Benefits are uneven across task types — structured tasks benefit more than judgment-heavy tasks
- AI tools reshape communication and analysis workflows in professional design contexts
