# Responsible AI and Ethics


## Summary
Responsible AI and Ethics is a thematic node anchoring research on the ethical design, deployment, and governance of AI systems -- including questions of fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy, safety, and human oversight. In this research vault, the node connects AI's technical capabilities to their social consequences in design and knowledge work contexts: papers examine how AI tools can perpetuate bias, undermine privacy, displace workers, and erode professional autonomy if deployed without ethical guardrails. The community also includes work on how practitioners and organizations are (or are not) building ethical awareness and practice into AI adoption workflows.

## Key Concepts
- Fairness, accountability, transparency, and explainability (FATE) in AI
- Human oversight: keeping humans in the loop for consequential AI decisions
- Privacy and data consent in AI-assisted professional workflows
- AI ethics frameworks: principles vs. regulation vs. practice
- Practitioner ethics: designer responsibility for AI-powered products
- Ethical imagination: using design fictions and speculative methods to surface AI harms

## Theoretical Framework
Applied ethics; critical AI studies; value-sensitive design (Friedman); responsible innovation frameworks; professional ethics traditions (AECT, ISPI codes applied to AI); situated in this vault between Algorithmic Bias and Fairness, Responsible AI Governance, AI and Future of Work, and GenAI in UX and Design Practice communities.
