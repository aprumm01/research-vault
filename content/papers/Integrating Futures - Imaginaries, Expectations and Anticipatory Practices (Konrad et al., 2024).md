# Integrating futures imaginaries expectations and anticipatory practices practitioners of artificial intelligence between now and future

## Summary
ARTICLE HISTORY Artiﬁcial intelligence (AI) is a world-changing technology due to its abilities to learn independently, process big data, and automate human work Imagining the socio-technical future is necessary, but challenging, in the era of AI that rapidly developing technology has made turbulent In this study, we addressed the need to understand the ways AI practitioners actualise their AI-related futures imaginaries at the.

## Key Concepts
- **Futures imaginaries**: How AI practitioners envision and conceptualize AI-related futures
- **Anticipatory practices**: Ways practitioners actualize their future visions in present work
- **Expectations** about AI as world-changing technology with learning, big data, and automation capabilities
- Socio-technical future imagination in turbulent AI era
- Gap between futures imaginaries and current practice
- AI practitioners navigating between present realities and future possibilities

## Theoretical Framework
- **Futures studies**: Understanding how future-oriented thinking shapes present action
- **Socio-technical imaginaries**: Collective visions of technological futures
- **Anticipatory governance**: How expectations shape technology development
- Practice theory for understanding how imaginaries become actualized

## Methods
case study, interview

## Main Arguments
- AI practitioners actively work to actualize their futures imaginaries through anticipatory practices
- Imagining socio-technical future is necessary but challenging in rapidly developing AI era
- Practitioners navigate between present technical work and long-term visions of AI impact
- Expectations about AI's world-changing capabilities shape current development practices
- Understanding how practitioners integrate futures thinking essential for responsible AI development
- Gap exists between imaginaries and actualization requiring bridging strategies
- Turbulent technological change makes future-oriented thinking both critical and difficult

## Limitations & Critiques
- Case study and interview approach may not capture full range of practitioner perspectives
- Focus on expectations may miss emergent or unarticulated dimensions of practice
- Challenge of studying futures imaginaries that may evolve rapidly
- Need to examine how imaginaries vary across different AI practitioner communities
- Relationship between stated imaginaries and actual practices requires critical examination
- Temporal dimension: futures imaginaries may become outdated as AI develops
