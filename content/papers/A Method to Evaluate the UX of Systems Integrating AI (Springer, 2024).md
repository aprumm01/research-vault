# A Method to Evaluate the UX of Systems Integrating AI (Springer, 2024)

# Study Partner Note: User Experience + Artificial Intelligence

## Summary
This paper presents a comprehensive method for evaluating user experience (UX) in AI-infused systems, arguing that conventional UX assessment tools are inadequate for the unique complexities of AI-integrated products. The work matters because it bridges a critical gap between sophisticated AI technologies and poor interaction quality, restoring human-centered design considerations to AI product development.

## Key Concepts
- **AI-infused systems**: Products integrating artificial intelligence into everyday use
- **User-centered design**: Design approach prioritizing user needs and experiences
- **UX assessment method**: Systematic evaluation framework tailored to AI product interactions
- **Interaction quality**: Measure of how meaningfully users engage with dynamic AI systems
- **Dynamic and evolving systems**: Technology that adapts and changes based on user input and learning

## Theoretical Framework
The authors adopt a human-centered design lens grounded in user experience research, rejecting the technology-first perspective that has dominated AI development. This approach emphasizes understanding what users value and how they experience AI-infused products in practice.

## Methods
The study emerges from the Meet-AI research project (funded by Politecnico di Milano's Department of Design) involving eight design experts who systematically examined design, usability, and interaction challenges in AI products to develop and validate a new UX assessment method.

## Main Arguments
- Conventional UX tools and methods are insufficient for evaluating AI-infused systems due to their dynamic and evolving nature
- There exists a significant gap between AI technological sophistication and actual user experience quality
- A comprehensive, user-centered evaluation method is necessary to guide AI product design
- Understanding user values and meaningful engagement should take precedence in AI system development
