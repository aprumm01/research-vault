# iJET | Vol. 20 No. 2 (2025) International Journal of Emerging Technologies in...

## Summary
This 2025 paper in the International Journal of Emerging Technologies in Learning examines art education in the era of artificial intelligence, investigating how generative AI tools are reshaping art education practice, student creativity development, and the role of the art teacher. The study examines art educators' and students' experiences with AI image generation and other AI creative tools, documenting both opportunities (democratizing visual creation, expanding creative exploration) and challenges (questions of originality, artistic identity, and skill development). The paper proposes pedagogical frameworks for integrating AI into art education that preserve the developmental value of creative practice.

## Key Concepts
- AI in art education (iJET 2025)
- Generative AI and visual creativity development
- Art teacher role in AI-augmented education
- Artistic identity and AI-generated images
- Pedagogy for AI-integrated art education

## Theoretical Framework
Art education theory; creative cognition; constructivist art pedagogy; draws on art education research and AI creativity literature to frame AI's impact on artistic development.

## Methods
Empirical study of art education contexts integrating AI tools; student and teacher surveys and interviews; qualitative analysis of AI-mediated art creation and learning.

## Main Arguments
- AI image generation tools democratize visual creation but risk bypassing the skill development and creative struggle central to art education
- Art teachers report mixed experiences: AI expands what students can explore but creates challenges for assessing genuine creative development
- Artistic identity questions become acute when students produce striking AI images without corresponding skill development
- Art education needs pedagogical frameworks that use AI as a reflective tool for understanding visual principles rather than a replacement for hands-on making

## Limitations & Critiques
Single journal context; the specific AI tools examined are rapidly evolving; art education has particular disciplinary concerns about craft and originality that may not transfer to other creative education contexts.
