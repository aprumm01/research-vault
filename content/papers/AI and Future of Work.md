# AI and Future of Work


## Summary
AI and Future of Work is a thematic node that clusters research examining how artificial intelligence -- particularly generative AI -- is reshaping employment, professional roles, skill demands, and labor market structures. In this research vault, the node anchors a community of papers addressing job displacement risks, reskilling demands, the emergence of new AI-managerial labor, worker-led task crafting in response to GenAI, and the uneven distribution of AI's impacts across occupations, genders, and skill levels. The corpus spans macro-level labor economics perspectives, qualitative practitioner studies, and critical examinations of who benefits from and who bears the costs of AI-driven work transformation.

## Key Concepts
- Job displacement vs. job transformation vs. job creation under AI
- AI managerial labor: new work of monitoring and refining AI outputs
- Job crafting in response to GenAI integration (Law and Varanasi)
- Uneven impacts: experienced vs. junior workers, feminized labor categories
- Reskilling and upskilling demands for AI-augmented workplaces
- Complementarity vs. substitution: AI as tool vs. AI as replacement

## Theoretical Framework
Labor economics; occupational sociology; HCI work practice research; job crafting theory (Wrzesniewski, Dutton, Zhang); critical AI studies; situated in this vault within intersecting communities examining GenAI in UX and design practice, responsible AI ethics, and job displacement and reskilling debates.
