## Summary
Herring & Scheidt (2004) analyze weblogs as a genre of computer-mediated communication, examining the structural and interactional properties of blog posts and comments. Using content analysis of a sample of blogs, the study describes genre conventions, linking patterns, and social interactivity. The paper contributes to early empirical characterization of the blogosphere as a communicative environment.

## Theoretical Framework
Computer-mediated discourse analysis (CMDA); genre analysis of CMC.

## Methods
Content analysis; computer-mediated communication analysis; coding scheme; systematic sampling of web content
