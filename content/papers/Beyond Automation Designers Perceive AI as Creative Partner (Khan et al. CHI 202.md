# Beyond Automation: Designers Perceive AI as Creative Partner (Khan et al. CHI 2025)

**DOI:** [10.1145/3706598.3713500](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713500)

**Year:** 2025
**Venue:** CHI 2025
**Institution:** Polytechnique Montreal

## Key Finding
Designers view AI as creative partner in divergent thinking stages of UI/UX design

## Connections

## Summary
Khan et al. investigate how UI/UX designers perceive generative AI in the divergent thinking stages of design, finding that designers have moved beyond viewing AI as merely a productivity tool and increasingly perceive it as a creative partner that extends their creative possibilities. The paper documents how professional designers are conceptually and practically repositioning AI in their creative process, with implications for tool design, education, and professional identity. Published at CHI 2025, it captures an emerging shift in professional design culture around GenAI.

## Key Concepts
- AI as creative partner (beyond tool/automation framing)
- Divergent thinking in UI/UX design
- Designer-AI creative collaboration
- Professional perception shift toward AI

## Theoretical Framework
Design cognition and human-AI interaction frameworks; studies how designers' mental models of AI's role in creative work are changing as GenAI tools become more capable and integrated into professional practice.

## Methods
Empirical qualitative study with professional UI/UX designers; likely interviews or design sessions examining perceptions of AI during divergent thinking phases; published at CHI 2025.

## Main Arguments
- Designers are conceptualizing AI as a creative partner, not just an automation tool
- This perceptual shift is occurring specifically in divergent thinking contexts where creativity is central
- The "AI as creative partner" framing changes how designers engage with and evaluate AI outputs
- Design education and tool development should account for this partnership model, not just utility framing
