# The Application of Generative AI in Design-Based Elementary Education

**DOI:** [10.32431/kace.2026.29.1.011](https://doi.org/10.32431/kace.2026.29.1.011)

## Summary
This paper examines the application of generative AI in design-based elementary education, investigating how young students can engage with AI as a design tool and what pedagogical frameworks support meaningful AI-integrated design learning at the K-6 level. The research documents teacher strategies, student engagement patterns, and learning outcomes when elementary students use age-appropriate AI design tools in project-based contexts. The paper argues that early AI design education can build both technological literacy and design thinking skills simultaneously.

## Key Concepts
- Generative AI in elementary/K-6 design education
- Design-based learning for young children
- AI literacy development in early education
- Age-appropriate AI design tools
- Teacher preparation for AI-integrated design instruction

## Theoretical Framework
Design-based learning; constructionist pedagogy (Papert); AI literacy frameworks; draws on child development and early childhood education technology research.

## Methods
Classroom-based research study; qualitative and quantitative data from elementary design courses using AI tools; teacher interviews and classroom observation; student work artifact analysis.

## Main Arguments
- Elementary students can engage meaningfully with AI design tools when appropriately scaffolded
- Design-based contexts provide authentic purposes for AI use that make learning more motivating and meaningful
- Early AI design education builds both technical AI literacy and transferable design thinking skills
- Teachers need specific professional development to facilitate AI-integrated design learning at the elementary level

## Limitations & Critiques
Limited to specific age groups and tool contexts; the rapid evolution of AI tools may quickly date specific findings; scale effects and equity implications of AI in elementary design education require further investigation.
