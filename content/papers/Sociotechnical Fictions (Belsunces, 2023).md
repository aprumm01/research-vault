# Sociotechnical Fictions: The Performative Agencies of Fiction in Technological Development (Belsunces, 2023)

## Summary
Coins and develops the notion of 'sociotechnical fiction' — a type of fiction distinct from literary or cinematic forms that operates within the technosciences to materialize non-existent entities through the production of new technological assemblages. Drawing on actor-network theory and a performative approach, the paper argues that sociotechnical fictions are deeply entangled with rational and instrumental practices, connecting the anticipatory dimension of technology with its material legitimation.

## Key Concepts
- **Sociotechnical fiction**: Imagination operating inside technoscience to materialize imagined entities through assemblage-building
- **Performative agency of fiction**: How fictions do things — recruiting allies, mobilizing resources, building futures — not just describe them
- **Actor-network theory (ANT)**: Framework used to trace how fictions enroll human and nonhuman actors in technological projects

## Main Arguments
1. Sociotechnical fiction is distinct from science fiction — it is embedded in the technosciences and operates with material stakes
2. Fictions in technological development have performative agency: they recruit allies and resources, not just imagination
3. The concept of sociotechnical fiction extends and sharpens the imaginaries literature by focusing on enactment
