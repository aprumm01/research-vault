# Integrating user experience in user interface design education a problem-based learning approach enhanced by generative ai-supported design

## Summary
(PBL) approach augmented by generative artiﬁcial intelligence (AI) technologies The PBL framework emphasizes addressing real-world challenges, fostering self-directed learning and collaboration among interdisciplinary student teams Generative AI tools were employed to enhance creativity, streamline design processes, and provide immediate feedback, thereby reducing development time and facilitating iterative problem-solving.

## Key Concepts
- **Problem-Based Learning (PBL)** approach for UI/UX design education
- **Generative AI integration** to enhance PBL framework in design education
- Real-world challenges as foundation for learning activities
- Self-directed learning and interdisciplinary collaboration among student teams
- AI tools for enhancing creativity and streamlining design processes
- Immediate feedback through AI reducing development time
- Iterative problem-solving facilitated by AI tools

## Theoretical Framework
- **Problem-Based Learning (PBL)**: Student-centered pedagogy emphasizing authentic problems
- **Constructivist learning theory**: Knowledge construction through active engagement
- UX/UI design education frameworks
- Technology-enhanced learning principles

## Methods
- Implementation of PBL framework augmented with generative AI technologies
- Real-world design challenges as learning contexts
- Assessment of student collaboration and design outcomes
- Evaluation of AI tool impact on creative process and efficiency

## Main Arguments
- PBL framework effectively integrates UX principles into UI design education
- Generative AI tools enhance creativity and streamline design processes in educational settings
- Real-world challenges foster self-directed learning and interdisciplinary collaboration
- AI provides immediate feedback reducing development time
- Technology augmentation supports iterative problem-solving in design education
- Combination of PBL and AI tools prepares students for contemporary design practice
- AI-enhanced PBL addresses both pedagogical and practical design skills

## Limitations & Critiques
- Need to assess long-term impact on student learning outcomes
- Potential over-reliance on AI tools affecting development of foundational skills
- Scalability of PBL approach with AI integration across different contexts
- Balance between AI assistance and independent design thinking
- Evaluation criteria for AI-augmented design education outcomes
- Generalizability to different educational settings and student populations
