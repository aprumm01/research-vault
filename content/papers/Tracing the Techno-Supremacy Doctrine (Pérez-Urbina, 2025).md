# Tracing the Techno-Supremacy Doctrine (Pérez-Urbina, 2025)

## Summary
Critical discourse analysis of 14 texts by the 'AI executive elite'—highly influential individuals shaping how AI is funded, developed, and deployed worldwide—tracing the 'Techno-Supremacy Doctrine' (TSD): a belief system characterized by excessive trust in technology's inherent superiority for solving complex societal problems. Uses a two-phase qualitative and quantitative methodology to show TSD as a consistent discursive framework operating across AI leadership regardless of company or national context.

## Key Concepts
- **Techno-Supremacy Doctrine (TSD)**: A belief system asserting that technology is inherently superior to human judgment in solving societal problems
- **AI executive elite**: A small group of highly influential executives who control AI funding, development, and public narrative
- **Critical discourse analysis**: Methodology for revealing power relations and ideological assumptions in text

## Main Arguments
1. AI executives share a consistent discursive framework (TSD) that positions technology as the primary solution to societal problems
2. TSD depoliticizes AI by framing social challenges as technical problems with technical solutions
3. This discourse concentrates power in the hands of a small technical elite under the guise of universal benefit
