# Studio Pedagogy


## Summary
Studio Pedagogy is a thematic node capturing the distinctive teaching and learning culture of design studios -- the educational model in which students develop design competence through iterative making, critique, and dialogue in a shared studio environment under faculty mentorship. Originating in Beaux-Arts architecture education and adapted across design disciplines, studio pedagogy emphasizes learning by doing, peer critique as formative assessment, the role of the tutor as reflective interlocutor rather than lecturer, and the studio as a community of practice. In this research vault, studio pedagogy frames debates about how AI tools change design education: does AI enhance or undermine the productive struggle, critical dialogue, and identity formation that make studio learning effective?

## Key Concepts
- Critique (the "crit") as the primary pedagogical event in studio education
- Learning through making: iterative design, prototyping, and reflection
- The studio as community of practice and professional socialization site
- Tutor as reflective coach: Schon's reflective practicum
- Design judgment as the goal of studio pedagogy
- AI disruption of studio pedagogy: scaffolding vs. bypassing productive struggle

## Theoretical Framework
