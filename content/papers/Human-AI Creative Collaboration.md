# Human-AI Creative Collaboration


## Summary
Human-AI Creative Collaboration is a thematic node examining how humans and AI systems work together in creative tasks -- including design, writing, music, and visual art -- and what productive collaboration looks like when human creativity, judgment, and intentionality interact with AI's generative capabilities. In this research vault, the node anchors inquiry into how UX designers, interaction designers, and design students experience and negotiate AI as a creative partner: when does AI enhance creative output? When does it constrain or replace human creative agency? Papers in the corpus examine the mechanics of creative collaboration, the role of prompting and intention-setting, and the implications for creative ownership, attribution, and professional value.

## Key Concepts
- Co-creativity: models of human-AI creative partnership
- Initiative and control: who drives the creative direction in human-AI collaboration
- Prompt engineering as a creative skill: framing and directing AI output
- Creative ownership and attribution in AI-assisted work
- Serendipity and surprise: AI as a source of unexpected creative directions
- Guardrails and filtering: maintaining human judgment over AI-generated content

## Theoretical Framework
Creativity research (Csikszentmihalyi, Amabile); design cognition; HCI research on mixed-initiative systems and intelligent creative tools; connected in this vault to GenAI in UX and Design Practice, Cognitive Offloading and De-skilling, Prompt Engineering, and AI in Design Education communities.
