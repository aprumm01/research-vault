# AI Industry Expectations and Underperforming Imaginaries (Stanusch et al., 2024)

## Summary
Reports a multi-institutional collaborative study of AI industry imaginaries across academia, platform companies, and civil society, arguing that dominant AI narratives function as 'underperforming imaginaries'—visions that reshape institutional practice and resource allocation even when they fail to materialize technically. Drawing on comparative content analysis of industry discourse and expert interviews, the paper maps how expectation cycles are sustained through repeated forecasting, reframing, and institutional investment in unrealized futures.

## Key Concepts
- **Underperforming imaginaries**: AI transformation narratives that restructure institutions and labor even when technical predictions fail
- **Expectation cycles**: Recurring patterns of AI hype, partial delivery, and reframing that sustain imaginary momentum
- **Industry discourse analysis**: Comparative study of how different institutional sectors construct AI futures

## Main Arguments
1. AI industry imaginaries are underperforming in technical terms but overperforming in institutional impact
2. Expectation cycles sustain imaginary momentum even through repeated failures to deliver
3. The gap between imaginary and performance is itself generative—producing new rounds of investment and narrative
