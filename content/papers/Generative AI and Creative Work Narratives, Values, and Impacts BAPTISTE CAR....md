# Generative AI and Creative Work: Narratives, Values, and Impacts (Caramiaux et al., Microsoft Research, 2023)


## Abstract
*Abstract not yet found — search by title or check source PDF*

## Summary
Caramiaux and colleagues at Microsoft Research and partner institutions present a qualitative study of how creative professionals across disciplines (visual artists, musicians, writers, designers) narrate their experiences with generative AI, examining the values at stake in creative AI adoption and the impacts practitioners report on their creative work. The study surfaces three dominant narrative types: generative AI as tool (instrumental), as collaborator (relational), and as threat (existential) — and documents how these narratives correlate with different value commitments and adoption strategies. The paper argues for a values-centered account of creative AI adoption.

## Key Concepts
- Creative professional AI narratives (tool / collaborator / threat)
- Values in creative AI adoption
- Generative AI impacts on creative practice
- Human-AI creative relationship types
- Creative identity and AI

## Theoretical Framework
Values in design (Friedman & Hendry); creative cognition; sociotechnical systems; qualitative HCI methodology; the paper draws on values-centered design and creative practice research traditions.

## Methods
Semi-structured interviews with 30+ creative professionals across multiple disciplines (visual art, music, writing, game design, graphic design); thematic analysis of narrative types and value structures.

## Main Arguments
- Creative professionals construct coherent narratives about AI that reflect their professional values and shape their adoption strategies
- The tool narrative treats AI as an accelerant for execution; the collaborator narrative treats it as a creative partner with its own aesthetic contributions; the threat narrative foregrounds concerns about livelihood and creative authenticity
- Practitioners' values (originality, craft, human connection, economic security) are the strongest predictors of which narrative they adopt
- Design of AI tools for creative professionals should be informed by the diversity of value commitments, not just productivity metrics

## Limitations & Critiques
The three-narrative typology may oversimplify a complex landscape; Microsoft Research institutional context may influence findings; the study captures a specific moment in creative AI adoption that may have shifted.
