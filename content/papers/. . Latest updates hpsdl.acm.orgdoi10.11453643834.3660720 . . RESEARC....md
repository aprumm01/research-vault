## Summary
This 2024-2025 conference paper examines how generative AI is being adopted by UX practitioners and teams within companies, documenting GenAI integration at individual, team, and organizational levels. The study finds that organizational context and team culture significantly mediate whether GenAI integration enhances or disrupts UX workflows, and that companies with deliberate GenAI strategies produce better outcomes than those with ad hoc adoption.

## Key Concepts
- Generative AI in UX practice (individual, team, organizational)
- Practitioner-level AI adoption in design research
- Organizational culture and AI integration
- Team-level AI workflow coordination

## Theoretical Framework
Technology adoption; organizational behavior; human-AI collaboration; multi-level analysis.

## Methods
Practitioner interviews and survey study across multiple companies; multi-level analysis of adoption patterns.

## Main Arguments
- GenAI adoption in UX is shaped by organizational context as much as individual attitudes
- Team coordination around GenAI requires explicit norm-setting
- Companies with deliberate GenAI strategies show better UX outcomes
- UX tool designers must account for team and organizational use dimensions

## Limitations & Critiques
Multi-level study requires large samples; organizational context is highly variable; captures a snapshot of a rapidly evolving adoption process.
