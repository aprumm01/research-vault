## Summary
Longitudinal case study spanning Spring 2023–Spring 2024 in introductory UI/UX web design courses at Lindenwood University, finding that integrating text-based and image-based AI tools significantly enhanced student creative capabilities and project quality. Reveals that student resistance to AI is lower for tasks outside their primary major, with CS students accepting AI for design tasks more readily than for coding.

## Key Concepts
- AI adoption in education
- Student motivation and resistance
- UI/UX design education
- Text-based vs. image-based AI tools
- Workflow integration

## Theoretical Framework
Technology adoption lens applied to educational settings; creative process in design education; case study methodology tracking behavioral change over multiple semesters.

## Methods
Longitudinal case study across three semesters (Spring 2023–Spring 2024); survey data on AI tool adoption and usage; analysis of project outcomes; separate analysis by student major and background.

## Main Arguments
- Text-based AI generators (ChatGPT) markedly improve writing efficiency and coding quality
- Image-based AI tools facilitate better ideation and color selection
- Students more receptive to AI for tasks outside their primary major—CS students resist AI coding assistance but welcome AI for design tasks
- AI tool awareness and usage grew significantly across all students over the study period

## Limitations & Critiques
Single-institution case study without a control group; cannot establish causal relationships; reflects a specific snapshot in AI tool availability (Stable Diffusion, Lensa.ai era) that has already evolved; findings may not generalize across disciplines or institutional types.
