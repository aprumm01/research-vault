## Summary
Van Tiem et al. provide a foundational definition and conceptual overview of performance technology as a field, articulating its core principles, distinguishing features, and relationship to adjacent disciplines like training, organizational development, and management. The work situates performance technology within a systematic, results-oriented tradition and outlines the competencies, methods, and values that define professional practice in the field.

## Key Concepts
- Performance technology definition
- Human Performance Technology (HPT)
- Performance improvement
- Competency framework
- Organizational performance

## Theoretical Framework
Systems thinking and performance-based learning theory ground the definition of performance technology as a field concerned with analyzing causes of performance gaps and designing comprehensive interventions to close them.

## Methods
Conceptual framework and definitional analysis; synthesis of field perspectives on performance technology's identity, boundaries, and core principles.

## Main Arguments
- Performance technology is defined by its focus on results and systematic diagnosis of performance causes
- The field is distinguished from training by its broader scope: performance improvement includes environmental, motivational, and organizational factors
- HPT practitioners must develop competencies across analysis, design, implementation, evaluation, and change management
- Performance technology serves as a bridge between learning theory and organizational effectiveness
