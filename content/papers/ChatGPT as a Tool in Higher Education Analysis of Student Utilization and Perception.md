# ChatGPT as a Tool in Higher Education: Analysis of Student Utilization and Perception

## Summary
Surveys 260 management students at the University of Gdańsk to explore how they use ChatGPT for academic purposes, finding widespread adoption for information retrieval, writing assistance, and idea refinement. Students primarily value efficiency but raise concerns about content reliability and potential negative effects on creativity and critical thinking.
