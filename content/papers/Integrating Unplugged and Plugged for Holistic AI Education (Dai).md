## Summary
This longitudinal study by Lively and Hutson at Lindenwood University examines how student motivation shapes outcomes when AI tools are integrated into web design and UX education across five semesters (Spring 2023 to Spring 2024). The study finds that text-based AI tools significantly improved writing efficiency and coding, while image-based tools enhanced ideation and color selection. Importantly, student motivation levels moderated the degree to which AI tools augmented creative capabilities.

## Theoretical Framework
Self-determination theory and technology-enhanced learning frameworks inform the analysis of how intrinsic and extrinsic motivation interact with AI tool use to shape student learning outcomes.

## Methods
Longitudinal case study; Spring 2023 to Spring 2024 across introductory web design and UX courses; observation of student work and outcomes with AI tools.

## Main Arguments
- Student motivation is a key mediating variable in AI-enhanced design education
- Text-based AI tools improve writing and coding efficiency across motivation levels
- Image-based AI tools particularly benefit students in ideation and visual exploration
- AI integration must be paired with motivational support to achieve its potential in design education
