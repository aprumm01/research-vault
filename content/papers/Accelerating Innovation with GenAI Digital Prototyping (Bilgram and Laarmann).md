# Accelerating Innovation with GenAI: Digital Prototyping (Bilgram and Laarmann)

## Summary
Bilgram and Laarmann report on six months of intensive experimentation with LLMs (especially GPT) in commercial innovation projects, demonstrating concrete use cases across the early innovation phases: exploration (need identification, trend analysis), ideation (idea generation, concept development), and digital prototyping (text-to-code generation for UX/UI). They argue that generative AI is democratizing prototyping by enabling non-technical team members to generate functional digital prototypes through natural language, collapsing the gap between concept and implementation in early innovation stages.

## Key Concepts
- AI-augmented innovation management
- LLM-enabled digital prototyping (text-to-code)
- Democratization of prototyping
- Early innovation phases (exploration, ideation, prototyping)
- No-code/low-code AI-assisted development

## Theoretical Framework
Practitioner-oriented; draws on innovation management literature and design thinking methodology; positions LLMs as general-purpose technologies applicable across the innovation front-end without requiring specialized technical expertise.

## Methods
Practitioner report; six months of experimental application of LLMs in internal and client-facing innovation projects; concrete use case documentation; reflection on team dynamics and AI-human interaction patterns.

## Main Arguments
- LLMs have crossed a threshold where they provide genuine value in creative and generative innovation tasks — previously thought to be human-only domains
- Text-to-code generation is the most transformative capability for non-technical innovators: it enables functional prototype creation without software skills, accelerating iteration dramatically
- Human-AI innovation teams require deliberate design: prompting, iteration, and quality evaluation are new skills that innovation teams must develop
- LLMs excel at divergent generation but require human judgment for convergent selection and quality assessment — the human role shifts from generation to curation and direction

## Limitations
- Based on a small innovation consultancy's experience; generalizability to larger organizations with different constraints is uncertain
- Published in 2023 based on GPT-3/4-era tools; capabilities have advanced significantly since, making specific claims about limitations or capabilities partially dated
