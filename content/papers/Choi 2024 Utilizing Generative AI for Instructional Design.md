# Choi 2024 Utilizing Generative AI for Instructional Design: SWOT Analysis


## Abstract
Abstract Recently, generative AI has been at the center of disruptive innovation in various settings, including educational sectors. This article investigates ChatGPT, which is one of the most prominent generative AI in the market, to explore its usefulness and potential for instructional design. Four researchers used a set of prompts to generate a course map for an online course that is aimed to teach the topic of makerspace and conducted SWOT analysis to identify strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats of using generative AI for instructional design. The findings suggest that there is promise in using ChatGPT as an efficient and effective tool for creating course maps, yet it still requires the domain knowledge and instructional design expertise to warrant quality and reliability of the tool.

## Summary
This paper presents a SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis of generative AI tools in instructional design practice. Published in TechTrends (2024), it examines how instructional designers can leverage GenAI while navigating its limitations and risks.

## Key Concepts
- **Generative AI in ID practice**: How tools like ChatGPT can assist at various stages of the instructional design process (analysis, design, development, implementation, evaluation)
- **SWOT framework**: Structured evaluation of GenAI — strengths (efficiency, ideation support), weaknesses (accuracy, bias), opportunities (personalization, scalability), threats (over-reliance, ethical issues)
- **ADDIE integration**: Examining where GenAI fits within the traditional ADDIE model stages

## Theoretical Framework
Grounded in instructional design theory and the ADDIE model; uses strategic management's SWOT analysis as an evaluative lens applied to emerging technology adoption in professional practice.

## Methods
Conceptual/analytical paper applying the SWOT framework to synthesize existing literature and practitioner knowledge about generative AI capabilities and limitations in instructional design contexts.

## Main Arguments
1. GenAI offers genuine productivity gains for instructional designers, particularly in content drafting, brainstorming, and rapid prototyping of learning materials
2. Significant weaknesses exist around factual accuracy, hallucination, and the need for subject-matter expert review — GenAI cannot replace ID expertise
3. The greatest opportunity lies in personalizing learning experiences at scale, which was previously impractical
4. The primary threat is uncritical over-reliance that erodes core instructional design competencies and introduces unreviewed errors into learning materials
