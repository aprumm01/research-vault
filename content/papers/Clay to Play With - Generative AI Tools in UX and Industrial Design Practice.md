## Summary
This paper explores industrial and UX designers' use of generative AI tools through the metaphor of "clay to play with" — positioning AI as a malleable, generative material that enables exploratory, low-stakes design experimentation. Through design research with practitioners in product and experience design contexts, the authors find that successful AI integration occurs when designers approach tools playfully and experimentally rather than instrumentally, treating AI outputs as starting points for design exploration rather than finished products. The paper argues for a materials-centered approach to understanding AI in design practice.

## Key Concepts
- AI as design material (not just a productivity tool)
- Playful and exploratory AI use
- Material encounters in design practice
- Generative AI in industrial design
- Experimentation and serendipity with AI

## Theoretical Framework
Material-centered design practice theory; draws on Ingold's material agency, Schön's reflective practice, and design studio research to frame AI as a new design material rather than a software tool.

## Methods
Design research with UX and industrial design practitioners; qualitative study combining design workshops, observation, and reflective interviews about AI material practice.

## Main Arguments
- Treating AI as a design material — something to manipulate, resist, and explore — produces richer outcomes than treating it as a productivity tool
- Playful experimentation with AI generates serendipitous discoveries that instrumentally-minded use misses
- The "clay" metaphor captures both the formability of AI outputs and the skill required to shape them toward design intent
- Design education should develop AI material literacy: understanding what this material can and cannot do, and how to develop craft with it

## Limitations & Critiques
The materials metaphor is productive but may not scale to all design contexts; the study's practitioner sample may represent early adopters; empirical evidence for learning outcomes from materials-based AI pedagogy is not yet established.
