# User Experience Design Professionals' Perceptions of Generative Artificial Intelligence

## Summary
Interviews 20 UX designers across companies from startups to large enterprises to understand how GenAI impacts UX design practice, finding that experienced designers are confident in their originality and empathic skills and see GenAI as assistive. However, junior designers face heightened risks of skill degradation, job replacement, and creativity exhaustion, and broader issues of copyright, AI literacy access, and human agency remain unresolved.
