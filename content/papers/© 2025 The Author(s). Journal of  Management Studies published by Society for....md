# Rethinking How We Theorize AI in Organization and Management (Ramaul et al., Journal of Management Studies, 2025)

## Summary
Ramaul et al.'s 2025 Journal of Management Studies paper calls for a fundamental rethinking of how AI is theorized in organization and management research, arguing that dominant theoretical frameworks (tool view, actor view, sociotechnical view) each capture important but partial aspects of AI's organizational significance. The paper proposes an integrative "relational constitutive" framework that treats AI as constitutively entangled with organizational processes rather than as a distinct entity or tool acting on pre-existing structures. The work is a significant theoretical contribution to how management scholars conceptualize AI's organizational role.

## Key Concepts
- AI theorization in organizational and management research
- Tool view, actor view, and sociotechnical view of AI
- Relational constitutive framework for organizational AI
- Entanglement of AI and organizational processes
- Ontological assumptions in AI management theory

## Theoretical Framework
Organizational theory; philosophy of technology; draws on Orlikowski's sociomateriality, Latour's actor-network theory, and process philosophy to develop the relational constitutive framework.

## Methods
Theoretical analysis and framework development; critical review of existing AI theorization in management literature; demonstration application of the relational constitutive framework.

## Main Arguments
- Existing management theories treat AI as either a tool (passive instrument), actor (autonomous agent), or sociotechnical element (embedded system), each missing dimensions the others capture
- AI in organizations is neither a fixed entity nor a transparent tool but a relational constitutive force that shapes and is shaped by organizational processes
- The relational constitutive framework allows management researchers to analyze how AI changes organizational practices, structures, and identities without reducing AI to either tool or actor
- Empirical management research on AI needs theoretical pluralism that can handle AI's ontological novelty

## Limitations & Critiques
High level of theoretical abstraction may limit practical applicability; the relational constitutive framework's implications for empirical research design need elaboration; the paper's philosophical commitments may be contested by more positivist management researchers.
