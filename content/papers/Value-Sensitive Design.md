# Value-Sensitive Design

## Summary
Umbrello and van de Poel argue that value sensitive design (VSD) requires significant modification to address the unique ethical challenges of machine learning-based AI systems, particularly the opacity of learning processes and the risk that embedded values become "disembodied" as AI adapts over time. They propose a threefold modified VSD approach: using AI for Social Good (AI4SG) principles as design norms, distinguishing between values that are promoted versus merely respected, and extending the VSD process across the full AI lifecycle. A SARS-CoV-2 contact tracing app serves as an illustrative use case.

## Key Concepts
- Value sensitive design (VSD)
- AI for Social Good (AI4SG) principles
- Value disembodiment in machine learning
- Lifecycle-spanning design ethics
- Transparency and accountability in AI

## Theoretical Framework
The paper extends Friedman and colleagues' value sensitive design methodology, which calls for the systematic investigation of human values in technology design. It integrates AI4SG principles as domain-specific design norms and draws on philosophy of technology to address the challenge of values that drift or become opaque as ML systems learn.

## Methods
Conceptual and normative paper. The authors analyze existing VSD literature, identify challenges specific to ML-based AI, synthesize AI4SG principles, and develop a modified design framework. A worked example using a COVID-19 contact tracing app illustrates how the modified approach operates in practice.

## Main Arguments
- Standard VSD is insufficient for AI because ML systems can acquire unintended behaviors that are opaque to designers and users
- AI4SG principles provide actionable design norms that can anchor VSD work in the AI context
- Distinguishing between values the design promotes (active good) and values it merely respects (avoiding harm) enables more nuanced ethical evaluation
- VSD must extend beyond the design phase to encompass monitoring and redesign throughout an AI system's operational lifecycle

## Limitations & Critiques
The paper is primarily theoretical and the illustrative use case is relatively brief, leaving the practical operationalization of the modified VSD approach underspecified. The AI4SG principles themselves are acknowledged to be somewhat fuzzy and contested, which complicates their use as stable design norms.
