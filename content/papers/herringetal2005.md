## Main Arguments
- Claim that linking to other blogs constitutes conversation is not self-evident and merits critical scrutiny
- Conversational nature of blogging requires empirical investigation rather than assumption
- Technical practice of linking may differ from genuine conversational interaction
- Weblog genre definition includes requirement to link to and reference source material
- Blogging practices need systematic analysis to understand their communicative nature
- Distinction between linking as citation versus linking as conversation

## Limitations & Critiques
- Need for empirical data to support or refute conversational claims about blogging
- Challenge of defining what constitutes "conversation" in asynchronous hyperlinked medium
- Variability in blogging practices across different blog types and communities
- Evolving nature of blogging as genre may affect generalizability of findings
