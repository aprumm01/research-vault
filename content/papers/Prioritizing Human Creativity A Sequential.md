# Prioritizing Human Creativity: A Sequential

## Summary
This paper proposes and evaluates a sequential pedagogical approach to teaching generative AI in graphic design education that explicitly prioritizes human creativity before introducing AI tools. The approach sequences instruction so students develop foundational design skills, creative processes, and critical aesthetic judgment before engaging with AI tools, countering the risk that early AI exposure atrophies creative development. The paper documents outcomes from courses using this sequence and argues that AI integration in design education must be pedagogically intentional about skill development sequencing.

## Key Concepts
- Sequential AI introduction pedagogy in graphic design
- Human creativity prioritization before AI use
- Creative skill development sequencing
- Foundational design skills and AI literacy integration
- Pedagogical sequencing for AI-augmented design education

## Theoretical Framework
Design pedagogy; skill development theory; constructivist learning; draws on design education research and cognitive skill acquisition literature to justify sequential rather than concurrent AI integration.

## Methods
Pedagogical intervention study; comparison of student outcomes in sequentially-ordered AI integration vs. simultaneous introduction; qualitative analysis of student work and reflections.

## Main Arguments
- Students who develop foundational creative skills before AI exposure show stronger critical AI use and more distinctive creative voices than those introduced to AI simultaneously
- Sequential introduction allows students to build a reference point for what their un-augmented creative process produces, making AI assistance more transparent and controllable
- Graphic design education must be explicit about sequencing AI introduction to prevent premature cognitive offloading
- The sequential approach preserves the creative struggle that produces deep design learning while eventually incorporating AI as an additional creative resource

## Limitations & Critiques
Single institutional study; the optimal sequencing timing (how long before AI introduction?) is not fully established; may be less applicable to students entering with prior AI tool experience.
