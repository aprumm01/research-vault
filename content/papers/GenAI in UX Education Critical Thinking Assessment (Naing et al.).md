# GenAI in UX Education: Critical Thinking Assessment (Naing et al.)

## Summary
Naing et al. investigate how generative AI integration in UX education contexts affects students' development of critical thinking competencies. The study examines whether AI tool use scaffolds or undermines students' ability to evaluate design decisions, reason about user needs, and identify gaps in AI-generated outputs. Findings reveal that without structured critical thinking assessment frameworks, students tend to uncritically accept AI outputs, particularly in early design phases, while more structured reflection prompts can mitigate this tendency.

## Key Concepts
- Critical thinking in UX education
- GenAI in design studio courses
- Assessment of AI-mediated learning
- Scaffolded reflection for AI tool use
- UX design student competency development

## Theoretical Framework
Draws on critical thinking frameworks (Paul-Elder model), UX design pedagogy literature, and human-AI interaction research; positions critical thinking as a developmental competency that must be explicitly assessed and scaffolded in AI-integrated design curricula.

## Methods
Empirical study of UX design students using generative AI tools in coursework; assessment instruments measuring critical thinking dimensions (analysis, evaluation, synthesis) before and after structured reflection interventions; comparison across conditions.

## Main Arguments
- Generative AI tool use in UX education has a default tendency to suppress critical engagement — students readily adopt AI suggestions without systematic evaluation
- Structured reflection prompts and critical thinking rubrics can substantially improve students' ability to interrogate AI outputs and identify their limitations
- Assessment must evolve beyond artifact quality to include the reasoning process through which students evaluate and adapt AI-generated work
- Faculty who have not developed their own critical AI practices struggle to model or assess them effectively with students

## Limitations
- Study context is specific to UX design education; critical thinking dynamics may differ in other design disciplines or at different educational levels
- Short-term intervention; longitudinal tracking of critical thinking development in AI-integrated curricula is needed
