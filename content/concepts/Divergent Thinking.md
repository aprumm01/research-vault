# Divergent Thinking

## Definition
The generative phase of ideation involving exploring a wide range of ideas and solutions to drive innovation and creativity. This cognitive process is well-supported by most creativity support tools.

## Key Papers
```dataview
LIST
FROM "papers"
WHERE contains(builds_on, this.file.link) OR contains(supports, this.file.link) OR contains(critiques, this.file.link)
SORT file.name ASC
```

## Tensions
- **Design Fixation**: Fixation on initial ideas constrains divergent exploration
- **Visual Homogenization**: Standardized AI outputs can limit the diversity of generated ideas
