# Process-centric Education

## Definition
A pedagogical approach emphasizing the journey of research, iteration, and critical thinking over final artifacts, countering AI's tendency to prioritize polished outputs over learning processes.

## Tensions
- **Good First Draft Phenomenon**: Process-centric education is directly threatened when AI generates polished outputs that bypass the iterative learning process, allowing students to skip the valuable struggle of research, revision, and refinement.
