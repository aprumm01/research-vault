# Value System Rift

## Definition
A growing divide between experienced designers circumspect about loss of traditional creativity and junior designers enthusiastic about AI adoption, reflecting different values regarding craft and skill development.

## Tensions
- **AI Augmentation**: The augmentation narrative often assumes universal benefit, but the value system rift reveals that different practitioners evaluate AI's impact through incompatible value frameworks.
- **Democratization of Design**: While democratization is often framed positively, experienced designers may view it as devaluing hard-won expertise and traditional paths to professional competence.
