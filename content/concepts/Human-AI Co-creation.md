# Human-AI Co-creation

## Definition
A collaborative dynamic where humans and AI work together as active participants in creative processes, with humans maintaining creative judgment and final decision-making authority while AI contributes generative capabilities.

## Tensions
- **Cognitive Offloading**: Risk that reliance on AI generation may reduce human cognitive engagement in the creative process
- **Design Fixation**: AI suggestions may anchor thinking and limit exploration of alternative creative directions
- **Ownership Ambiguity**: Unclear attribution when creative outputs emerge from human-AI collaboration
