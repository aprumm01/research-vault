# Hybrid Intelligence

## Definition
The fusion of human and artificial intelligence leveraging complementary strengths—humans provide contextual understanding and ethical reasoning while AI offers computational scale and pattern recognition.

## Tensions
- **Cognitive Offloading**: Effective fusion requires calibrated effort allocation, risking over-delegation to AI
- **Metacognitive Laziness**: Seamless integration may reduce human monitoring of their own reasoning processes
