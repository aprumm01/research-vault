# Surface-Level Processing

## Definition
Engaging with AI-generated summaries rather than source texts, obtaining factual information without developing interpretive and critical capacities.

## Key Papers
```dataview
LIST
FROM "papers"
WHERE contains(builds_on, this.file.link) OR contains(supports, this.file.link) OR contains(critiques, this.file.link)
SORT file.name ASC
```

## Tensions
- **Deep Learning (Educational)**: Surface processing directly opposes the effortful, connection-making engagement characteristic of deep learning
- **Critical Thinking**: Accepting AI outputs without engaging source material prevents development of analytical and evaluative skills
