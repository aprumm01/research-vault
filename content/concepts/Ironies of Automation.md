# Ironies of Automation

## Definition
Paradoxical effects where automation intended to simplify work makes human roles more critical and complex, including the monitoring paradox and the substitution myth.

## Tensions
- **Automation Benefits**: While automation promises efficiency gains, the ironies reveal that human oversight often becomes more demanding, not less.
- **AI Efficiency Claims**: Claims about AI reducing workload often ignore the new forms of cognitive labor required to monitor and correct automated systems.
