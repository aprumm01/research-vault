# De-skilling

## Definition
The outcome where reliance on technological systems decreases existing skills or prevents skill development. Documented across aviation, healthcare, and creative work domains.

## Tensions
- **AI Augmentation**: The augmentation perspective assumes AI enhances human capability, but de-skilling suggests the opposite outcome where capabilities diminish through dependency.
- **Hybrid Intelligence**: Hybrid intelligence models presume complementary human-AI collaboration, yet de-skilling threatens the human contribution to such partnerships.
- **AI Literacy Dimensions**: Developing AI literacy could either mitigate de-skilling through informed use or accelerate it by normalizing delegation to AI systems.
