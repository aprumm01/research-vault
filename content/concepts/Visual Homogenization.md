# Visual Homogenization

## Definition
The phenomenon where AI-assisted designs cluster more tightly in visual feature space than manual designs, suggesting convergence toward AI's training distribution and reduced design diversity.

## Tensions
- **Divergent Thinking**: Visual homogenization directly opposes the goal of generating diverse, varied design alternatives that divergent thinking seeks to produce.
- **AI-driven Creativity**: While AI can spark creative ideas, the homogenization effect suggests systemic constraints on the diversity of creative outcomes achievable through AI collaboration.
