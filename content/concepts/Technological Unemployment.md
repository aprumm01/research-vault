# Technological Unemployment

## Definition
Job displacement resulting from AI replacing routine and intellectual tasks, affecting both manual and knowledge work as AI capabilities expand.

## Tensions
- **AI Augmentation**: The augmentation perspective suggests AI creates new roles rather than simply eliminating jobs.
- **Hybrid Intelligence**: Hybrid approaches envision human-AI collaboration that preserves meaningful work, contrasting with displacement narratives.
