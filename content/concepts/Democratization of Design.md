# Democratization of Design

## Definition
The lowering of barriers to prototyping through generative AI tools, enabling non-designers to participate more directly in design activities previously requiring trained expertise.

## Tensions
- **De-skilling**: While democratization opens design to broader participation, it may simultaneously erode the deep skills that professional designers have cultivated, creating tension between accessibility and expertise.
- **Value System Rift**: The democratization agenda can conflict with experienced designers who value traditional craft and skill development, contributing to generational divides in the field.
