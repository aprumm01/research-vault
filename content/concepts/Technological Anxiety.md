# Technological Anxiety

## Definition
Uneasiness when facing AI applications, arising from concerns about intellectual property, job displacement, and threats to core professional values.

## Tensions
- **AI Augmentation**: Anxiety often stems from framing AI as replacement rather than augmentation of human capabilities.
- **Hybrid Intelligence**: The hybrid intelligence paradigm offers an alternative to anxiety-inducing replacement narratives, but requires trust-building.
