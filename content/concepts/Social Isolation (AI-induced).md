# Social Isolation (AI-induced)

## Definition
Increasing feelings of isolation as the sense of community from collaborative struggle diminishes due to AI mediation, replacing shared experiences with individual AI consultation.

## Tensions
- **Cooperative Learning**: AI-induced isolation directly contradicts cooperative learning principles that emphasize community and shared experience as central to educational development.
- **Hybrid Intelligence**: While hybrid intelligence envisions productive human-AI partnerships, AI-induced isolation reveals potential social costs when AI replaces human collaboration.
