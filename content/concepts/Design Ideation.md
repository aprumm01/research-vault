# Design Ideation

## Definition
The early-stage creative process of generating design concepts, supported by AI through text-based prompting. Requires both divergent exploration and convergent refinement.

## Key Papers
```dataview
LIST
FROM "papers"
WHERE contains(builds_on, this.file.link) OR contains(supports, this.file.link) OR contains(critiques, this.file.link)
SORT file.name ASC
```

## Tensions
- **Design Fixation**: AI-generated examples during ideation can inadvertently anchor designers to particular solutions, constraining the creative exploration that ideation requires
