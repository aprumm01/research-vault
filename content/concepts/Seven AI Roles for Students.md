# Seven AI Roles for Students

## Definition
A pedagogical framework defining seven roles AI can play: tutor, coach, mentor, teammate, student, simulator, and tool—each serving different educational purposes.

No direct tensions identified. This framework provides a structured way to conceptualize AI's educational roles, offering guidance for appropriate AI integration rather than standing in opposition to other concepts.
