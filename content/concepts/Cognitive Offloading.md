# Cognitive Offloading

## Definition
The process of externalizing cognitive tasks to AI tools, reducing internal engagement with evaluation, monitoring, and decision-making processes. This delegation can impair critical thinking development when AI is treated as a cognitive substitute rather than supplement.

## Tensions
- **Deep Learning (Educational)**: Cognitive offloading may prevent the deep engagement necessary for meaningful learning
- **Critical Thinking**: Delegating analytical tasks to AI can atrophy critical thinking skills
- **Epistemic Agency**: Over-reliance on AI reduces active control over one's own belief formation
