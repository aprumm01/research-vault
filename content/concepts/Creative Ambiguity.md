# Creative Ambiguity

## Definition
Outputs that resist easy interpretation, offering incomplete or semantically open-ended cues that can catalyze reflection and creative reframing rather than representing failure.

## Key Papers
```dataview
LIST
FROM "papers"
WHERE contains(builds_on, this.file.link) OR contains(supports, this.file.link) OR contains(critiques, this.file.link)
SORT file.name ASC
```

## Tensions
- **Design Fixation**: While creative ambiguity opens interpretive space, design fixation closes it; ambiguous outputs can counter fixation by preventing premature commitment to specific solutions
