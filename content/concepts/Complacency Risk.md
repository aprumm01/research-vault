# Complacency Risk

## Definition
The tendency for students and practitioners to accept AI outputs uncritically, producing work that is technically adequate but personally under-engaged, lacking deep cognitive investment.

## Key Papers

## Tensions
- **Critical Thinking**: Complacency directly undermines critical thinking by encouraging acceptance rather than evaluation of AI-generated content.
- **Evaluative Judgment**: The development of evaluative judgment requires active engagement with quality assessment, which complacency bypasses through uncritical acceptance.
