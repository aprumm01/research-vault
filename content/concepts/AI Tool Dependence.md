# AI Tool Dependence

## Definition
A pattern where users habitually delegate cognitive tasks to AI, treating it as a cognitive substitute rather than a supplement, impairing independent thinking and skill development.

## Tensions
- **Critical Thinking**: Dependence on AI reduces opportunities for independent critical analysis
- **Epistemic Agency**: Over-reliance undermines learners' sense of ownership over their knowledge construction
- **Deep Learning (Educational)**: Habitual AI delegation promotes surface-level engagement rather than deep cognitive processing
