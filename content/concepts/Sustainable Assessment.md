# Sustainable Assessment

## Definition
Assessment that prepares students to engage in self-directed evaluation throughout their lives, requiring the capacity to judge AI outputs independently without external verification.

## Tensions
- **AI Tool Dependence**: Sustainable assessment aims to develop lifelong self-evaluation capacity, which is undermined when students become dependent on AI tools for judgment and verification rather than developing internal standards.
