# Epistemic Substitution

## Definition
When AI performs the cognitive operations that both reflect and develop professional expertise—distinct from cognitive offloading because the delegated operations are precisely those through which expertise develops.

## Tensions
- **Deep Learning (Educational)**: Substituting AI for expertise-building operations prevents deep learning
- **Epistemic Agency**: Delegating expertise-developing tasks undermines autonomous knowledge construction
