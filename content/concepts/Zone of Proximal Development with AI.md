# Zone of Proximal Development with AI

## Definition
Vygotsky's concept applied to AI-mediated learning, positioning AI as a 'more expert other' that helps students extend abilities through appropriate scaffolding.

## Tensions
- **AI Overreliance**: While AI can effectively scaffold learning within the ZPD, overreliance risks students becoming dependent on AI support rather than internalizing capabilities and eventually performing independently.
- **De-skilling**: The scaffolding benefits of AI-mediated ZPD must be balanced against risks that excessive AI assistance prevents students from developing skills they would otherwise acquire through productive struggle.
