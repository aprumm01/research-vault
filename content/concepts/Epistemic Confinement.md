# Epistemic Confinement

## Definition
A structural trap where students fully offload initial problem framing to AI and subsequently fail to critically evaluate this foundation, drawing conclusions constrained within an AI-constructed intellectual space.

## Tensions
- **Critical Thinking**: Accepting AI-constructed frames without critique undermines critical analysis
- **Epistemic Agency**: Being confined to AI-defined intellectual spaces reduces autonomous knowledge building
