# Reciprocal Learning Partnership

## Definition
Mutual information exchange between students and AI where both agents learn from each other's outputs, framing AI interaction as bidirectional knowledge construction.

## Tensions
- **AI Tool Dependence**: While reciprocal learning partnerships frame AI as a collaborative partner, this framing can inadvertently foster dependence if students come to rely on the partnership rather than developing independent capabilities.
