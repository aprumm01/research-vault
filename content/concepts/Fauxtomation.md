# Fauxtomation

## Definition
Human labor covering up for automation shortcomings while creating an illusion of machine autonomy—technologies framed as efficient but shown to increase workload.

## Tensions
- **Teacher Professional Autonomy**: Fauxtomation in education often shifts labor onto teachers while diminishing their professional judgment and autonomy.
