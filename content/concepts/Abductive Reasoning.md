# Abductive Reasoning

## Definition
Filling knowledge gaps using precedent and problem understanding, contrasting with scientific induction/deduction. This design thinking mode forms relationships across unrelated information and is central to creative problem-solving.

None identified. Abductive reasoning operates as a complementary mode of inference alongside induction and deduction rather than in opposition to other cognitive concepts.
