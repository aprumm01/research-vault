# Sociology of Expectations

## Definition
An analytical framework examining how expectations about future opportunities influence technological innovations, revealing how fictional expectations create their own reality through assertions beyond empirical facts.

## Tensions
- **Evidence-based Approaches**: The sociology of expectations highlights how technological development is often driven by speculative promises rather than empirical evidence, creating tension with approaches that prioritize proven outcomes.
