# Evaluative Judgment

## Definition
The capacity to assess AI outputs against task-relevant quality criteria, positioned as a prerequisite skill for effective student-AI collaboration. Combines internal conception of quality with holistic disciplinary standards.

## Tensions
- **Metacognitive Laziness**: When users lack motivation to critically assess AI outputs, evaluative judgment is bypassed
- **Complacency Risk**: Over-trust in AI systems can erode the practice of evaluative judgment
- **AI Overreliance**: Habitual delegation to AI reduces opportunities to develop and exercise evaluative capacities
