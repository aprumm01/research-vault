# Urban Legends in Education

## Definition
Widely circulated beliefs about learners and learning that sound plausible but lack empirical support, including the Digital Natives Myth and Learning Styles Hypothesis.

## Tensions
- **Evidence-based Pedagogy**: Urban legends persist despite contradicting empirical research, highlighting the gap between popular beliefs and evidence-based educational practice.
