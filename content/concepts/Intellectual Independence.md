# Intellectual Independence

## Definition
Students' expressed concern with maintaining their own thinking and distinguishing AI-assisted mechanics from original intellectual contribution.

## Key Papers
```dataview
LIST
FROM "papers"

```

## Tensions
- **Cognitive Offloading**: The desire for intellectual independence conflicts with the efficiency gains from offloading cognitive tasks to AI, creating a tension between autonomy and productivity.
- **AI Tool Dependence**: As AI tools become more capable and integrated into workflows, maintaining intellectual independence becomes increasingly challenging and requires deliberate effort.
