# AI Winter

## Definition
Historical periods of reduced funding and progress in AI following disappointed expectations, resulting from gaps between inflated claims and actual technological delivery.


## Tensions
- **Technological Promise**: AI winters result from the gap between inflated technological promises and actual capabilities, highlighting the dangers of overpromising in technology development.
