# AI as Facilitator

## Definition
A conceptual role positioning AI as a structural guide that scaffolds creative processes while users retain agency, supporting both divergent and convergent thinking phases.

## Key Papers
```dataview

FROM "papers"
WHERE contains(builds_on, this.file.link) OR contains(supports, this.file.link) OR contains(critiques, this.file.link)

```

## Tensions
- **Design Fixation**: Facilitation structures may inadvertently constrain rather than expand creative exploration
- **AI Overreliance**: Users may defer to AI facilitation rather than developing their own process management skills
