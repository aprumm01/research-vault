# Deep Learning (Educational)

## Definition
Active cognitive processing involving critical thinking, making connections between old and new knowledge, and solving complex problems. Contrasted with surface-level engagement with AI-generated content.

## Tensions
- **Metacognitive Laziness**: Lack of motivation to monitor and regulate one's own learning undermines deep engagement
- **Surface-Level Processing**: Direct opposition to deep learning; engaging only with AI summaries rather than grappling with source material
- **Cognitive Offloading**: Delegating thinking to AI prevents the effortful processing required for deep learning
