# Design Fixation

## Definition
A cognitive barrier where designers adhere to initial ideas, previous solutions, or early AI-generated outputs, limiting exploration of alternative creative possibilities and constraining ideation.

## Key Papers
```dataview
LIST
FROM "papers"

SORT file.name ASC
```

## Tensions
- **Divergent Thinking**: Fixation constrains the exploratory phase of ideation
- **Creative Ambiguity**: Premature commitment to solutions reduces tolerance for ambiguity
- **AI-driven Creativity**: AI outputs can become anchors that limit creative exploration
