# Ownership Ambiguity

## Definition
Blurred lines between originality and plagiarism when using generative AI, with unresolved debates about authorship, intellectual property, and creative control.

## Tensions
- **Human Authorship**: When AI contributes to creative work, traditional notions of human authorship become complicated.
- **Creative Ownership**: The question of who owns AI-assisted creative output remains legally and ethically contested.
