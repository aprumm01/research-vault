# Cognitive Tension

## Definition
Interpretive conflict when a user's internal model collides with AI output, operating through dissonance and prediction error to motivate reframing and insight.

## Tensions
None identified. Cognitive tension functions as a generative mechanism rather than standing in opposition to other concepts; it is the productive discomfort that drives learning and creative breakthrough.
