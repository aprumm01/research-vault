# Technological Determinism

## Definition
The attribution of agency primarily to technology rather than human or social actors, treating technological change as an autonomous force that shapes society while obscuring human choices and power dynamics.

## Tensions
- **Human-Centered AI**: Technological determinism minimizes human agency, while human-centered AI emphasizes designing technology around human needs and choices.
- **Sociotechnical Imaginaries**: While related in examining technology-society relationships, sociotechnical imaginaries emphasize the co-construction of technology and society rather than technology as autonomous force.
