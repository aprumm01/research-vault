# AI-driven Creativity

## Definition
Unexpected creative emergence in the designer's mind through human-AI collaboration, rather than autonomous generation by AI—creativity as an outcome of interaction between human cognition and AI capabilities.

## Tensions
- **Design Fixation**: AI-driven creativity can be undermined when designers become anchored to AI-generated suggestions, limiting the exploratory thinking that leads to novel outcomes.
- **Visual Homogenization**: The creative potential of AI collaboration may be constrained by tendencies toward convergent outputs that cluster around training data distributions.
