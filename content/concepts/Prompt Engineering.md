# Prompt Engineering

## Definition
The skill of crafting purposeful natural language inputs to elicit useful outputs from large language models, involving decomposing problems and specifying tasks accurately.

## Tensions
- **De-skilling**: While prompt engineering represents a new skill set, there is tension with concerns that focusing on AI interaction skills may come at the expense of developing foundational domain expertise and traditional competencies.
