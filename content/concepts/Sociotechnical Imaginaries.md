# Sociotechnical Imaginaries

## Definition
Collectively held, institutionally stabilized visions of desirable futures attainable through technology that shape expectations, coordinate actors, and guide policy and curriculum development.

## Tensions
- **Evidence-based AI Integration**: Sociotechnical imaginaries often run ahead of empirical evidence, creating tension between vision-driven adoption and evidence-based approaches to AI integration.
- **Technological Determinism**: While imaginaries acknowledge social construction of technology futures, they can also reinforce deterministic narratives about inevitable technological trajectories.
