# Peer Learning Erosion

## Definition
The displacement of traditional collaborative problem-solving among students by individual AI consultation, weakening social learning communities and opportunities for reciprocal knowledge construction.

## Tensions
- **Cooperative Learning**: Peer learning erosion undermines the foundational premise of cooperative learning approaches that value peer interaction as essential to knowledge construction.
- **Positive Interdependence**: When students turn to AI instead of peers, the mutual reliance that creates positive interdependence in learning communities breaks down.
